# The swipe file

Lines from shipped games and game sites, by kind. They are here to copy the
sound of, not the words. Where a line holds a value the game fills in, it is
shown as N or [key].

The full notes, with every source, are in `docs/text-style/research/`.

## The whole game in one line

- "Join the numbers and get to the 2048 tile!" (2048)
- "Guess the Wordle in 6 tries." (Wordle)
- "Group words that share a common thread." (Connections)
- "Create words using letters from the hive." (Spelling Bee)
- "Find all the mines without treading on any of them." (Simon Tatham's Mines)
- "Eat the treats before the other worms, but don't hit anything!" (Nibbles)

## How to play

- "Use your arrow keys to move the tiles. When two tiles with the same number
  touch, they merge into one!" (2048)
- "The color of the tiles will change to show how close your guess was to the
  word." (Wordle)
- "Eat as many apples as you can to grow as long as possible. Use the arrow keys
  to control your Snake and the spacebar to pause. Be careful not to hit the
  wall or eat your tail!" (Coolmath Snake)
- "Aim at the bricks and shoot the ball." "The ball bounces off the bricks and
  wall." "If the bricks come down to the bottom, it's GAME OVER." (BBTAN)
- "Swipe your finger to throw the balls and break the bricks. Collect items
  along the way!" (Ballz)
- "Use the Arrow Keys or A and D to rotate your ship. Press W or UP to move the
  ship forward. Press SPACE to shoot." (Coolmath Asteroids)
- A fact line above a solitaire game's rules: "Klondike type. 1 deck. Unlimited
  redeals." (PySolFC)
- Where this version differs: "Coolmath's Snake game is different from most.
  When you eat an apple, your tail grows by four blocks instead of the usual
  one."
- An aside, kept out of the rules: "Just like sharks, this Snake can only go
  forwards."

## Buttons

New Game. Cancel. OK. Continue. Restart. Undo. Close. Start. Play Again. Try
again. Next Level. Reset Puzzle. Deal Row. Flip board. Main menu.

Of 235 button labels read, 82% are one or two words and none ends with a full
stop.
