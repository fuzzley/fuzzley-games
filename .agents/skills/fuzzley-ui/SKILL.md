# The shared UI libraries

The site and the games share how they look and much of how they behave
through three libraries under `frontend/lib/`, the games' rules and views
share a fourth, `@fz/core`, which has no framework, and a game drawn with Phaser
takes a fifth, `@fz/phaser`, which the `phaser-core` skill covers. Each library's `AGENTS.md` is
the full account of what it holds and the rules it keeps; this skill says
which piece to reach for, and how an app takes them up.

| Library       | Folder                 | For                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Its rules                        |
| ------------- | ---------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| `@fz/style`   | `frontend/lib/style`   | Every app, Angular or not: the palettes and tokens, the global stylesheet, fragments, Sass mixins, `theme.js`, the fuzzball and the page's icons, and the stylelint preset                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | `frontend/lib/style/AGENTS.md`   |
| `@fz/ui`      | `frontend/lib/ui`      | Every Angular app: dialogs, the confirmation, the announcer, the theme's service and pickers, icons, the radio group, storage, the fuzzball and the wordmark                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | `frontend/lib/ui/AGENTS.md`      |
| `@fz/game-ui` | `frontend/lib/game-ui` | Angular games only: the game bar, its overflow and choice menus, its Pause and Sound, the viewport and breakpoints, the page frame, global stylesheet and Sass toolkit, How to play's catalog lists, the stopwatch, the seeds' source, the run's lifecycle                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | `frontend/lib/game-ui/AGENTS.md` |
| `@fz/core`    | `frontend/lib/core`    | A game's code with no framework, its rules above all: the seeded random and its seeds mixed in named streams, the readers for stored JSON and a data file's keys, counts in words, points grouped in thousands, a clamp, a lerp and the curves a flourish moves by, the next mark on a ladder of points, a clock of fixed steps, a log of numbered events, a field played by them, a new run's id and seed, a frame's sounds gathered, a key pressed as a shortcut, a record's keys typed as its own | `frontend/lib/core/AGENTS.md`    |
| `@fz/phaser`  | `frontend/lib/phaser`  | Games drawn with Phaser: the canvas component, the host, a scene's lifetime helpers, and headless Phaser for specs                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | `frontend/lib/phaser/AGENTS.md`  |

Besides what its row names, `@fz/core` holds a run's stages and the keys
every run shares (`@fz/core/run`), and the keys a game steers by while they
are held, the last pressed winning (`HeldKeys` in `@fz/core/keys`).

Imports go one way: an app may import a library, a library another library,
and a library never an app. `yarn constraints` fails a library that depends
on an app.

## Palettes and their modes

- The palette in use is `data-theme` on `<html>`, which `theme.js` sets
  before anything paints and `ThemeService` changes. Each palette sets every
  role, and its `color-scheme`, from `frontend/lib/style/src/tokens/_palettes.scss`;
  `frontend/lib/style/src/themes.ts` lists them with their modes.
- There are light palettes and dark ones, and an app's chrome wears whichever
  is in use. So read roles, and never branch on a palette's id.
- A tint of `--fz-text` turns with the mode: dark in a light palette, light
  in a dark one. What lies on a game's table rather than on its chrome takes
  `--fz-on-felt`, the felt's own ink, instead.
- An element can carry a palette of its own (`data-theme` on it, as a swatch
  does): the tints and surfaces are declared wherever a palette is, so they
  follow it there too.

## Adopting the libraries in an app

The Solitaire Collection is the worked example; the site does the same, and
so do Minesweeper (`frontend/games/minesweeper/`) and Sudoku
(`frontend/games/sudoku/`), games built from the libraries alone, which show
the same steps with nothing else around them, and Snake
(`frontend/games/snake/`), Brick Breaker (`frontend/games/brick-breaker/`)
and Asteroid Breaker (`frontend/games/asteroid-breaker/`), whose boards
Phaser draws in the palette's roles, Brick Breaker's bricks and Asteroid
Breaker's rocks and pilots in inks of their own beside them.

1. **Depend on them.** List `"@fz/style": "workspace:*"` in the app's
   `package.json`, and for an Angular app `@fz/ui` and, for a game,
   `@fz/game-ui`, with their peers `@angular/aria` and `@angular/cdk`. Run
   `yarn install` from `frontend/`. Nothing in `lib/` is built: the app
   compiles each library from its source.
2. **Add the Vite plugin.** `fuzzleyStyle()` from `@fz/style/vite`. A game
   gets it from `@fz/dev/vite`'s `gameConfig()`, as
   `frontend/games/minesweeper/vite.config.ts` does; the site adds it in
   `frontend/site/vite.config.ts`. It writes `theme.js` and the page's icons
   into the build and serves them in development.
3. **Load `theme.js` first.** A plain tag at the top of `<head>`, with no
   body, since the Content Security Policy allows no inline script:
   `<script src="theme.js" vite-ignore></script>`, as in
   `frontend/games/solitaire/index.html` (the site's, at its root, is
   `/theme.js`). Link the icons beside it the same way, the ICO before the
   SVG, as that page does: `favicon.ico` with `sizes="32x32"`, so a browser
   that draws both picks the SVG, then `favicon.svg`, then
   `apple-touch-icon.png` as `rel="apple-touch-icon"`.
4. **Load the global stylesheet first.** `@use "@fz/style/global";` at the
   top of the app's own, as in
   `frontend/games/solitaire/src/ui/app/styles/global.scss`, or
   `@use "@fz/style/tokens";` alone for a page that keeps its own reset and
   element styles. A game on `@fz/game-ui` uses `@fz/game-ui/global`
   instead, which loads it, as
   `frontend/games/minesweeper/src/app/styles/global.scss` does. Every
   library rule sits in a cascade layer and the app's rules in none, so the
   app's always win: adjust a shared style by writing your own, never by
   out-specifying the library's.
5. **Lint with the preset.** `extends: ["@fz/style/stylelint"]`, as in
   `frontend/games/solitaire/stylelint.config.js`, listing in its
   `overrides` only a file that must write a color, such as a drawing's.
6. **Wire the Angular pieces.** Provide `THEME_COLOR_ROLE` where the
   browser's toolbar should take another role than `bar` (Solitaire's
   `frontend/games/solitaire/src/ui/app/main.ts` sets its own, so it
   provides `null`), and have the specs' setup call `installDialogPolyfill`
   from `@fz/ui/testing` and give each test `provideMemoryStorage()`, as
   `frontend/games/solitaire/test/test-setup.ts` does.
7. **Check text on the table.** Text on glass over the app's own table depends
   on the table too: hold it to 4.5:1 over every table color, as
   `frontend/games/solitaire/test/ui/app/styles/contrast.spec.ts` does. A game
   that draws on the palette's own roles checks what it draws in every palette
   instead, as `frontend/games/minesweeper/src/app/board/contrast.spec.ts` holds
   Minesweeper's numbers on their squares, and
   `frontend/games/sudoku/src/app/board/contrast.spec.ts` Sudoku's digits and
   notes on every shade of a square,
   `frontend/games/snake/src/view/contrast.spec.ts` Snake's snake and apple on
   both shades of square, and
   `frontend/games/brick-breaker/src/view/colors/contrast.spec.ts` Brick
   Breaker's tints, bricks, balls and capsules on its field, and
   `frontend/games/asteroid-breaker/src/view/colors/contrast.spec.ts` Asteroid
   Breaker's rocks, ship, pickups and pilots on its field. Each compiles the
   app's global stylesheet and measures it with `@fz/style/testing`.

Keep the players' `localStorage` keys as they are, whatever the app moves
onto `LocalStorageService`: renaming one loses what players saved.

## Changing a library

- **Everything shared carries `fz`**: tokens, classes, layers, selectors.
  An app's own names carry none.
- **A component reads no viewport.** What changes with the layout is an
  input the app binds, such as the bar's `compact`, to what a game's
  `GameViewportService` says or the site's own breakpoints.
- **A thing belongs in a library only when the apps mean the same by it.**
  Otherwise it stays in the app, until a second one needs it.
- **A palette role goes into every palette at once**, and a role that
  carries text, or sits behind it, gets a pair in
  `frontend/lib/style/src/tokens/contrast.spec.ts`.
- **Accessibility is the component's job**, by the `web-game-accessibility`
  skill's rules, so an app can't forget them.
- Run `yarn verify` from `frontend/`: every app compiles the libraries, so a
  change to one is checked by all of them.
