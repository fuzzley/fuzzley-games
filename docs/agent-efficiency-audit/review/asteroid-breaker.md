# Asteroid Breaker's AGENTS.md, unit by unit

`frontend/games/asteroid-breaker/AGENTS.md` is mostly an index of the code. Its Layout section is 93.8 KB of the file's 130.1 KB (72%), and it walks the source folder by folder, naming 919 different identifiers. All but one of them (Phaser's `maxLights`) are still in the tree, so nothing has drifted, but nearly every sentence repeats a doc comment that sits on the thing it names. Most of the rest is already in the two skills (the Tests section and the long commands are in `asteroid-breaker-testing` nearly word for word) or in the layers above. I would cut Layout down to one line a folder, drop what the skills and the upper layers already say, start two small skills for the scene's contract and for the sounds, and leave a sheet of rules of about 7.5 KB. KB here is 1,000 bytes, as in the brief's 130 KB.
