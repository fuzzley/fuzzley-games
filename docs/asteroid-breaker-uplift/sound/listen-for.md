# What to listen for

Nobody heard these sounds as they were built. Each was measured and its
spectrum looked at, which catches what is broken and not what is wrong. This
page lists, family by family, the sounds their makers were least sure of,
what each might turn out to be, and what to try first if it is. It is the
place to start on the audition page (`audition/`), and where an ear's
verdict should be written back.

A sound not listed measured as meant; it may still not sound as meant.

## Rocks

| Sound                 | It may sound                                             | Try first                                                       |
| --------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `break-stone-*`       | like a woodblock's "tok": the small one knocks at 560 Hz | halve the knock's decays, or spread its partials more by take   |
| `break-ice-*`         | louder than stone at the same level, and tiring          | thin the slivers; bring its level down 2 dB                     |
| `fuse-volatile`       | too loud: 5 dB over the other small sounds to the ear    | move it to the level `tick`, or pull its band down              |
| `break-lodestone-*`   | like a cartoon wobble                                    | slow the wobble to 6 to 14 Hz, or wobble a filter in its place  |
| `hit-iron`            | piercing when hit again and again: it rings on C6        | lower the anvil to G5                                           |
| `break-boulder`       | weak before its split, with holes between its pieces     | a creak of 60 ms before the snaps; more rumble under the pieces |
| `break-cluster`       | like one steady band at 600 to 900 Hz                    | spread its knocks from 300 to 1,400 Hz                          |

- **Changed because of the instruments:** the first build of the large
  stone, the large lodestone, the boulder and every blast kept 5 to 18% of
  its energy above 300 Hz, so a phone would have lost it. Each now keeps 42
  to 76%: the knocks' upper partials carry them, the blast has a knock in
  its middle, and its thump is a quarter of what it was.
- **A chain of blasts** goes off 90 ms apart: the blast before is about
  13 dB down as the next starts.
- **Longer than the kit's lengths, on purpose:** iron rings on (a large one
  for 0.7 s), and a large crystal for 0.64 s.

## Hazards

| Sound           | It may sound                                 | Try first                                         |
| --------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| `comet-pass`    | like a jet through a flanger                 | halve how much of its swept comb is mixed in      |
| `burr-cling`    | scratchy: its zip sits at 2 to 5 kHz         | lower the zip's band to 1.5 to 3.5 kHz            |
| `comet-warning` | too gentle to be a warning: three soft notes | sharpen each pulse's start; shorten the gaps more |

- **The warning** is three pulses on A, C and E, each brighter than the
  last and sooner after it, never louder.
- **`lodestone-hum`** loops at exactly two seconds; its seam jumps by 0.07
  of the largest jump inside it.
