Asteroid Breaker: the uplift's sounds

Every sound here was made by a synth written for the plan, from arithmetic alone, and measured as it was built. None was heard as it was made. This page is where they are heard, beside the sounds the game ships today, and your ear decides.

Every mark is about the uplift's sound, never today's, and every sound starts as good: use it, so only the exceptions need a hand. Needs work means the idea is right but the sound isn't yet, and the note beside it says what is wrong. Don't use it means the game should go without this sound. The notes at the foot of the page list only what isn't good as it is; copy them from there.

Moments of play

Each is a few seconds of the game as it would sound, played with today's sounds, with the uplift's, or one after the other.

The beds

One loop a sector, under everything, and two layers that fade in over it as static builds. The game has none today.

Every sound

By family, each take on a button of its own, with the picture of its spectrum the build drew: time across, pitch up from 40 Hz to 20 kHz.

Your notes

What needs work or should go, and anything you wrote a note on, kept in this browser. Everything else counts as good to use. Copy it into the conversation, or into the work log.
