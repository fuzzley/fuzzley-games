# Work log: S2, the mix

The package `implementation-plan.md` §6 calls S2: the plan's play-back
rules (takes in turn, variation, place, voices, ducking and the bed's
layers) decided as pure functions in `@fz/core/sound_mix`, played by
`@fz/phaser/sound`'s `SpritePlayer` where a game hands it a mix, with a
source in several formats, a limiter, a mono switch and a looped layer
player for the beds, and taken up by `@fz/dev`'s sound lab in place of its
own `variedPlay()` and `panOf()`. No game's code changes: Asteroid Breaker
takes the mix up in S3, and Snake, Brick Breaker and Brick Fuzz Pop give no
mix and play as they did. It runs in `.claude/worktrees/abu-s2`, on
`abu/s2`, cut from the integration branch at `da697c6e`.

## Commit 1: `feat(core)`, the mix's rules

**What it did.** `frontend/lib/core/src/sound_mix.ts`, a new entry, with
its spec, its line in `package.json`'s exports and description, and its
entry in `frontend/lib/core/AGENTS.md`. Each rule of `plan.md`'s table
"How the game plays them" that a player decides is a function here, its
numbers a record or a parameter with the plan's as defaults, and nothing
drawn but by the source handed in:

- **Takes in turn:** `nextTake()`, the take after the last from `NO_TAKE`,
  and `takeMarkers()`, the names a sound's takes have in a sprite (its own
  name for one take, else `<name>-1` on), as the kit's sprite map names
  them; I checked every sound of `assets/sound/sounds.json` but the beds
  against `assets/sound/sprite/sounds.json` by that rule, and all 172 takes
  are there.
- **Variation:** `isVaried()`, `variedDetune()`, `variedGain()` and
  `gainOf()`, after `VARIATION` (40 cents, 1.5 dB, the levels `tick` and
  `small`).
- **Place:** `acrossOf()` and `panOf()`, to `PAN_WIDTH` (0.6).
- **Voices:** `voiceGivingWay()` over `VOICES` (4, 8, 24).
- **Ducking:** `ducksOthers()`, `isDucked()` and `duckedGain()`, after
  `DUCK`.
- **The beds' layers:** `loopPhase()`, `fadedShare()` and `fadeGain()`,
  with `FADE_SECONDS` (2).

**Choices the plan left open.**

- **A tuned sound isn't varied at all.** The kit's audition page detunes
  only an untuned sound but varies the gain of every sound at `tick` and
  `small`, a tuned one too; `plan.md` says "never a tuned one" of both, and
  "a tuned sound varies by its takes alone", so `isVaried()` is false for a
  tuned sound and a player varies neither.
- **Which voice gives way:** "the oldest and quietest" is read as the
  quietest by its level, and of those the oldest, within the narrowest
  limit that is full (the sound's own, then its family, then all). Ordered
  by level first, a sound's own four, all at one level, take their turns
  oldest first, as `SpritePlayer` plays them today; ordered by a play's
  varied gain, they would give way at random. A new play always sounds: it
  never gives way itself, as today.
- **The duck's shape:** down 6 dB over 20 ms, held until a third of a
  second from the huge sound's start (`heldUntil`), and back up over
  100 ms, linear in gain as the audition's ramps are. The audition held a
  quarter of a second and came back over 0.15 s; "for a third of a second"
  is read as the time it is held down. Where a second huge sound starts
  inside the first's duck, the levels take the lower of the two ducks'
  gains (`duckedGainUnder()`), so they stay down through its attack rather
  than jumping back up for 20 ms, which the audition's `setValueAtTime(1)`
  does.
- **The layers fade over the bed's two seconds.** C6 gives two seconds to a
  sector's crossfade and no time to the pulse's and the shimmer's fades;
  `FADE_SECONDS` serves both, and a player may be handed another.
- **No `bed` level.** The kit's `sounds.json` has six levels, the sixth the
  beds', but the beds loop apart from the sprite and no rule here reads a
  level of theirs, so `SOUND_LEVELS` holds the sprite's five.
- **"Its lows less" is left.** The plan's Place row pans a sound's lows less
  than the rest. That takes a crossover on every play, two filters and two
  panners, which the audition doesn't do either; nothing here pans by band,
  and a later package can add it in the player.
- **A crossfade is a quarter sine** (`fadeGain()`), so a bed fading out as
  the next fades in at the same pace keeps their summed power, where a
  straight line dips 3 dB at the middle.
- **It imports nothing,** not even `clamp()` from `./math`, so the sound
  lab's dev server can compile it alone (commit 4).

## Commit 2: `feat(phaser)`, the player plays by a mix

**What it did.** `@fz/phaser` comes to depend on `@fz/core`
(`workspace:*`, one line in `yarn.lock`), and `@fz/phaser/sound`'s
`SpritePlayer` takes an optional third argument, a `SpriteMixing`: a
`SoundMix` and the source a play's variation draws from (`Math.random`
unless given). Without it every play goes through the same code it did, and
every spec of the player before passes unchanged; with it:

- **Names.** The game plays a sound by the mix's name (`tap`), and the
  player plays its takes in turn by the names `takeMarkers()` gives
  (`tap-1`, `tap-2`), worked out once as the player is made, which throws
  a `RangeError` for a take the sprite lacks or a sound of no takes. Two
  constructor overloads keep a player with no mix typed by its spritemap's
  names, as the games' are, and type one with a mix by the mix's.
- **Variation and place.** A busy noisy sound's detune and volume are
  varied by the mix's rules, laid over its marker's own config and the
  play's. A play given a place across the field, from -1 to 1 (`play()`'s
  new third argument `across`, or `playAt()`, made once as `playRisen()` is,
  for S3's frame of sounds), is panned by `panOf()`; one given none keeps
  its config's pan.
- **Voices.** The voices sounding, playing or paused, are kept in a list,
  each pooled sound a `Voice`; each play drops those done, and
  `voiceGivingWay()` names the one that gives way. One of the same sound
  starts over, as the oldest of four does today; one of another sound is
  stopped, and the play takes a free sound of its own pool or a new one.
  With no mix the limits are four of a sound and no others, each sound its
  own family at one level, which is today's rule, by the same function.
- **Ducking.** A `tick` or `small` play's volume is multiplied by
  `duckedGainUnder()` of the time since the last two `huge` plays started,
  on the scene's clock (`scene.time.now`), with the duck's attack taken
  out (`DUCK_AT_START`): a play's volume is set once, as it starts, so
  there is no ramp to smooth, and a busy play in the huge sound's own frame
  is ducked from its start. Only a huge play before it ducks it, so S3's
  frame of sounds plays its huge sounds first, where `FrameSounds` plays in
  the sprite's order.
- **The config made once.** A mixed play is handed a config made once and
  filled each play: every key Phaser gives a marker's config reset first
  (`PLAIN_PLAY`), then the marker's own, then the play's, so nothing of a
  play before is left in it.
- **A held sound** loops its first take, and isn't a voice, varied or
  ducked.
- **Output.** `src/sound/output.ts` holds `limitSound()`, which puts a
  `DynamicsCompressorNode` set as the audition's limiter was (`LIMITER`:
  -3 dB, a hard knee, 20:1, 3 ms, 100 ms) between the
  `WebAudioSoundManager`'s `masterVolumeNode` and the context's
  destination, once a master volume, and `playMono()`, which has the
  destination take one channel or two. A mixed player limits the game's
  sound as it is made; `followMono()` is the player's switch for S4. Both
  do nothing unless the manager is Web Audio's, which the headless game's
  isn't, so their spec fakes a `WebAudioSoundManager` over fake nodes.
- **Formats.** `SoundSprite.url` may be a list, handed to
  `load.audioSprite()` as Phaser's loader takes one, the first the browser
  plays loaded.

**Choices the plan left open.**

- **Ducking a play as it starts.** The audition ducks a bus that every
  small sound plays through, so one already sounding is turned down too.
  Phaser connects each sound to `manager.destination` as it makes it, and
  a bus would mean swapping that for the small sounds' sake, inside
  Phaser's construction, untestable headless. So a ducked play is turned
  down by the duck's gain as it starts, through the config, which the
  specs read; a tick already sounding as a blast starts sounds on (a tick
  lasts a tenth of a second or so), and a held hum isn't ducked, since
  stepping a loop's volume each frame would click.
- **A voice that gives way stops at once,** as the oldest of four starts
  over at once today. A short fade would be gentler, and needs a tween a
  play; it is left, as nobody can hear it here.
- **The limiter is the mix's.** Snake and the two brick games give no mix
  and get none, so they play exactly as they did; S3's mix brings it to
  Asteroid Breaker.
- **`playAt()`** is new, beside `playRisen()`, so S3's frame of sounds can
  hand a place through `play()`, which the games' scene specs spy on.

## Commit 3: `feat(phaser)`, the beds' layer player

**What it did.** `@fz/phaser/sound` gains `LayerPlayer`
(`src/sound/layer_player.ts`), for S4's beds: made with a scene and its
layers, each a `SoundLayer` (a key, a URL or a list of formats, and a
volume), it
loops each while the scene holds it (`hold()`, called each frame as
`SpritePlayer.hold()` is), stepping after the scene's update (`POST_UPDATE`,
let go of as the scene ends):

- **One bar line.** The first layer to start sets the bar line, on the
  sound's own clock: `soundTime()`, new in `src/sound/output.ts`, the audio
  context's `currentTime` under Web Audio, by which Phaser schedules each
  loop, else the scene's clock. A layer started while another plays starts
  at `seek` `loopPhase()` gives, as far into its loop as the time since the
  bar line, so a pulse of 8 s joining a bed of 32 s lands on its beat. Each
  starts 50 ms ahead (`delay`), its place on the bar line taken for then.
  Once nothing plays, the next to start sets the bar line again.
- **Fades by volume.** Each frame moves a layer's share toward 1 where it is
  held and 0 where it isn't, by `fadedShare()` over `FADE_SECONDS` (or the
  time the player is given), by the time the sound's clock kept since the
  last frame, and sets its volume to its own times
  `fadeGain()`; a layer faded out is stopped and let go of. A layer let go
  and held again while it fades plays on, fading back in from where it
  stands. A bed let go as another is held crosses into it at one loudness.
- **Fetched as it is first held,** or earlier where the scene says
  `fetch()`. A layer's file is asked of the scene's loader, once, unless it
  is in the cache already, the first frame a scene holds it or has fetched
  it, and the layer starts once it is in the cache, at its place on the bar
  line; one that fails to load stays silent.
- **A sound destroyed by another,** as the sound manager's `removeAll()`
  destroys each, is let go of, and the layer starts again while it is held,
  as `SpritePlayer` makes a sound anew.

**Choices the plan left open.**

- **Phase by seeking, not by keeping every layer playing.** "Every layer
  started together" could be read as starting all six files at once and
  fading them by volume alone; that keeps four beds and two layers
  decoding silently through every wave. A layer starts as it is held, at
  the phase the bar line gives, which lands it where it would have been.
- **The layers are the scene's to hold.** The player has no switch of its
  own for sound off: `SpritePlayer.follow()` mutes the game, and a scene
  that wants no bed holds none, as S4's settings will.
- **A layer is fetched as it is first held,** not as the player is made.
  A crossfade between sectors needs one player, holding all four beds,
  and Web Audio keeps a file decoded, about 12 MB for a bed of 32 s in
  stereo, so loading every bed at once would hold some 40 MB through a run
  that may never leave the first sector. C6's "once the first wave is
  drawn" is then the scene's to keep, by when it first holds a bed. A bed
  held for the first time at a sector change starts once fetched, the one
  let go fading meanwhile, so S4 can `fetch()` the next sector's bed ahead.
- **The fades keep the sound's clock,** as the bar line does, not the
  frame's. Before the player's first touch, and while the window is away,
  the browser holds the audio context suspended and its clock stands still
  while the game steps on, so a bed held then would have faded in, unheard,
  and come in at full the moment the sound was let go.
- **Each layer starts 50 ms ahead.** Web Audio starts a sound due in the
  past as soon as it can, from the place it was told, so one due "now"
  starts a few milliseconds late, off the bar line; started a moment
  ahead, its place taken for that moment, it starts on it.

## Commit 4: `refactor(dev)`, the sound lab plays by the mix

**What it did.** `@fz/dev` depends on `@fz/core` (`workspace:*`, one line
in `frontend/yarn.lock`), and its sound lab takes the mix's rules up in
place of its own formulas: `src/sound_lab_mix.ts`'s `variedPlay()` returns
`@fz/core/sound_mix`'s `variedDetune()` and then `variedGain()` at the
lab's own numbers, kept as they were, `VARY_CENTS` (60) and
`VARY_DECIBELS` (1.5), and `panAt()`, which replaces its `panOf()`, is
core's `panOf()` at its `PAN_WIDTH` (0.7). The page,
`src/sound_lab_page.ts`, changes only in calling `panAt()`, so the lab
plays as it did, the detune drawn before the gain. `pageModules()` in
`src/sound_lab.ts` has Vite's compiler take core's module too
(`PAGE_IMPORTS`), resolved by its real path with `createRequire()` rather
than imported, since Node runs the plugin; core's module imports nothing,
so it compiles alone. ESLint holds every module of the library but
`src/sound_lab_mix.ts` and the specs to importing nothing of `@fz/core`.
The lab's specs pin its numbers through `variedPlay()` and `panAt()`, and
`src/sound_lab.spec.ts` serves the lab's mix with the compiler off, as
Analog turns it, and finds core's module compiled. `lib/dev/AGENTS.md`
says what the lab takes, how its numbers differ from the games' mix, and
that the library depends on `@fz/core`, imported from
`src/sound_lab_mix.ts` alone; `lib/core/AGENTS.md` says the lab plays by
the mix's formulas.

**Choices.**

- **The lab keeps `variedPlay()`, holding no formula of its own,** and
  names the pan `panAt()`, so its numbers sit in code a spec runs (the
  page is outside the specs and their coverage), and no name of the lab's
  is also core's.
- **Only core's module is added to the page's compiled modules,** by name,
  rather than all of `lib/core/src/`: the page takes one, which imports
  nothing; a module that imported another would need it named too, so the
  doc of `PAGE_IMPORTS` says each imports nothing. In both games with a lab
  the module is in the app's program already, through `@fz/phaser/sound`,
  so Analog compiles it there; `PAGE_IMPORTS` is for a game whose app plays
  through no mix.
- **A change to `@fz/core` now reaches `@fz/dev` in `yarn verify:changed`,**
  and `@fz/style` through it, as `@fz/style` dev-depends on `@fz/dev`: the
  lab's specs run core's code, so they should run, but the run is longer
