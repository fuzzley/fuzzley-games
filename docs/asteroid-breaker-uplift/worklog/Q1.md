## Commit 1: `perf(asteroid-breaker): …`, a hidden copy out of its layer

### What it did

- **`Copies`** keeps each copy in its layer only while it shows: its images
  join their layer once as it is made, taking their places, and leave it
  until they show; a copy that hides leaves it again. It keeps the thing's
  frame, tint, turn, scale and opacity itself, sets a change on the copies
  that show alone, and sets all of them on a copy as it shows, its frame
  anew, so one that hid through a bake takes up its frame's new size.
  `hide()` does nothing while nothing shows. It throws for a layer whose
  scene keeps no order (below), as a copy shown again would otherwise draw
  over what was made after it.
- **`layer_order.ts`**: `keepJoinOrder()`, which `BeltScene.create()` calls
  over the field's layers before the painters are made, gives each object,
  as it first joins one of them (the scene's `ADDED_TO_SCENE`, which a
  layer emits only for an object out of every list), its place among all
  that layer has held as its depth, which a layer sorts its children by
  before it draws them. Nothing else in the scene sets a depth or moves an
  object between layers, so the order of what shows is the order it was
  before. As the scene ends it destroys what lies out of its layer then,
  which Phaser, destroying only what its lists hold, would not, and stops
  listening. `listOf()` reads the list an object lies in, which Phaser's
  types say is always set but is null out of every list.
- **The scene** keeps what its layers have held (`layeredObjects`), and the
  specs' `objectsHeldBy()` counts it with what the lists hold, for the
  specs that hold a frame of play to making no game object
  (`belt_scene.spec.ts`, `flourishes.spec.ts`), as `objectsIn()` sees what
  the lists hold alone.
- **Specs**: `layer_order.spec.ts` holds the rocks' layer to the bodies that
  show, a rock that hides and shows again to its place, and a hidden image
  to being destroyed as the scene stops; `copies.spec.ts` holds the throw;
  `belt_scene.spec.ts` holds a copy that hid as the field baked again to
  its frame's new size as it shows (it fails with the frame set on show
  only when its name changed); the halo painter's spec now reads which of
  a halo's images lie in its layer, and the spark painter's what its drawn
  sparks show and that its next slot shows nothing, as a hidden copy keeps
  what it last showed.
- **Docs**: the game's `AGENTS.md` (`Copies`, `keepJoinOrder()`, how a
  copy out of its layer takes up its frame, `objectsHeldBy()`), its
  `asteroid-breaker-testing` skill (what `imagesIn()` and `layersOf()` see
  now, and `objectsHeldBy()`) and the repository's
  `phaser-canvas-performance` skill, §4, the lesson: a pool mostly hidden
  costs its layer's render and its painters' setting it, so what hides
  leaves the layer, its depth keeping its place, written in one place as
  §5 asks.

### Review

A fresh reviewer read the staged change and found no correctness bug: the
order of what shows is the same, as nothing in the scene sets a depth or
moves an object, and every object joins in the order it did. Its findings:

1. Nothing held a copy that hid through a bake to taking up its frame as it
   shows, which `imagesIn()` no longer sees. **Fixed**: the spec in
   `belt_scene.spec.ts` above, which fails with the frame set on show only
   when its name changed.
2. `Copies` relies on its layer's scene keeping the join order, guarded by
   a comment alone. **Fixed**: its constructor throws for a layer that
   gave its first image no depth, and `copies.spec.ts` holds it.
3. The skill's paragraph named the copies past the edges, where every copy
   leaves its layer, and sat above §5's "every depth from one place"
   without saying how it keeps it. **Fixed**: it names `Copies`, and says
   `keepJoinOrder()` writes every depth in one place, the order things first
   joined a layer. The fifth it claimed was being measured; the sitting
   above bears it out.
4. Style: the six new fields lacked doc comments, and the setters said
   "each copy" where they set only those that show. **Fixed.** Putting the
   set's lookup before the depth check, to skip a prototype lookup:
   **declined**, as the set is typed by the narrowed object, and the check
   runs only as an object joins a layer.
