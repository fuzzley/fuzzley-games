# Work log: R3, the wave

R3 builds `implementation-plan.md`'s §6 R3 in Asteroid Breaker's rules: the
last few rocks of a wave lean toward the pod as a lodestone does, so a
wave's tail is no longer than it was; the three set pieces (C24), each a
new kind of boulder in `ROCK_KINDS`, with their rules, their art on L1's
facets and their sounds; and strays when they can still matter, the first
within 5 s of play on any field and one sent at once, once a wave, to a
pilot with no one in tow. The report times each wave's tail, before and
after. The newest entry is last.

## 1. The report times each wave's tail

- **Did:** a wave's last few rocks are its whole rocks while they are as
  many as `LAST_FEW` (3) on a field of 40 by 40, or fewer, grown by the
  field's area as a wave's counts are (`fill.ts`'s `lastFewOn()`: 5 on 73
  by 40, 7 on 88 by 40); `rocks.ts`' `isLastFew()` says whether a field's
  are. The autopilot's report gains `tailSteps`, the steps from the last
  step it held more than its last few to its clearing, 0 for a wave it
  didn't clear, and the whole wave for one begun with no more than its last
  few. `report:waves` prints it as a `tail` column after `time`, from the
  play with nothing able to take the ship, as the time is, and with
  `--seeds` the middle tail beside the middle time and the longest.
  `AGENTS.md` and the content skill say so.
- **Outside R's files:** none but `AGENTS.md` and the content skill.
- **Choices:** the tail counts whole rocks, not a broken volatile rock
  waiting to go off, as a rock to shoot is what the player hunts. It is
  timed from the last step with more than the last few, so a large rock
  broken into more pieces than the last few starts the tail again. The
  middle tail with `--seeds`, not the longest, since one seed's tail is the
  noisiest figure the report has.
