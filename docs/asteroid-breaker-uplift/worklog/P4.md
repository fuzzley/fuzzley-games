## 1. The sparks on the field, their halos and their tails

### What it did

- **`src/view/art/sparks.ts`** (new), every rule of how a spark is drawn,
  pure: its two sizes, the kit's motes' larger two (`SPARK_CUTS`: medium,
  0.25 units to its points, and large, 0.35, every third slot's,
  `sparkSizeOf()`), each slot's spark turned a little its own way
  (`sparkTurnOf()`, up to 0.3 radians, by the golden share so neighbours
  never lie alike), its frames (`sparkFrames()`: the points, cut by
  `breaks.ts`' `sparkPoints()`, in the accent's energy body, the middle,
  0.55 of them, in its hot tone where the points reach 2 CSS pixels, as
  the kit's `moteSvg()`), its halo (`sparkHalo()`: round, 2.7 times its
  points, at 0.85), whether it shows (`isSparkShown()`: the rules'
  `sparkBlinks()` and the flourishes' `blinkShown()`, so one in its last
  second shows for the first half of each blink), whether the ship draws
  it in (`isSparkDrawn()`: within the rules' `drawReach()`, the speed's
  reach or a Magnet's, across the wrap), and its tail (`sparkTail()`: the
  spark heading toward the pod the short way across the wrap, its tail
  behind it `SPARK_TAIL_LENGTH` long, as far as a spark is drawn in a
  tenth of a second, 1.2 units, and as wide as the spark;
  `SPARK_TAIL_FRAME`, two layered wedges in the body, 0.3 and 0.4 opaque,
  the nearer half the stronger).
- **`src/scene/painters/spark_painter.ts`** (new), `SparkPainter`, in a
  new `sparks` layer over the pickups (`layers.ts`, add-only), so an orb
  hides none: a spark for each of the field's `MOST_SPARKS` slots, in its
  slot's size and turn, and a tail under each, made as the scene is, and
  that many halos reserved from L4's halo painter, glowing in the accent
  after each spark is drawn. Each is drawn between its last place and its
  next, again past an edge it reaches across, its tail as `sparkTail()`
  says.
- **`frame.ts`** (add-only): `FieldView` gives the scene the field's
  `sparks`, `sparking`, `pullReach` and `setup.sparkReach`, what
  `drawReach()` reads. **`art.ts`** (add-only) adds the sparks' frames to
  the atlas. **`belt_scene.ts`** (add-only) makes the halo painter before
  the kit's literal, so it is handed to the spark painter, and draws the
  sparks after the pickups.
- **Specs:** `sparks.spec.ts` in `pure` (the sizes, turns and halos, the
  blink, the draw reach at each speed, across the wrap and by a Magnet,
  the frames within their reach at every scale, the body's 3:1 against
  the field in every palette, the tail's frame, length, way and width for
  each size, across the wrap too) and `spark_painter.spec.ts` in `dom`,
  beside the painter (a spark for each slot, each placed, sized, turned
  and zoomed, between steps, past an edge, its halo in the accent, the
  larger's tail toward the pod and across the wrap, none out of reach, none
  once the ship is gone, the blink in its last second, and none once
  gathered). `layers.spec.ts` takes the new layer, and L4's
  `halo_painter.spec.ts` counts the halos the sparks reserve.
- **Docs:** the game's `AGENTS.md` names `sparks.ts`, the `sparks` layer
  and `SparkPainter`.

### Choices the plan left open

- **Two sizes, not three.** The kit's motes come in three; its smallest, a
  diamond 0.15 units across, shows as a speck at a phone's unit and reads
  as a dot, not a spark, so the game draws the larger two, every third
  slot the larger, as the kit's mocks scatter them.
- **A tail only while drawn in.** A spark shed from a rock flies out with
  no tail; it streaks only once the ship draws it, as the kit's
  `moteDrawnSvg()` draws a mote "drawn in toward the pod", so the tail
  says the pod is gathering it. Whether the ship draws it is the rules'
  own reach, so the tail starts the frame it turns.
- **The tail is a frame stretched, not a stroke.** Two wedges in the
  atlas, turned and stretched per spark, keep 64 sparks to images made
  once; a `Graphics` stroke per spark would clear and draw again each
  frame.
- **No least reach.** The kit draws a mote's points 1.3 pixels out at
  least and its glow 3.4, but at the smallest unit the layout check holds
  a field to, 6 CSS pixels, a medium spark's points reach 1.5, and its
  frame baked for that scale at least 1.36, so neither least ever binds
  and the view keeps neither.
- **One word, "spark".** The kit calls them motes; the rules, the tally
  and the sounds call them sparks, so the view's names do too
  (`SPARK_CUTS`, `SPARK_FRAMES`, `sparkHalo()`), the kit's name kept in
  the docs that point to it. The rock's held-hit flourish keeps its frame
  `spark`; the sparks' are `spark-medium`, `spark-large` and `spark-tail`.

## 2. Static's arcs: the zap's, and those crawling over the hull

### What it did

- **`src/view/art/arcs.ts`** (new), every rule of how an arc of static is
  drawn, pure. `cutArc()` ports the kit's `makeArc()` and its `zigzag()`
  number for number, `@fz/core`'s `createRandom()` standing for the kit's
  `seeded()`, which it matches draw for draw, but cuts the arc in its own
  frame, from (0, 0) to (1, 0), in shares of its length; `placeArc()`
  lays it between any two places, stretched and turned, as the kit would
  have cut it between them (the kit's arcs are the same under a turn): a
  spec holds two of the kit's arcs, cut from its seeds, to six places.
  Both write into `Arc`s made once (`createArc()`), and `arcShift()` says
  where an arc's copies past the field's edges lie, by the bounds of its
  every line, grown by half its widest stroke (`ARC_OVERHANG`). The zap's
  arcs are cut once, from each of `ZAP_ARC_SEEDS`, 32, for each count of
  steps an arc's length takes (`arcSteps()`, 5 to 12), as long as its
  steps run, as a held hit's sparks are cut once. As it strikes,
  `strikeOf()` keeps where the pod zapped from, where the rock lay and
  how far its rim lay toward the pod, its outline turned as the rock was
  (`breaks.ts`' `rimToward()`, as a held hit's spark is thrown from), or
  a rock of its size's once its slot no longer holds it, its arc's steps
  and its event's serial (`ZapStrike`); each frame `zapArc()` lays the
  leap's, the one the kit's seed picks among the 32; it runs from
  `ARC_FROM`, 1.2 units past the
  pod's middle, to `ARC_RIM`, 0.86 of the way to that rim, the short way
  across the wrap, none to a rock over the hull (`zapEnds()`), as the
  kit's zap mock places them; leaping anew every 45 ms where motion is
  welcome, its first held where it isn't (`zapLeap()`), cut from its
  event's serial and its leap as the kit seeds it (`zapSeed()`), two
  branches then one (`zapBranches()`), whole for 90 ms and gone by 180
  (`zapStrength()`, `ZAP_ARC`, the kit's `zapFx()`); stroked as the kit's

  share of the unit and never under its CSS pixels, a branch 0.62 as wide

  halo strength); and the spark where it lands, the kit's `spark()`, 0.55
  units, shrinking by 0.08 and turning 0.6 radians a leap, with its glow
  (`ARC_SPARK`, `ARC_SPARK_FRAME`, `arcSparkHalo()`, `arcFrames()`). The
  hull's: three arcs (two where the pod's fine marks don't show, as the
  kit's `small`, spread as it spreads two), cut once from each of
  `HULL_ARC_SEEDS`, 32, as the kit's `podSvg()` cuts them from a seed,

  the pod is drawn (`hullArcs()`), crawling through the seeds every
  100 ms where motion is welcome and holding one where it isn't
  (`hullArcSeed()`), in the pilot's ink under a white core

  controls (`hullArcInk()`).
- **`pod_art.ts`** (add-only): `HULL_RUNS`, the runs between the middles

- **Specs:** `arcs.spec.ts` in `pure` (the kit's two arcs, the steps by

### Choices the plan left open

- **The arcs last the kit's 180 ms.** The plan's moment for a zap is
  60 + 350 ms, a hold of the field first and each rock flashing as hit,
  which are F2's flourishes; the arcs keep the kit's `FX_MS.zap` and its
  leaps (`ZAP_ARC`), which F2 may stretch if the moment wants them
  longer.
- **"Static's last step" is full static.** The fur's last step is ×4,
  from 30; the kit draws the hull's arcs at a "full charge", and the plan
  says arcs crawl at the last step. They crawl at full, 40
  (`isStaticFull()`, R2's `FULL_STEP`), so they say the next dash is a
  zap, which nothing else on the pod says; the fur already says ×4.
- **The arcs follow the pod.** A zap is a dash, and the pod flies 7.5
  units in its 220 ms, so each arc leaps from where the pod is drawn each
  frame to where its rock lay when struck, and keeps the zap's place only
  once no pod is drawn.
- **The hull's arcs crawl every 100 ms,** about six frames, and are still
  without motion: the kit draws them still, and an arc held still reads
  as a mark on the hull, which is what the still form wants.
- **Strokes in one `Graphics`.** Phaser 4's WebGL strokes each segment as
  a quad, so the halo's wide strokes overlap a little at each joint,
  which shows at twice the pixels as a faint square at a kink; at the
  kit's 0.2 opacity it reads as the glow's grain, and a stroke per
  segment, or a mesh, would cost a frame more than it gives.
- **No glow where the arc leaves the hull.** The kit's `zapFx()` glows at
  each arc's start, 0.7 units at 0.7 of its strength; its zap mock, which
  the user chose from, leaves it out for one glow about the pod. With six
  arcs that is six glows ringing the pod, so the port follows the mock,
  and the pod's own glow, if the moment wants one, is F2's.
- **Arcs cut once, chosen by the kit's seed.** A zap's arcs are cut once,
  32 seeds for each count of steps (5 to 12), 256 arcs, as a held hit's
  sparks are, and a leap takes the one its kit's seed picks, modulo 32,
  so a zap makes nothing as it strikes. Up to eight steps, any arc under
  8.9 units, most of a zap's, the arc is the kit's for that seed exactly,
  as its cut is in shares of its length; a longer one, to a rock out past
  the dash's end (up to about 12.5 units as tuned), is cut as long as its
  steps run, within half a step of its own length, and sways a little
  more or less than the kit's would. The hull's are cut once
  too, 32 seeds' worth, and crawl through them, about three seconds
  before the first comes round.
- **Six slots, the oldest taken.** A zap arcs to `ZAP_ARCS` rocks at most;
  the debug menu may raise the field's `sparking.arcs`, and then the
  later arcs take the oldest slots rather than the painter making images
  in a frame of play.
