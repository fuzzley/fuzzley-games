## Commit 1: the faces, the look and the fur as geometry in `@fz/style`

### What it did

- **`@fz/style/fuzzball`** ports the kit's fuzzball as plain geometry on
  the mark's grid about its body's middle, `{ x, y }` points
  (`GridPoint`), the shape `@fz/phaser/shapes`' `Point` has, so a canvas
  takes it as it is:
  - **The faces:** `FuzzballFace`, its nine names, `FUZZBALL_FACES` in the
    sheet's order, and `eyesOf()`, a face's marks (`EyeMark`: an ellipse, a
    polygon or a round-ended stroke, each in the eyes' ink or the glints'
    white), the eyes' marks first and their glints over them, so the rest
    face is the mark's own eyes and glints number for number, in the order
    the library has always drawn them.
  - **The look:** a `FuzzballLook` says how far a look shifts the eyes.
    `FUZZBALL_LOOK` is today's, 1.3 across and 0.9 down, from where the mark
    has them, as the brick games move their eyes' frames; `FUZZBALL_LOOK_FAR`
    is the kit's, 1.9 and 1.35, taking the eyes about the body's middle as
    it looks, for a caller that asks for more. `lookOffset()` is how far a
    look shifts them, for a scene that moves the eyes' frame, and
    `restingLook()` where a face looks unless told (a plead looks up).
  - **The fur:** `Strand`, `strandsOf()` and `FUZZBALL_STRANDS` move here
    from `@fz/phaser/fuzzball`, the same code; `combFur()` combs them for a
    `FurPose` as the kit's `combFur()` does: static's step (`lift`, 0 to
    `MOST_LIFT`, 3), a push (`lean`), a fright (`puff`), glass round it
    (`within`) and the small form; `sweptFur()` sweeps any fur by a push
    alone, for `combedStrands()`'s lean; and `sparksOf()` gives static's
    sparks at its last step, the kit's ticks and stars, with `SPARK_LOOK`,
    how they are drawn.
  - **The pose:** `fuzzballShape()` poses a face, a look and fur together,
    with the kit's rules: the small form's fur wider (`SMALL_FUR_WIDTH`),
    sparks from `SPARKS_FROM` (2.5) and never in the small form, and how far
    it reaches. `fuzzballForm()` is the form a size takes, the small one at
    `SMALL_UNDER` (12) CSS pixels across the grid and under, as the kit
    draws it.
  - **SVG:** `fuzzballDrawing()` gives a pose as the parts a page's own
    `<svg>` binds, on the mark's view box to a hundredth, as the mark's own
    numbers are; `fuzzballSvg()` takes a `face`, and with none writes the
    file it always wrote, byte for byte.
- **Docs:** `frontend/lib/style/AGENTS.md`'s `src/fuzzball.ts`, and the
  `fuzzley-ui` skill's row for the fuzzball anywhere else (an addition to a
  shared file, §7.3).

### Choices the plan left open

- **Static's fur is the kit's own,** every other strand taller, sparking
  with ticks between the tips and stars, rather than `combedStrands()`'s
  even comb and its five zigzags. `plan.md`'s "Where the code goes" leaves
  the choice to slice 4; the sheet is what the user chose, and
  `combedStrands()` stays the brick games' comb, so their looks hold.
- **The sparks are data, not drawn from a seed.** The kit draws them from
  `seeded()`, which is `@fz/core`'s `createRandom()`, but `@fz/style`
  imports no `@fz/core`, and a spark that moves from one drawing to the
  next is a choice for a scene to make. So the three jumps are laid out
  once, from the seed the sheet's "x4: on end, sparking" was drawn at (4),
  to a thousandth, and follow the fur's tips wherever it is combed.
- **The look keeps its default and lets a caller ask for more,** as the
  brief says: `FUZZBALL_LOOK` stays the brick games', and the kit's is
  `FUZZBALL_LOOK_FAR`, which also draws the eyes about the body's middle
  as a look grows, as the kit's `eyesOf()` does, since the mark's eyes sit
  a unit right of it.
- **The static step is `lift`, from 0 to 3,** the kit's charge by the
  brief's name: whole steps are ×1 to ×4, and between two steps the fur is
  between them, for a scene that eases it. A step past either end is the
  nearest, as the kit's `ramp()` clamps it.
- **One file.** The geometry first went in files beside `fuzzball.ts`,
  imported with their `.ts`, as the library's rule says; the root's
  typecheck failed in every app (TS5097), since an app compiles the page's
  files with its own options, which allow no `.ts` in an import, while Node
  loads `fuzzball.ts` as it is for `src/build/vite.ts`'s icons, which needs
  it. No relative import suits both, so the fuzzball is one file, and its
  header and `AGENTS.md` say why.

### The review

A fresh `general-purpose` agent read the staged change against the kit,
the sheet and the plan. It found the fur, the eyes, the sparks, the pose,
the form and `fuzzballSvg()`'s default output sound, and the one-file
reasoning right. Its findings, and how each was answered:

1. **`fuzzballDrawing()` said nothing of how far it reaches.** Lifted,
   puffed or swept fur and every spark lie past the mark's view box, which
   an `<svg>` clips. Taken: the drawing has a `reach`, its fur's and its
   sparks' with their halos, and its doc says what draws it must let them
   show past the box, the body never growing (which `<fz-fuzzball>` does,
   in commit 3).
2. **The specs held only each face's first mark to the kit,** so a lid
   drooping the wrong way on the right, a spiral wound one way on both, or
   a glint on the wrong side would pass. Taken: every mark of every face,
   glints too, is held to the kit's numbers in a few words each (its kind,
   tone, middle and radii, or its points' count, ends and width), and the
   small form's too.
3. **`SPARK_LOOK` lacked the kit's least core width,** 0.8 of a pixel,
   which wins below about 46 pixels across the grid, and said nothing of
   round ends or of the core's color. Taken: `tickCoreLeast`, round ends
   and corners in its doc, and the core's mix, toward the text by 0.4 in a
   light palette and toward white by 0.82 in a dark one (`coreMix`), the
   kit's `sparkTones()`, as data each drawer takes.
4. **`sparksOf()`'s `@throws` didn't hold** for fur one strand short.
   Taken: it checks the count first; a spec tries the small form's and one
   short.
5. **`strandsOf()` read a doubled space as a 0.** Taken: it splits on any
   run of spaces and refuses a strand of five numbers, as the kit does.
6. **Small ones.** `fuzzballSvg()` took the eyes from a whole drawing;
   it now takes them from `eyesOf()`. An SVG mark said `glint: boolean`
   where a grid mark says `tone`; both say `tone`. `JUMPS`' doc opened in
   two sentences, the header's "with nothing but" didn't read, and
   `GridPoint` said it lay from the body's middle while `aboutBody()` takes
   a point of the view box: each reworded.

None reshaped the change, so there was no second review.
