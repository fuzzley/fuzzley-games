## Commit 2: one step for each play

`refactor(core): work out a play's take, variation and duck in one step that the player and the sound lab share`

**What it did.** S3 left the lab's `GameMix.plan()` repeating
`SpritePlayer`'s steps for each play. `@fz/core/sound_mix` now holds them as
`mixedPlay()`, which writes a play's take, detune and gain (a `MixedPlay`)

last, the seconds since the latest sound that ducks others started, and the
source the variation is drawn from, by the mix's variation and duck unless
others are given. Both callers take it, and keep beside
it what it leaves to them: each sound's last take, when the latest duck

`earlierDuck` and `duckedGainUnder()` go too. Under `DUCK_AT_START`, whose
attack is 0, `duckedGain()` never falls as the seconds since a duck grow: it
is down at once, held, then comes back up. Both callers' times only move
forward, so the earlier of two ducks always started longer ago and its gain
is never the lower; the minimum of the two was always the latest's. A new
spec holds `DUCK_AT_START` to that (fifty times over its length, none lower
than the one before), and `mixedPlay()`'s specs hold the take in turn, the

by the step `SpritePlayer` takes.

## Commit 4: the voice

`feat(asteroid-breaker): give the fuzzball its voice, a call at the events it speaks at, the rarest of a frame, once in four seconds at most, 3 dB down`

**What it did.** `src/view/effects/voice.ts` holds the rule, Phaser-free and
with no clock but the field's: `callOf()` gives the call an event calls
for, by the kit's `plays` (a dash `voice-whee`, a wave cleared
`voice-cheer`, one knocked loose `voice-ouch`, one caught again or a stray
caught, into the line or home, `voice-yay`, a stray's arrival `voice-ooh`,
one floating home `voice-aww`), and `Voice` keeps it: one dash in three
calls (`DASHES_A_CALL`, the first, the fourth, the seventh, counted over
every field), a frame's rarest call wins (`CALLS_RAREST_FIRST`: cheer, aww,
ouch, yay, ooh, eep, whee), placed where its event happened, and it speaks
at most once in four seconds of the field's steps (`PATIENCE_MS`). A call
due while it keeps its patience is dropped, not put off, and its patience
starts afresh with each new field, which only comes after a card.
`FieldSounds` hears each event through it and plays its call among the
frame's other sounds, and `volumeOf()` plays the voice family 3 dB down
(`FAMILY_DECIBELS`). `voice.spec.ts` holds each call, the dashes' turn,
the patience by steps however many frames pass, the drop, a new field,
the rarest of a frame (each call over the next commoner one, heard after
it) and where; the scene's spec holds the patience end to end on a field
that steps, and its record of every event's sounds now holds the calls.
The sound lab's field clock now moves with each frame's time, as the
patience needs; its scenes say the calls, and the run's end moves to 4.5 s,
after the cheer's patience.

**Choices.** The run's end (`lost`) calls `voice-aww` too, as the kit's
"The run ends" scene plays it, the pilot bailing out home. `voice-eep`
waits on R2's `grazed`, and `voice-hum` stays unplayed: the plan names the
voice's moments (a near miss, a rescue, being knocked loose, a wave cleared
and a stray's arrival, one dash in three) and waiting for the first move is
not among them; a hum at every wave's wait would make the voice common.
The kit's audition sets each call a tenth of a second after its event's
sound; the game plays it in the same frame, since a call put off by the
field's steps would be lost where the field stops stepping, as a cleared
one does.

**S3's two cases, which the coordinator left to this package.** A wave's
clearing takes each one still loose back as caught again, so
`crew-caught` plays beside `wave-clear`, and the voice cheers, the rarer
call. It stays: it says the loose ones came back, which the card then
shows in the crew, it is a tuned sound of the rescue family in the stinger's
key, a level under it, and it sounds only where someone was loose at the
clearing. One loose caught with the line full floats home and plays
`crew-home` and `voice-aww`, as its time running out does. It stays too:
the rules log it as floated, out of the run, the same event as a timeout,
so the view can't tell the two apart, and a catch heard there would need
R1's event to say so, a change to the rules outside this package.
`lodestone-hum`, the coordinator's other case, is answered in the next
commit's entry.
