## Commit 1: the split

`refactor(asteroid-breaker): split each event's sound from its flourishes, the sound in the view's new sounds.ts and the flourishes left in effects.ts, the rock an event names read in frame.ts`

**What it did.** Each event's sound left `EVENT_EFFECTS` for a record of its
own, `EVENT_SOUNDS`, in a new `src/view/effects/sounds.ts`, with what played
it: `SOUNDS`, `SoundName`, `THRUST`, the chain's cents, `detuneOf()` and
`createFrameSounds()`, and a new `soundOf()`, which returns an event's
sound, a break's and a held hit's by the rock's size, as `effectsOf()` did.
`effects.ts` keeps the flourishes alone (`EventEffects` lost its `sound`).
The readers of the rock an event names, `rockKindOf()` and `rockSizeOf()`,
which the sounds, the flourishes and `FlourishPlayer` all use, moved to
`src/view/field/frame.ts`, beside `FieldEvent`, so neither stream's file
imports the other's; and both halves find a rock that rings by the
catalog's `hasTrait()`, so the rule that a ringing rock's held hit is heard
and seen by its ring alone reads the trait in one way. The scene asks each
half for its own; `tools/sound-lab.ts` and the scene's spec import the
sounds from `sounds.ts`.

The specs hold what they held: `effects.spec.ts` each event's flourishes,
`sounds.spec.ts` each event's sound, a break's and a held hit's by size,
iron's hit heard once by its ring, `detuneOf()` and `createFrameSounds()`,
and `frame.spec.ts` the readers, on events a real log records. Beside them,
`sounds.spec.ts` holds `soundOf()`'s throws for a break or a hit naming no
size and a hit naming no rock, as `effects.spec.ts` holds `effectsOf()`'s,
since each throws on its own. The game's `AGENTS.md` and the content skill
name `sounds.ts` for the sounds.
