## Commit 1: the split

`refactor(asteroid-breaker): split each event's sound from its flourishes, the sound in the view's new sounds.ts and the flourishes left in effects.ts, the rock an event names read in frame.ts`

**What it did.** Each event's sound left `EVENT_EFFECTS` for a record of its
own, `EVENT_SOUNDS`, in a new `src/view/effects/sounds.ts`, with what played
it: `SOUNDS`, `SoundName`, `THRUST`, the chain's cents, `detuneOf()` and
`createFrameSounds()`, and a new `soundOf()`, which returns an event's
sound, a break's and a held hit's by the rock's size, as `effectsOf()` did.
`effects.ts` keeps the flourishes alone (`EventEffects` lost its `sound`).
The readers of the rock an event names, `rockKindOf()` and `rockSizeOf()`,
which the sounds, the flourishes and `FlourishPlayer` all use, moved to
`src/view/field/frame.ts`, beside `FieldEvent`, so neither stream's file
imports the other's; and both halves find a rock that rings by the
catalog's `hasTrait()`, so the rule that a ringing rock's held hit is heard
and seen by its ring alone reads the trait in one way. The scene asks each
half for its own; `tools/sound-lab.ts` and the scene's spec import the
sounds from `sounds.ts`.

The specs hold what they held: `effects.spec.ts` each event's flourishes,
`sounds.spec.ts` each event's sound, a break's and a held hit's by size,
iron's hit heard once by its ring, `detuneOf()` and `createFrameSounds()`,
and `frame.spec.ts` the readers, on events a real log records. Beside them,
`sounds.spec.ts` holds `soundOf()`'s throws for a break or a hit naming no
size and a hit naming no rock, as `effects.spec.ts` holds `effectsOf()`'s,
since each throws on its own. The game's `AGENTS.md` and the content skill
name `sounds.ts` for the sounds.

2. The log's subject was a placeholder, and it didn't say that

3. `EventEffects`' doc opened with two sentences, and `soundOf()`'s with a

The fixes move two functions and their specs without changing what any of

## Commit 2: the game on the uplift's sprite

`feat(asteroid-breaker): play the uplift's sprite, as Ogg or M4A, by the view's mix of the plan's ninety-two sounds, each event by its plan's sound, today's sprite kept in the sound lab as its before`

**What it did.**

- **`SOUNDS`** in `src/view/effects/sounds.ts` is the plan's ninety-eight
  sounds less the six beds, by name, each with its family, its level, its
  takes and whether it is tuned, in one record (`sound()`), which is
  `@fz/core/sound_mix`'s `SoundMix`, so it is the mix the scene hands its
  player. A sound's takes are typed as a literal, and `SoundTake` names each
  take as `takeMarkers()` does (`shot-gun-1` to `shot-gun-5`, `chain-ping`
  for a sound of one take). The scene types its sprite by it
  (`SoundSprite<SoundTake>`, over the imported `sprite.json`), so a take of
  `SOUNDS` the map lacks fails to compile, which I checked by giving
  `hit-iron` a fifth take; the scene's spec holds the other way, every take
  the map names and no other, and `tools/build-sounds.spec.ts` holds the
  record to the recipes, every sound but the beds by name, family, level
  and takes. `SOUND_NAMES` is the record's keys.
- **The scene** loads `sounds/sprite.ogg` and `sounds/sprite.m4a`, the Ogg
  first, which every browser but Safari plays, and hands `SpritePlayer`
  the mix (`new SpritePlayer(this, SOUND_SPRITE, { mix: SOUNDS })`), so each
  sound's takes play in turn, a busy noisy one varied, the voices held to
  the mix's limits and the busy levels ducked under a huge one, through
  S2's limiter. A frame plays its sounds the loudest level first
  (`createFrameSounds()`, over `PLAY_ORDER`), since the player ducks a
  busy sound only under a huge one played before it, as S2 left it: a
  blast and the breaks it sets off in its step are heard in one frame.
- **Each event's sound is the plan's** (`soundOf()`, after
  `assets/sound/sounds.json`'s `plays`): a rock's break by its kind at its
  size (`BREAK_SOUNDS`, typed by each kind's sizes in `ROCK_KINDS`, so a new
  kind or size fails to compile without its sound), a volatile rock's break
  its fuse, a rock a comet smashed (a break counted in no chain) the
  comet's smash; a held hit by its kind (`HIT_SOUNDS`), iron's by its ring,
  `hit-iron`; a blast by its size, the small one's sound no rock makes yet,
  as no rock that blasts comes small; a catch by its pickup
  (`CATCH_SOUNDS`);
  the gun's shot for every `fired` until commit 3; a dash, the Shield
  spent, the comet's warning, the wave's clearing; and, as rebased onto
  R1's crew in tow (`4e9f6339`), the run's end the pod lost (`pod-lost`),
  one knocked loose the pod hit (`pod-hit`), one caught again
  `crew-caught`, one floated home `crew-home`, and a stray caught, into
  the line or straight home, `stray-catch`. The breaks still rise along
  the chain by `detuneOf()`, as today, until commit 4 moves the ladder to
  `chain-ping`. A pickup dropped and faded, a stray's arrival and points
  scored stay silent until commit 4, and so does `crew-loose` after a
  knock's `pod-hit`.
- **Today's sprite** moved, by `git mv`, to `tools/sound-lab/sounds.wav`
  and `sounds.json`, with the names it plays them under and what played
  each in `tools/sound-lab/before.ts` (`BEFORE_SOUNDS`, `BEFORE_PLAYS`,
  `BEFORE_SPRITE`), and `tools/sound-lab.ts` plays it from there, so its
  typecheck held throughout; its comment that cited the recipes that are
  gone went with the record it sat over.

**Choices the plan left open.**

- **What is tuned** is the family's, as the kit's audition page had it:
  the chain, the pickups, the rescues, the stingers and the voice are in
  key, the rocks, the hazards and the ship are not. The kit's `sounds.json`
  says nothing of tuning, so this is the one place the kit's own play-back
  decided it.
- **A catch with no signature of its own,** the Nova's, plays none, as its
  ring, which it always sets off, is heard; R2 takes the pickup out.
- **The Nova** plays `blast-volatile`, as it played the blast before; the
  plan names no sound for it, and R2 takes it out.
- **The Shield spent** plays `shield-break`, its plan's sound; the kit's
  `hit-iron` also lists `shielded`, which today's game played as iron's
  ring, but the plan's sound for a spent Shield is its own.
- **A held hit of a kind that holds none** (stone, ice, crystal, volatile)
  is given `hit-stone`, so the record is whole; only the boulder, iron and
  the lodestone hold a hit today.
- **One knocked loose** plays `pod-hit`, "pod hit with someone in tow",
  the kit's for the hit; its `crew-loose`, the line snapping, which the
  kit's audition plays 40 ms after it, is commit 4's, with the rest of an
  event's sounds beyond its first.
- **R1's events heard as they are logged:** a wave's clearing takes back
  each of the crew still loose (`takeAllBack()`), each logged as caught
  again, so `crew-caught` plays beside `wave-clear`; and one loose caught
  with the line full goes home, logged as floated, so it plays the soft
