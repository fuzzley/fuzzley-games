## Commit 1: `feat(phaser): …`, a wash and a painter of commands on a canvas

### What it did

- **The wash** (`src/shapes/shapes.ts`). `WashCommand` is an ellipse, an
  `Ellipse` (its middle, its two radii and its turn, in radians), filled
  with a radial gradient of `WashStop`s, each a color at an alpha a share
  of the way out, the whole at the command's own `alpha`, as every
  command's since L0. `wash(ellipse, { stops, alpha })` makes one, its
  radii no smaller than nothing and its stops sorted and held from 0 to 1,
  since a canvas throws at a stop outside them and SVG would clamp an
  unsorted one where a canvas sorts it. `PaintCommand` is
  `DrawCommand | WashCommand`, as §4 says: `DrawCommand` is as it was, so
  every switch over it in the games' art specs stays whole, and the
  compiler keeps a wash out of an atlas.
- **SVG** (`src/shapes/svg.ts`). `svgOf()` takes a `Painting`, a size and
  its paint commands, which a `Drawing` (as it was, a frame's shapes)
  still is; each wash is an `<ellipse>` filled with a `<radialGradient>` of
  its own, `wash-0`, `wash-1` and on, in one `<defs>` before the shapes, at
  its alpha as its fill's opacity and turned in degrees about its middle.
  The gradient's units are its ellipse's bounds, so its edge is the
  ellipse's before the turn. A drawing with no wash has no `<defs>`, so
  every drawing of today is written byte for byte as before.
- **The painter** (`src/scene/paint_canvas.ts`, the canvas painter's file
  §7.3 names). `paintCommands(context, commands)` paints each command in
  turn on a 2D context: a `DrawCommand` call for call as Phaser's canvas
  renderer plays what `atlasFrameOf()` hands a `Graphics` (`fillRect`, the
  rounded rectangle's four arcs, `arc` and `fill` or `stroke`, a path
  through the points, a polygon closed back to its first), in
  `rgba(r,g,b,alpha)` styles as Phaser writes them, alpha 1 where a
  command has none; and a wash as a circle of radius 1, translated, turned
  and scaled to its radii, filled with `createRadialGradient()` of its
  stops at its alpha (`globalAlpha`), inside `save()` and `restore()`. A
  wash with no width or no height paints nothing, as SVG draws none.
  `paintTexture(scene, key, width, height, commands)` paints them into
  the canvas texture of a key through `drawCanvasTexture()`, so it uploads
  once, painted, and paints it again after a lost context.
- **Drawn again after a lost context, one registry.** `bakeTexture()` kept
  a map of each scene's bakes by key to draw again; that map is now
  `keepDrawn(scene, key, draw)` in `bake_texture.ts`, which both
  `bakeTexture()` and `paintTexture()` go through, so a key is drawn again
  the last way it was drawn, whichever of the two drew it. `bakeTexture()`
  behaves as before, its specs unchanged.
- **The docs.** `frontend/lib/phaser/AGENTS.md`'s `src/shapes/` and
  `src/scene/` paragraphs and its rule on textures drawn at run time; the
  `phaser-core` skill's paragraphs on `shapes` and on textures drawn at run
  time, and its triggers (`wash`, `WashCommand`, `PaintCommand`,
  `paintCommands`, `paintTexture`), additions to a shared file (§7.3).

### Choices the plan left open

- **An ellipse, not a circle.** The brief says "a center, a radius"; §4
  says "an ellipse that fades from its middle by stops", and every soft
  shape of the kit is an ellipse, turned, so the wash has two radii and a
  turn. A circle is an ellipse with both radii alike.
- **A color in each stop.** The kit's gradients are each one color at
  several opacities, but a stop of its own color costs nothing and lets a
  wash fade from one color to another; the doc says a stop's color and
  alpha.
- **No linear wash.** The kit draws its vignette as four strips under
  linear gradients and its streaks of wind as rectangles under one. §4
  names only the radial wash, and both port to it (commit 2 says how), so
  the vocabulary gains one kind, not two.
- **Shared redrawing.** The brief's own file for the painter is
  `paint_canvas.ts`; its redrawing after a lost context shares
  `bakeTexture()`'s registry rather than keeping a second map with the same
  listeners, which would also have drawn a key twice, in either order,
  where both painted it. `bake_texture.ts` belongs to no package (§7.3);
  the change keeps its export and its behaviour.
- **`Painting` beside `Drawing`.** Widening `Drawing` to paint commands
  broke Brick Breaker's place tile, a `Drawing` it hands `atlasFrameOf()`;
  so `Drawing` stays a frame's shapes and `svgOf()` takes the wider
  `Painting`, which a `Drawing` is.
- **The spec holds the painter to Phaser, not to a list.** Each shape is
  painted on a context that records every call, and drawn by
  `atlasFrameOf()` into a `Graphics` whose `generateTexture()` plays it on
  another such context; the two lists of calls must match, but for the
  set-up Phaser's renderer makes before the shape and the restore after it,
  and the `Graphics`' default styles, which paint nothing and are cleared
  from its buffer first.

### Choices the plan left open

- **The stars' streams.** The kit seeds each depth by its name's length,
  so its far and mid stars come from one stream and the mid lie on the
  first far ones; each depth here has a stream of its own.
- **The air holds still.** `plan.md`'s "Nearly still" lets each place's
  air drift on a wind of its own; C12, which settles what moves, names the
  near stars and the Storm's fork alone, and a drifting air would be a
  third picture the field's size moving every frame. So the air is painted
  with the landmark, still, and the kit's winds are not ported.
- **One slide for the nearer stars.** The kit slides its mid stars by 1.2%
  of the pod's travel and its bright ones by 3%; C12 makes the nearer stars
  one picture, so they slide together, by 3% (`NEAR_SLIDE`).
- **A streak of wind is a wash.** The kit's is a strip under a linear
  gradient that fades at both ends; the vocabulary has only the radial
  wash, so a streak is a long ellipse, its radius across it its thickness,
  fading from its middle by the kit's stops read from the middle out. It
  is a hair wide, and the quiet measure leaves it out, as the kit's does.
- **The vignette is four washes.** Each edge's strip is a wash whose
  middle lies on the edge, its radius into the field the vignette's depth
  and its radius along the edge eight times the field's longest side, so
  it fades in from the edge as the kit's linear strip does, within a tenth
  at its very ends.
- **A bank's shade, in a light palette, is one flat color.** The kit lays
  the shades of a bank's billows in a group at 0.45 opacity, so where two
  cross they show once. A group's opacity needs a picture of its own, so
  each shade here is the rim's color mixed 0.45 of the way into the field,
  painted whole, which is the kit's over the bare field and within a
  twentieth of the light wash's tint of it elsewhere.
- **A flash is what a flicker adds.** A flicker lifts its fork's marks by
  1.2 times again at its height (`FLICKER_LIFT`). A flash is those marks
  at that lift, then the billows and faces that stand before them in the
  landmark, as they are, over the area the marks reach, which the painter
  shows over the front as strongly as the flicker is: the lift adds to the
  fork, and a billow before it hides the added light as it hides the fork,
  its own color over itself changing nothing. The kit draws the air and the
  vignette over the forks; here the lift lies over them, which only the
  left edge's vignette, under a fifth of a short side deep, would show.
- **Sizes by the CSS pixel.** The kit's least sizes (a star's radius, a
  line's width, a seam) and `FINE_FROM` are pixels of its sheet; here they
  are CSS pixels, times the pixel ratio, so a phone at 3× draws its stars
  as wide as a laptop at 1×, and fine marks show where a unit is 9 CSS px
  or more.
- **The constellation is the game's.** Endless keeps the constellation
  `pilot_art.ts` gives (`CONSTELLATION`), at the place the frame lays it,
  where the kit has a copy of its own, which it lays with two draws of the
  run's seed before it strews Endless's smudges; so Endless's air is drawn
  from the same seed as the kit's, two draws sooner, and differs from it.
- **Lines are canvas paths.** The kit strokes its lines with round ends
  and corners; a paint command's line takes the canvas's own, square ends
  and mitred corners, as the painter plays it as a `Graphics` would. On a
  fork's sharp corners that shows as a small point, a hair wide and as
  faint as the line.
- **Geometry's fallbacks.** A ring indexed past its end takes a corner
  (`?? NO_CORNER`, `?? corner`), as the game's art does, rather than a
  non-null assertion, which lint asks for and the code has none of.

### Choices the plan left open

- **Image copies, not a tiled sprite.** A `TileSprite` over the nearer
  stars builds its pattern only for a new frame object, so a repaint at a
  new size needs its texture removed and made again; removed, a second
  scene in the same game drew a destroyed texture (`glTexture` null) and
  the scene's texture keys reordered. Nine images of one texture tile it
  with no pattern and no removal, one copy per pixel.
- **A bleed past the field.** The field's camera keeps its viewport on
  the field and moves its scroll as the field kicks and shakes, up to
  8 CSS px; the flat field never showed that, a painted place would, as a
  bare strip. So the back and the front are painted that much larger each
  way, and the nearer stars' copies reach it.
- **The texture keys are shared.** Like the atlas's, `belt-place-*` keys
  live in the game's texture manager, which every scene of it shares; a
  later scene paints them again, and none is removed.
- **A flash under what stands before it.** A flash first held the lifted
  marks and the billows before them, so a billow hid the added light; but
  over the front, at a flicker's height, it rubbed out the air and the
  vignette where those billows lay, cut at the flash's straight edges. So
  the Storm is painted in a fourth picture apart, from the bank at its
  foot on, with the air and the vignette, laid over the flashes, and a
  flash holds only its lifted marks: the place at rest is the same picture
  as before, in two textures. Only a place that flashes has the fourth,
  so the other three keep three field-sized textures.
- **One repaint for all its pictures.** A new Endless wave repaints the
  back and the near stars with the front, though only the front holds the
  constellation: it happens once a wave, under the Cleared card, and two
  sets of keys would double what `paints()` compares.
