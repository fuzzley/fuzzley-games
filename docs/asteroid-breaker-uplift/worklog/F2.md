## Commit 1: `refactor(asteroid-breaker): …`, the rings as bands

### What it did

F1 left it ("`RingPainter`'s rings are still `Graphics` circles"), and the
coordinator handed it on: a spent Shield's, a lost pod's, a vent's and the
sights' rings, and the still marks, were stroked with `strokeCircle()`, a
hundred corners each made anew each frame, as many as twelve at once, each
again past an edge it reaches across; F2's rings add to them.

- **`RingPainter`** fills each ring and mark as a band of triangles
  through `rounds.ts`' `strokeRound()`, as the wave's ring already was,
  `RING_WIDTH` wide about the same circle, in the same color and
  opacity: the same picture, its sides within half a pixel of the circle,
  and, like the blast's bands, never overlapping itself at part alpha,
  where a wide stroke's joins overlap inside each bend. Each copy past an
  edge takes its own fill style, so each band is its own run of
  triangles. A ring the frame it is played, swept out nowhere yet, fills
  nothing, as the circle of no size it stroked drew nothing.
- **The specs:** `roundsOf()` and `FilledRound`, which `flourishes.spec.ts`
  kept for the blasts and the boulders, move to `src/testing/belt_board.ts`
  with the fill's opacity, beside a new `bandsOf()`, which reads each
  round as a ring's band (its middle and the circle down its middle,
  rounded to a millionth of a pixel, its color and its opacity), so both
  flourish specs read rings alike; the rings', the still marks' and the
  sights' specs read each ring's band where they read a stroked circle;
  `playingIn()` counts every band the ring painter fills, the wave's
  among them, in place of its strokes and the wave's triangles apart; a
  new spec holds a ring's band `RING_WIDTH` wide, its sides within half a
  pixel of its circle, with no circle drawn; and another a ring the frame
  it is played filling nothing.
- **The docs:** `RingPainter`'s, the game's `AGENTS.md` and the testing
  skill say each ring is a band and name `roundsOf()` and `bandsOf()`.
- **Outside F2's own files:** `src/testing/belt_board.ts`, add-only, takes
  `roundsOf()` and `FilledRound` from F1's `flourishes.spec.ts`, and gains
  `bandsOf()`, `Band` and `toMillionths()`.

### Review

A fresh `general-purpose` agent reviewed the staged change, found the
band's middle, radius, width, color, opacity and copies the stroke's, and
made five points, each taken:

1. **A ring the frame it is played showed a dot,** a band of no radius
   being a disc as wide as the band, where the stroked circle of no size
   drew nothing. Taken: a ring with no reach yet fills nothing, and a
   player's spec plays one and draws it at once.
2. **The spent Shield's spec read that dot,** stepping a single frame.
   Taken: it steps three, as the sights' spec does.
3. **`AGENTS.md` gave the wave's ring `RING_WIDTH`,** and named each copy's
   fill style as a rule. Taken: the sweeping rings and marks are
   `RING_WIDTH` wide, the wave's soft outside and line each a band; why
   each copy sets its fill is a comment at the line.
4. **The two specs each had their own band reader.** Taken: `bandsOf()`
   in `belt_board.ts`, beside `roundsOf()`.
5. **The testing skill didn't name the moved reader.** Taken: it names
   `roundsOf()` and `bandsOf()`, held through the scene's specs.

It noted that Phaser's `FillTri` makes two arrays for each triangle it
fills, so a band's triangles may cost no less script than a stroke's
segments: the frame's trace at F2's end measures it with the rest.
