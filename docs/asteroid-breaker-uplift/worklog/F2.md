## Commit 1: `refactor(asteroid-breaker): …`, the rings as bands

### What it did

F1 left it ("`RingPainter`'s rings are still `Graphics` circles"), and the
coordinator handed it on: a spent Shield's, a lost pod's, a vent's and the
sights' rings, and the still marks, were stroked with `strokeCircle()`, a
hundred corners each made anew each frame, as many as twelve at once, each
again past an edge it reaches across; F2's rings add to them.

- **`RingPainter`** fills each ring and mark as a band of triangles
  through `rounds.ts`' `strokeRound()`, as the wave's ring already was,
  `RING_WIDTH` wide about the same circle, in the same color and
  opacity: the same picture, its sides within half a pixel of the circle,
  and, like the blast's bands, never overlapping itself at part alpha,
  where a wide stroke's joins overlap inside each bend. Each copy past an
  edge takes its own fill style, so each band is its own run of
  triangles. A ring the frame it is played, swept out nowhere yet, fills
  nothing, as the circle of no size it stroked drew nothing.
- **The specs:** `roundsOf()` and `FilledRound`, which `flourishes.spec.ts`
  kept for the blasts and the boulders, move to `src/testing/belt_board.ts`
  with the fill's opacity, beside a new `bandsOf()`, which reads each
  round as a ring's band (its middle and the circle down its middle,
  rounded to a millionth of a pixel, its color and its opacity), so both
  flourish specs read rings alike; the rings', the still marks' and the
  sights' specs read each ring's band where they read a stroked circle;
  `playingIn()` counts every band the ring painter fills, the wave's
  among them, in place of its strokes and the wave's triangles apart; a
  new spec holds a ring's band `RING_WIDTH` wide, its sides within half a
  pixel of its circle, with no circle drawn; and another a ring the frame
  it is played filling nothing.
- **The docs:** `RingPainter`'s, the game's `AGENTS.md` and the testing
  skill say each ring is a band and name `roundsOf()` and `bandsOf()`.
- **Outside F2's own files:** `src/testing/belt_board.ts`, add-only, takes
  `roundsOf()` and `FilledRound` from F1's `flourishes.spec.ts`, and gains
  `bandsOf()`, `Band` and `toMillionths()`.

## Commit 2: `feat(asteroid-breaker): …`, a sector's last rock

### What it did

`plan.md`'s row: "a sector's last rock: the field slows to a third for a
moment, then the wave's ring, 250 ms". F1 slowed the flourishes at every
wave's last rock, as the scene couldn't tell a sector's last, and left
it, as the brief has it, for F2 to build: the slow at a sector's last
wave alone, and the wave's ring at every clearing, as the kit's
`clearedFx()` is a wave's.

- **The frame says so.** `FieldFrame` gains `endsSector`, whether the
  wave is its sector's last; `WavePlay` takes it, false unless told, and
  `GameService` tells it as it makes a wave's play, from the sectors'
  spans it already keeps (`sectorSpans()`), so an Endless wave, whose
  sector has no last, never ends one.
- **The scene reads it.** `PaintContext` gains `endsSector`, which the
  scene writes from each frame before its events play, as it writes the
  place, and the flourish player's stage reads: `"slow"` slows its clock
  only there. `"wave"` starts the wave's ring as long after the last rock
  as the slow lasts, slowed there (`SLOWED_MS` of the flourishes' time)
  or not (`SLOW_MS`), so on every wave it is gone as the Cleared card
  opens, as F1 sized it.
- **The specs:** `wave_play.spec.ts` holds the frame with no sector's
  end unless told, and with one; `game.service.spec.ts` a sector's last
  wave ending it, with another sector after it and as the last written,
  another of its waves not, and an Endless wave not;
  `flourish_player.spec.ts` the clock slowed at a sector's end, its pace
  kept at another wave's, and the ring's wait there; `flourishes.spec.ts`
  plays the clearing's specs on a sector's last wave, and holds another
  wave's last points rising at their pace, the ratio its slow spec
  compares, and its ring waiting unslowed.
- **The docs:** the frame's, `WavePlay`'s, `FlourishStage`'s, the
  timing's, the clock's and `cleared.ts`' docs, and the game's
  `AGENTS.md`, say the slow is a sector's last rock's.
- **Outside F2's own files, each added to:** `frame.ts` (`endsSector`);
  `painter.ts` (`endsSector`, and one doc line changed, `now`'s, to say a
  sector's last rock); `belt_scene.ts` (the context's `endsSector` in
  `create()`, a line writing it in `update()` beside the place, and one
  comment line changed, the clock's, to say a sector's); `game.service.ts`
