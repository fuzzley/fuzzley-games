# Work log: L6, the pod and its pilot

L6 is `implementation-plan.md`'s §6 L6, as C15 settles it: Bumble, the pod
the ship becomes, lit as the rocks are; its pilot upright in its round
canopy, a fuzzball 1.18 units across, its face chosen by `faceOf()`, its
look toward where the pod points or what threatens it, its fur leaning
from a thrust or a dash and lifted by the chain's step until R2 hands it
static's; the canopy's rim filling as the dash charges, with one flash as
it fills; the Shield's glass shell, the dashed ring while it is
untouchable; the flame and the exhaust after the kit's `fx.mjs`; a lost
run's pilot bailing out; and the strays adrift drawn in the same art. The
branch is `abu/l6`, in `.claude/worktrees/abu-l6`, cut from
`asteroid-breaker-uplift` at `4e7c1bee`, after L1, L5 and R1, and rebased
onto `7040517b` once L3 and S3 had landed.

Before the first edit, Asteroid Breaker was built at `4e7c1bee` and its
`dist/` kept in the scratchpad as the "before" of §9's frame budget.

## Commit 1: the pilot's face as a rule of the view

### What it did

- **`src/view/art/faces.ts`** (new) holds the face rule, which the pod's
  painter draws and P1's face cam will show, each from the same function:
  - `faceOf(field, moments, motion)`: over a cleared wave, happy; in a dash,
    squeeze; for `HIT_GUARD_MS` after a hit, dizzy; for 600 ms after a near
    miss or a Shield's save, eep; for 1.2 s after a stray brought home, one
    of the crew caught again or the clearing, happy; while the pod waits,
    sleepy; thrusting or firing (the held fire, not the autofire), set; a
    blink now and then where motion is welcome; else rest. With no pod,
    rest.
  - `FaceMoments`, the steps at which what a face answers last happened,
    which its keeper notes from each event (`hearFace()`: `joined`, `home`,
    `recaught` and `cleared` cheer, `knocked` knocks, `shielded` startles)
    and from each frame's field (`watchFace()`: a near miss is a live rock
    or a crossing comet within `NEAR_MISS`, half a unit, of the pod's hit
    circle, as C20 counts one).
  - `isBlinking(steps, pilot, motion)`: every 4 s for 160 ms, by the
    field's steps, never a clock, each pilot's color 700 ms after the color
    before it; `adriftFaceOf()`, a stray's plead between its blinks.
  - `lookOf(field, angle, into)`: at the nearest live rock or crossing
    comet within `WATCH_REACH`, 6 units of the hit circle, else the way the
    pod points as drawn.
  - `liftOf(field)`: the chain's multiplier less one, 0 to 3, falling as
    its time runs out or the wave clears.
  - `pushOf()` and `easeLean()`: the push on the fur, a unit at full
    thrust and `DASH_LEAN`, 2, in a dash, and the lean eased toward it, most
    of the way in 60 ms as it grows and 450 ms as it lets up, so the fur
    lags a thrust; at once where motion isn't welcome.
- **`FieldView`** (`src/view/field/frame.ts`, add-only) gains the chain's
  `chain` and `lastBreakAt`, which the rules' `Field` already holds, for
  `liftOf()`.
- **`AGENTS.md`** describes `faces.ts` beside the rest of `src/view/art/`.

### Choices the plan left open

- **Moments rather than a field alone.** The field keeps no step for a hit,
  a catch or a near miss, and the pod's guard is set alike by a hit, a
  Shield's save and a stretched field, so the face can't be read from the
  field's state alone. The keeper notes events as it hears them, at the
  step the frame stands at, within a frame of when they happened. P1 keeps
  its own `FaceMoments`, notes the same events and calls `watchFace()` each
  frame. Once R2 logs `grazed`, `hearFace()` can take it and `watchFace()`
  can go.
- **A cleared field comes first.** `stepField()` steps a cleared field no
  more, so a dash, a hit or a near miss at the clearing would otherwise
  hold its face, and a dash its push, for the whole Cleared card.
- **`liftOf()` copies the chain's rule** (`scoring.ts`'s private
  `chainNow()`) rather than narrowing `chainMultiplier()`'s parameter in
  the rules, which are not L6's, and which R2 rewrites as it takes the
  chain out; R2 points `liftOf()` at static's step.
- **The flourishes' `eyesOpen()`** still blinks the ship's and the strays'
  pilots by the scene's clock; commit 2, which draws them with `faceOf()`,
  takes it out.

## Commit 2: the pod, its pilot and the strays in the new art

### What it did

- **`src/view/art/pod_art.ts`** (new; `ship_art.ts` and its spec go) draws
  Bumble from the kit's `ship.mjs`, `PODS` and `pods.json`:
  - its hull (`podHullFrame()`), 24 facets (22 in the accent, the two
    nacelles' trim in the muted text) cut as `bumble()` cuts them, its
    collar of twelve, prow and engines, outlined in the field's color with
    the canopy's outline circle, flat, then each facet in its ink facing

    map hold it and Phaser's lights light it;
  - in the field's atlas (`podFrames()`): the glass, at `CANOPY`, 0.68
    units, with its shade away from the light; the rim, charged, lit toward
    the light; its gauge in 24 steps (`gaugeFrame()`), filling clockwise
    from the top; the rim's flash, 300 ms, growing a third as it fades
    (`flashAlpha()`, `flashScale()`); the glint; the Shield's wash and its
    shell at 1.64 units; the guard's dashed ring at 1.84; a dash's streaks
    and ghosts (`ghostScale()`, each 8% smaller further back); and the flame
    behind each engine in three flickers 50 ms apart by the field's steps
    (`flameFrame()`, `flickerAt()`), its body and bright middle as
    `fx.mjs`'s `energyOf()` colors them, as long as the thrust
    (`flameShare()`);
  - the exhaust's puffs (`PUFF`, `puffColors()`, `puffAlpha()`,
    `puffRadius()`), as the kit's `puffSvg()` swells and fades them, from
    the flame's hot middle to its body by a fifth of their life, then to
    the muted text;
  - where the field has under 10 CSS px a unit, no glint, no shade, no
    rim once charged, no streaks and the glass 0.3 CSS px wider, a 1 CSS
    px gauge inside it, as the kit's `podSvg()` drops them;
  - the reaches: `POD_REACH` 2.11 units (the old `SHIP_REACH` covered the
    rings too, as this does), `FLAME_REACH` 1.78, `STREAKS_REACH` 3.19.
- **`pilot_art.ts`**, rewritten: the pod's pilot, white for the scene to
  tint, at `PILOT_SIZE`, 1.1779 units, its fur flat filling 80% of the
  canopy, at each of four lifts and four leans (`LEANS`, none, half a
  thrust, a thrust, a dash; `furFrame()`), held inside the rim, or the
  glass where fine marks don't show; static's sparks at each lean, halo
  and core apart (`sparksFrames()`, none in the small form); a pilot
  adrift and in a fright (`ADRIFT_FRAMES`); and each seat's eyes at each
  of the nine faces (`eyesFrame()`), looking straight out, moved by
  `eyesShift()`. The field's atlas holds 104 frames at the specs' scale.
- **`PodPainter`** (`ship_painter.ts` renamed) draws, back to front, the
  streaks, the flame, the wash, the hull (lit, from the rocks' atlas), the
  glass, the fur tinted in the pilot's ink and turned the way it leans,
  static's sparks, the eyes at `faceOf()`'s face, moved by `lookOf()`, the
  rim or its gauge, the flash, the glint, the shell and the guard's ring,
  which blinks now while the pod no longer does. It hears each event
  through `hear()`, which `BeltScene.play()` calls first.
- **`StrayPainter`**: the stray adrift pleading, blinking by the field's
  steps, its eyes on the pod; the pilot bailing out in a bubble in tow's
  size (`BAIL_OUT_RADIUS`), wide eyed (`eep`) and its fur puffed, looking
  back where its pod was lost. **`TrailPainter`** lays the pod's ghosts,
  tapering. **`ExhaustPainter`** lets a puff go from each engine in turn,
  0.4 units behind it, drifting 1.5 units back over its life within 18° of
  straight, swelling from 0.13 to 0.4 units and fading; `EXHAUST_MS` is
  400, as `exhaustFx()` lives (it was 300).
- **`flourish.ts`** loses the flame's flicker and the blinks, which the
  view's art and faces now hold.

### Choices the plan left open

- **The hull shares the rocks' atlas and normal map** rather than a pair of
  its own: one bake, one `setDataSource()`, the lights already on it, and
  L1's log weighed the two alike. `litFrames()` in `art.ts` gathers what
  that atlas holds, the rocks then the hull, for the bake and its spec.
- **The flame's shape is `ship.mjs`'s per engine,** colored as `fx.mjs`'s
  energy is; its halo, a glow, is L4's.
- **The fur turns, its frames don't multiply:** four lifts and four leans
  baked leaning along x, the scene turning them the way the push goes, and
  the push quantized to the nearest lean (`leanStep()`).
- **Empty frames where fine marks don't show,** so the painter draws the
  same parts at every size.
- **The lean eases by the scene's frame time,** not the field's steps, and
  the flash fades by it, so both settle over a cleared wave whose field
  stands still; a cleared wave puts the flame out and ends the streaks, as
  `pushOf()` and `faceOf()` treat it. Between one pod and the next the
  painter forgets the charge and the lean.
- **The glass's shade mixes 0.22 of the accent in a light palette,** not
  the kit's 0.3: at 0.3 pilots fell to 2.71:1 on it in Soft pastel and
  2.90:1 in Classic felt; at 0.22 the least is 3.11:1.
