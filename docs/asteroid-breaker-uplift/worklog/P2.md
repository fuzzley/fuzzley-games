# Work log: P2, one card between waves

P2 builds `implementation-plan.md`'s C23 in Asteroid Breaker's shell: one
card between waves, the Cleared card, its sum counting up (whole at once
where motion isn't welcome), with the crew and the next wave by its number
and name, and one press going on from it, a flight key or a press on the
field being the next wave's first move; and the sector's card over the
waiting wave at the start of the run or of a sector, until the first move.
The Ready card goes, and the announcer says both cards. The newest entry is
last.

- **Branch:** `abu/p2`, in `.claude/worktrees/abu-p2`, cut from
  `asteroid-breaker-uplift` at `da697c6e`.
- **Owned:** §7.3's P2 files: `src/app/board/`, `src/app/play/`,
  `src/view/run_frames.ts`, `src/view/run_state.ts`, `src/view/tally.ts`
  and `src/testing/play.ts`. R1 changes the rules beside it, and touches
  `game.service.ts`, `board_card.component.ts` and `run_frames.ts` a
  little; what P2 changes there is the cards and the flow, and none of the
  rules' data.

## 1. One card between waves, its sum counting up, and a sector's card over its first wave

### What it did

- **The sum counting up.** `tally.ts`'s `tallyShownAt()` takes the cleared
  wave's tally and how long the card has shown, and returns each line and
  the total as they stand then: each line counts up in turn, a fifth of
  `TALLY_COUNT_MS` (1,200 ms) each, on `@fz/core/math`'s `easeOutCubic`
  over `shareDone`, and the total is the sum of the lines shown, so it
  never runs ahead of them. `nextWaveWords()` says the wave to come ("Next:
  wave 10, Boulder"). `tallyLines()` goes. `RunFrames` hands each frame's
  share of the count (`FrameChange.countMs`): the frame's own time while
  the wave stands cleared, or the whole count at once where motion isn't
  welcome, and nothing on the frame that clears it. `withFrame()` adds it to
  `RunState.tallyMs` once the card is open, up to `TALLY_COUNT_MS`, and
  keeps the same state once the sum is whole, and `nextWave()` sets it to
  nothing again. The clearing's words end with the wave to come ("Wave 1
  cleared! Score 1,040. Next: wave 2, Last.").
- **The Cleared card** is the trophy over the last sector's last, the
  title, the sum as a list (`dl.tally`), its lines and their total under a
  rule, in the page's text, bold, since the accent isn't held to 4.5:1 for
  text; the crew as the run holds it now (`<app-pilot-row>` over
  `crew().flying`, with its words hidden but to a screen reader); the next
  wave by its number and name (`GameService.waveAfter`, now public); Next
  wave; and "or just start flying". The run's score left it, as the mock
  shows none.
- **One press going on.** The flow was already there: the scene presses
  the key into the steering, then asks `go`, so the new wave's ship takes
  the last frame's steer as held and the key is its first move. A spec now
  holds it at each layer: `GameService` (`pressOn()`, new in
  `src/testing/play.ts`, asks `go` and hands one frame of a shot: wave 2
  plays, and no card shows), the card, and the board, whose scene spec
  clears a wave and goes on at one Space, which starts wave 2.
- **The sector's card.** `src/app/play/sector_card.ts` holds its words:
  `sectorCardOf()` returns it over the run's first wave, wherever that is in
  its sector, or a sector's first, and null over any other: what it opens
  ("A new run" or "A new sector"), the sector's name and its waves
  (`wavesInWords()`, moved from the Sectors dialog, which imports it), what
  is new there (`SECTOR_NEWS`, a sentence or two for each of the game's
  sectors by its id, Endless's among them) and the wave by its number and
  name. `GameService.sectorCard` shows it while the wave waits
  (`outcome() === "ready"`). The card over the run's start adds how to fly.
  `describeSector()` says it, and after it the wave's own words and "It
  starts when you fly.": as each run begins (the page's first too, from the
  constructor), as the run goes on into a sector, and after "Going on." as
  a run taken up there goes on.
- **The card until the first move.** A key or a press that goes on into a
  sector's first wave, or starts a run once an ended run's result is put
  away, would be that wave's first move, and its card would show for one
  frame. So `GameService` overrides `RunLifecycle`'s `go()`, the path the
  keys and presses take (not the buttons', which never reach the
  steering), to mind that one asked (`pressedGo`) until the next frame.
  A wave laid out meanwhile under the sector's card, by `nextWave()` or
  `begin()`, or let wait by `resume()` as a run taken up before its first
  move goes on, marks the press held (`holdAPress()`, `holdThePress`), and the
  next `tick()` lays the wave out again with that frame's steer as held. A
  held key is no move until it is let go and pressed again, so the card
  shows until a fresh move. A run started by a button, or a wave gone on to
  by Next wave, holds nothing.
- **The cards.** `<app-board-card>`'s `card` is a union by kind,
  `sector`, `paused`, `cleared` or `ended`, each carrying its own view model
  (the `SectorCard`, the `TallyShown`), taken into a `@let` and narrowed by
  an `@switch` on its kind that ends in `@default never;`. The Ready card
  and `waveTitle` go. The sector's card sits at the top of the field, clear
  of the ship waiting in its middle: what it opens in small capitals, the
  name large, its waves over a short rule in the accent, what is new, and
  "Wave 11, Ore · starts when you fly".
- **The cards' size.** The layer's own cards now lie in the field's room
  (`.room`), a size container of its own, since what they must fit is the
  field, whose height the deck takes a share of on a phone upright, not the
  board. On a field under 280 px wide or 420 px high they set smaller
  (`board-card-small`), the Cleared card's sum closer, its lines at 1.25;
  on one under 300 px high its sum shows its total alone. The board's own
  query, under 280 px wide or 360 px high, still sets the canvas's cards
  smaller. The room's `cq` units would narrow the toolkit's card, which
  sets its width two margins in from the board's shorter side, so the card
  is at most a margin in from each side of the field instead. These rules
  come last in the stylesheet, so they override those above them.
- **Outside P2's files:** `wavesInWords()` moved out of
  `src/app/sectors/sectors.component.ts`, P3's and P4's, into
  `sector_card.ts`, and the dialog imports it;
  `tools/check-layout.mjs`'s doc comment says which card the check finds
  over the waiting wave; the game's `AGENTS.md`, and the
  `asteroid-breaker-testing` and `asteroid-breaker-content` skills (the
  latter names `SECTOR_NEWS`, which a new sector writes for).

### Choices the plan left open

- The kicker says "A new run" over the run's first wave, wherever it
  starts, and "A new sector" over a sector the run goes on to; how to fly
  shows on the run's card only.
- Each sector's news is a sentence or two of what its waves bring first, in
  the shell, beside the card, since the rules' data stays R1's. A sector
  with none written shows none, and a spec holds every sector to one or two.
- The card's words end with the wave's own and "It starts when you fly.";
  the run's first move still says its wave, as before, so the run's first
  wave is said twice, as its card shows and as it starts.
- The tally's labels are today's: R1 and R2 change what they count.
- The crew is `crew().flying`, today's count of pilots; R1 reshapes `Crew`,
  so its integration picks what the row draws.
- A run a key or a press starts waits under its card as a sector gone on
  to does, so the key that put away the last run's end doesn't fly the
  next.
- On the shortest fields the Cleared card keeps its total, the crew, the
  wave to come, Next wave and "or just start flying", and leaves out the
  sum's lines, which the total counts up without.

### The review

The first review found nothing blocking, and six points, each checked
against the code:

1. **A run a flight key or a press starts showed its card for a frame at
   most,** as the key that put away the ended run's result was the new
   run's first move, and `start()`'s words took the place of the card's.
   Taken, but not as given: holding a press in every `begin()` holds one a
   button started too, and the specs' runs, begun by `startAt()` and flown
   at once on a held shot, would wait for a fresh one. So `go()`, the keys'
   and the presses' path, minds that one asked until the next frame, and a
   wave laid out meanwhile under the sector's card, by `nextWave()` or
   `begin()`, holds it. "Holds no press over a new run begun after a press
   went on" goes, since a key and New game in one frame can't happen in
   play, where the question takes frames; three specs take its place: a
   run a key starts waits under its card, goes at a fresh move, and one
   New game starts holds nothing.
2. **"Counts the next wave's sum up from nothing" passed without the
   reset.** Taken as given: the first sum now counts up whole before the
   next wave, and the second stands at one frame's time.
3. **The Cleared card had grown, and nothing checked it at the smallest
   shape.** Taken, further than asked: dropping "or just start flying" saved
   21 px of the 47 the smallest phone needed, and the card ran past the
   field on a small phone on its side and a short window too (Figures). So
   the layer's own cards set themselves by the field's height, in a room
   of its own, and a short field's Cleared card shows its total alone.
   That the layout check could open the Cleared card is left to P3 or P4.
4. **The small set from 560 px high held on every board, deck or not,** so
   a short window with no deck set its cards small with room to spare.
   Taken, differently: the room's query keys the set on the field's own
   height, which is what the deck took a share of, so no `deck` class is
   needed.
5. **Stale docs.** Taken: `GameService`'s class doc says its state changes
   as the sum counts up too, and the lines past the wrap in this log, the
   game's `AGENTS.md` and the testing skill are reflowed. The bar's comment
   is P1's, and is left (What it left).
6. **`wavesInWords()` moved out of P3's and P4's Sectors dialog.** Taken:
   the log says so plainly.
