## 1. The crew on the field: the line in tow, its bubbles and those knocked loose

### What it did

- **`src/scene/painters/tow_painter.ts`** (new), `TowPainter`, in a new
  `tow` layer under the ship (`layers.ts`, add-only), so the line ties on
  under the pod: the line, one `Graphics` drawn again each frame, a
  stretch from the hook behind the pod to each in tow, in the bubbles'
  rim color, 0.05 units wide, a CSS pixel at least; each in tow in a
  bubble 0.7 units in radius (`TOW_RADIUS`, the plan's), the stray's
  bubble frame scaled down, its pilot low in it in its ink, bobbing where
  motion is welcome, each a phase apart (`GOLDEN_ANGLE`); and each knocked
  loose, its bubble swelling from 0.7 to a stray's 1.15 over the rules'
  `LOOSE_SWELL_MS`, puffed and wide eyed for its fright, then pleading,
  its eyes on the pod across the wrap, blinking through its last 2 s
  (`LOOSE_BLINK_MS`) as a pickup does. It makes every image as the scene
  is created, five in tow and five loose (`MOST_CREW - 1` each), and
  hears the field's events for the faces (`hear()`), forgetting them on a
  new field (`start()`). `belt_scene.ts` (add-only) makes it, draws it
  after the shots and before the pod, and has it hear each event.
- **`src/view/field/tow_line.ts`** (new): where the line is drawn
  (`towLineAt()`), from the hook, `TOW_HOOK` behind the pod where it is
  drawn, through each in tow between its last place and its next, each
  laid the short way across the wrap from the one ahead, so the line is
  one unbroken chain that may run off the field; and how far off its
  copies lie, from the chain's bounds (`copyShift()`), so it is stroked
  again past each edge it reaches across, at the corner too. It writes
  into a `TowLine` made once.
- **`src/view/art/crew.ts`** (new), every rule of what a fuzzball on the
  field beside the pilot shows, pure: `towFaceOf()` (happy over a clearing
  or for 1.2 s after a catch, eep for 600 ms after a knock or while a
  rock or a comet passes within a unit of its bubble, `STARTLE_REACH`,
  sleepy while the pod waits, a blink by its color's turn where motion is
  welcome), `towLookOf()` (at the nearest rock or comet within the pilot's
  watch, else at the one ahead, the first at the pod), `looseRadius()`,
  `isLooseFrightened()`, `looseFaceOf()`, `looseLookOf()` and
  `looseBlinks()`.
- **`src/view/field/near.ts`** (new), `nearestThreat()`: the nearest whole
  rock or crossing comet within a reach of a circle, across the wrap, and
  the way to it. L6's `lookOf()` in `faces.ts` had it as two private
  functions; it now calls this, and so do the crew's look and startle, so
  the pilot at the controls and those in tow watch alike. No answer of
  `lookOf()` changed; its specs pass as they were.
- **Specs:** `near.spec.ts`, `crew.spec.ts` and `tow_line.spec.ts` in
  `pure`, and `tow_painter.spec.ts` in `dom`, beside the painter, on
  `startBoard()`: the line's stretches and their copy past an edge, each in
  tow placed, scaled, inked, faced and looking, between steps, the knock's
  eep and its forgetting on a new field, the bob, and each loose one's
  size, fur, face, blink and slot out of play. `layers.spec.ts` takes the
  new layer.
- **Docs:** the game's `AGENTS.md` names `near.ts`, `tow_line.ts`,
  `crew.ts`, the `tow` layer and `TowPainter`, and says the crew's bob
  holds still without motion while one knocked loose still blinks; the
  testing skill says a painter's spec may sit beside it.

### Choices the plan left open

- **One polyline under opaque bubbles.** The line runs from the hook
  through each link's middle and the bubbles, drawn over it, are opaque
  (`palette.bubble`), so only the thread between rims shows; nothing is
  trimmed at a rim, which keeps the line's arithmetic to a place per link.
- **The bubble in tow is the stray's frame scaled,** 0.7 / 1.15, rather
  than a frame of its own, so a loose one's swell is one image growing.
- **A loose one blinks where motion isn't welcome too,** as a pickup about
  to fade does: the blink says its time is running out. Its swell is the
  rules' (it can't be caught until it has swelled), so it shows either way.
- **A cheer beats a fright** in tow, as one knocked loose and caught again
  within 600 ms is good news; L6's pilot keeps its own order.
- **The tow faces hear the pilot's moments** (`hearFace()`), the same
  events, so the crew cheers and eeps with the pilot; a rock passing a
  bubble is read from the field each frame instead, as nothing logs it.
