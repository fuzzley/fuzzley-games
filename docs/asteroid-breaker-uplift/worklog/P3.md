## 1. The crew on the field: the line in tow, its bubbles and those knocked loose

### What it did

  `tow` layer under the ship (`layers.ts`, add-only), so the line ties on

  hears the field's events for the faces (`hear()`), forgetting them on a

  field beside the pilot shows, pure: `towFaceOf()` (happy over a clearing

  watch, else at the one ahead, the first at the pod), `looseRadius()`,
  `isLooseFrightened()`, `looseFaceOf()`, `looseLookOf()` and
  `looseBlinks()`.

### Choices the plan left open

- **One polyline under opaque bubbles.** The line runs from the hook
  through each link's middle and the bubbles, drawn over it, are opaque

  trimmed at a rim, which keeps the line's arithmetic to a place per link.
