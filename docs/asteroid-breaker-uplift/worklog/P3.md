### Choices the plan left open

- **One polyline under opaque bubbles.** The line runs from the hook
  through each link's middle and the bubbles, drawn over it, are opaque
  (`palette.bubble`), so only the thread between rims shows; nothing is
  trimmed at a rim, which keeps the line's arithmetic to a place per link.
- **The bubble in tow is the stray's frame scaled,** 0.7 / 1.15, rather
  than a frame of its own, so a loose one's swell is one image growing.
- **A loose one blinks where motion isn't welcome too,** as a pickup about
  to fade does: the blink says its time is running out. Its swell is the
  rules' (it can't be caught until it has swelled), so it shows either way.
- **A cheer beats a fright** in tow, as one knocked loose and caught again
  within 600 ms is good news; L6's pilot keeps its own order.
- **The tow faces hear the pilot's moments** (`hearFace()`), the same
  events, so the crew cheers and eeps with the pilot; a rock passing a
  bubble is read from the field each frame instead, as nothing logs it.

### Review

A fresh `general-purpose` agent reviewed the staged change.

1. **`towLookOf()` and the startle read a link no one rides,** whose spec
   passed only because both links lay at the origin. Fixed: both take a
   place in the line only while one of the crew rides it (`ridingAt()`),
   the first's "ahead" is the pod by its index rather than `tow[-1]`, and
   the spec now leaves a link laid where it was, one rider fewer.
2. **No spec held a bubble's copy past an edge.** Fixed: one in tow at the
   field's top edge shows again 40 units down, and one knocked loose at its
   left edge again 72 units across.
3. **"A bubble in tow" had two sizes:** L6's `BAIL_OUT_RADIUS`, 0.86, the
   art kit's, documented as a bubble in tow's, and the plan's 0.7 here.
   The line's links, 1.6 apart, hold the crew's to 0.7; the bail-out's is
   L6's look, so its value stays and its three doc comments now call 0.86
   the art kit's bubble in tow, larger than the game's.
4. **`drawBubbled()` read records its callers had to write first, and
   repeats `StrayPainter`'s seat and eyes.** Fixed the first: the drawing
   now holds where it is drawn and the way it looks, and is handed in.
   Declined the second: sharing it means reshaping L6's painter, outside
   P3's files; the two agree on `STRAY_DROP` and `eyesShift()`, the view's.
5. **Two scans of the rocks per bubble a frame** (the startle's and the
   look's). Declined: five bubbles, two scans each over the field's rocks
   and comets, a few hundred distances a frame; folding the startle into
   the look would tie the face's signature to the look's. The frame budget
   below measures it with five in tow.
6. **The bob's phase followed the place, not the pilot,** so a pilot
   jumped in its bubble as it was knocked loose or joined. Fixed: it is
   keyed by the pilot's color, and a spec holds the same pilot in tow and
   loose to the same seat on one frame (it failed with a phase by place).
