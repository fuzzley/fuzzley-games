# Work log: R4, the burr

R4 builds `implementation-plan.md`'s §6 R4 in Asteroid Breaker's rules:
the burr (C25), a small prickly seed that comes in off an edge, wraps once
it is in, turns toward the pod, and clings to it until a shot pops it or a
dash shakes it off, each one clinging taking a share of the pod's thrust
and top speed. Its numbers are a record the field holds; Iron reach's
waves 14 (Anvil), 17 (Foundry) and 19 (Tinder) and Storm's from 23 on send
burrs; it is drawn as six barbed thorns in a dull brown, adrift and
clinging to the pod; it plays the plan's three burr sounds, is announced
as it clings, and the autopilot shoots burrs and dashes them off, the
report counting how many cling. The newest entry is last.

- **Branch:** `abu/r4`, in `.claude/worktrees/abu-r4`, cut from
  `asteroid-breaker-uplift` at `b2bdb71c`.
- **Owned:** §7.3's R files: `src/rules/`, the autopilot and the report's
  helpers in `src/testing/`, `tools/report-waves.mjs` and
  `src/view/debug_tuning.ts`; and the burr's art beside L4's in

## 1. The burr's rule

- **Did:** `src/rules/hazards/burrs.ts` sends a wave's burrs, as many as
  its setup says (`BurrSetup.burrs`, `WaveData.burrs`, none by default,
  grown by the field's area as a wave's counts are), into slots made once,
  as many as may be adrift at once (`burrsAtOnce`, `BURRS_AT_ONCE` 2 on 40
  by 40, grown by area): the first `BURR_FIRST_MS` (6 s) into the wave,
  each next `BURR_NEXT_MS` (6 s) after the last was sent, waiting for a
  free slot. A burr comes in off an edge as a stray does (`comeIn()`,
  `BURR_TILT` 20°), logged `burred` at the edge, flies straight on until
  it lies wholly on the field, then wraps as a rock does (`Burr.wraps`),
  turning toward the ship within `BURR_SIGHT` (12 units) at `BURR_TURN`
  (30°/s), at `BURR_SPEED` (2.5 u/s) whatever the speed. A shot pops it
  (`burrMetBy()`, `popBurr()`): the field lands each shot on the first it
  met in its step, burr or rock, sooner winning; a burr popped counts as a
  break toward the most a Lance's shot breaks, scores `BURR_POINTS` (10)
  times static's multiplier among the rocks' points (`scoreBurr()`), sheds
  one spark (`sparks.ts`' new `shedSparksFrom()`, which `shedSparks()` now
  calls) and is logged `popped`. One that touches the ship clings
  (`clingBurrs()`, logged `clung` with how many cling), up to
  `MOST_CLINGING` (3), past which it pops for nothing; each clinging takes
  `BURR_SLOW` (0.2) of the ship's thrust and top speed (`flyShip()`'s
  `burrSlow`, `Ship.burrs`), none of a dash's throw. A dash shakes them all
  off as it starts (`shakeBurrs()`, logged `shaken` with how many and the
  way it heads). How burrs fly and cling is `Burring`, a value record the
  field holds (`TUNED_BURRING`, `WaveSetup.burring`), and the debug menu
  tunes its speed and its slow (`debug_tuning.ts`' `burrSpeed` and
  `burrSlow`, written back as `BURR_SPEED` and `BURR_SLOW`). The saved
  field keeps the burrs sent and the next's step (`burrsSent`,
  `nextBurrAt`), held to no more than the wave sends; burrs adrift or
  clinging are not kept. The stretch moves a burr as a crossing until it
  wraps, then as a wrapping thing. Endless sends none. The events' sounds:
  `clung` `burr-cling`, `popped` `burr-pop`, `shaken` `burr-shed`, `burred`
  none, so `sounds.spec.ts`' unplayed list loses the three; their
  flourishes are all quiet for now. `describe.ts` says "A burr clings." on
  `clung`. `field_picture.ts` gains the `burrs` and `burrsAtOnce` options
  and `placeBurr()`, and `placeShot()` now gives a shot its weapon's
  breaks, as a pulled shot has, so a spec's Lance flies on through what it
  breaks. `AGENTS.md`, the content skill and the testing skill say so.
- **Outside R's files,** each an entry the compiler asked for, added:
  `src/view/effects/sounds.ts` and `effects.ts` (the four events' rows),
  their specs and `flourish.spec.ts` (the event record's `burrs: 0`),
  `src/scene/belt_scene.spec.ts` (`EVERY_EVENT`'s four rows),
  `tools/sound-lab.ts` (its scene's setup gains `burrs: 0` and
  `burrsAtOnce: 0`); and `AGENTS.md` and the two skills.
- **Choices:**
  - Only a shot pops a burr. A blast, a set piece's vent, a zap's arc and a
    comet pass it by, so `hitWithin()` is unchanged: a burr is no rock, and
    the plan's way to rid the pod of one is to shoot it before it lands or
    dash once it has. A pop counts toward the Lance's breaks, so the Lance
    flies on through a burr as through a rock.
  - A burr clings to a ship untouchable after a hit, or new, as well as an
    unguarded one, since it hits nothing; but not to one waiting or
    dashing, so a dash through burrs is safe and a wave's start is still.
  - A popped burr's 10 points count among the rocks' in the tally, as a
    small target shot, rather than a tally line of its own, which would
    change the result card's and the tally's lines, P's and S's.
  - A burr past the most clinging pops for nothing (`popped`, no points),
    so the third burr's slow is the most a pod carries.
  - The first burr comes 6 s in, after the first stray's 4 s, so the two
    don't arrive together; the next 6 s after each send.
  - A burr flies at one speed at every speed of the run, as a stray does,
    and Slow's pickup leaves it be, so Slow's sentence stays true.
  - Clinging burrs stay on the pod through a clearing, and the next wave's
    new ship starts with none.
  - `RULES_VERSION` stays 2. C22 makes it 2 from R2 for the uplift's rules
    as a whole, and 2 has not shipped, so no player holds a best under 2
    that the burr's points or its waves could make unfair; the plan gives
    no reason to raise it within the uplift. The saved game's version stays
    2 too, also unshipped, the field's two new counts added under it.
