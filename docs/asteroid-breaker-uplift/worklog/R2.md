# Work log: R2, sparks, static and the zap

R2 builds `implementation-plan.md`'s C20 to C22 in Asteroid Breaker's
rules: the chain and the Nova go, and static takes the chain's place as
the multiplier, ×2 from 10, ×3 from 20 and ×4 from 30, carried by the run
from wave to wave; breaks shed sparks, which the pod and the bubbles in tow
gather into static; static drains once no spark has come for a while, and a
hit knocks it a step down; a near miss adds to it, and a dash begun at full
static is a zap, arcing to the rocks along its path. Bests are kept with
the rules they were made under. The saved run keeps static, the autopilot
gathers sparks, the report shows the most static each play reached, and
the debug menu tunes the spark's reach, static's drain and the zap's reach.
How sparks, static and the zap look and sound is P4's, L6's, F2's and S3's;
outside the rules R2 changes only what the compiler and the specs need. The
newest entry is last.

## 1. The chain and the Nova go; static takes the chain's place

- **Did:** took the chain out (`CHAIN`, `CHAIN_MS`, `Field.chain`,
  `lastBreakAt`, `chainMultiplier()`, `endChain()` and the event's `chain`)
  and put static in its place: `src/rules/sparks/charge.ts` holds
  `Field.charge` (`{ level, lastSparkAt }`), the `Sparking` record the field
  holds (`most`, `drain`, from `TUNED_SPARKING`), `stepOf()`, `FULL_STEP`,
  `multiplierOf()` (`multiplierAt()` over `tuning.ts`'s `STATIC_STEPS`),
  `drainStatic()` (after `STATIC_HOLD_MS` with no spark, `STATIC_DRAIN` a
  second) and `knockStaticDown()` (a hit takes it to the foot of the step
  below: 40 to 30, 25 to 10, under 20 to 0). Each step static comes to, up
  or down, is logged at the ship as `stepped`, with `step` and `fell`; a
  break logs static's step as it broke (0 for one a comet smashed), which
  `detuneOf()` raises its sound by. `chainPoints` is `staticPoints`, the
  tally's `chains` is `static`, and the tally's labels are "Static" and "No
  hit taken". The run carries static (`Run.charge`): a wave's field starts
  with it (`FieldSetup.charge`, through `waveStart()`), and a cleared wave
  hands the run what it ended with. The saved run, still version 2, keeps
  the run's static and the field's level and last spark, read only within
  their checks (`hasSoundStatic()`, `isStatic()`, `hasSparkedInTime()`).
  `describe.ts` says "Static full." as static comes to full. The Nova goes:
  its catalog entry, effect, rule and event, its symbol and tag, wave 9's
  pool, and `lasts()` and `isTagged()`, as every effect now lasts.
- **Outside R's files:** the view (`run_frames.ts` and `run_state.ts` hand
  the shell static's multiplier and step in place of the chain, a run's
  state starting from the static its run or its field taken up holds;
  `tally.ts`'s labels; `effects.ts`' `detuneOf()`, the `nova` row gone and
  `stepped` quiet; `flourish.ts`, `art.ts`, `symbols.ts`, `tags.ts` and
