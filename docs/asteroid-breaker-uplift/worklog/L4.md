## Commit 1: `feat(phaser): …`, the soft disc

### What it did

- **`softDisc(scene, key, size)`** (`src/scene/soft_disc.ts`, in
  `@fz/phaser/scene`): a white disc a size across, fading from its middle
  to nothing at its edge, painted once into the canvas texture of the key
  through L3's `paintTexture()`, as one `wash()` filling the texture, so it
  is painted again after a lost context, the last way its key was drawn,
  with no registry of its own.
- **Its fade** is the kit's halo's, `glowDefs()` in `fx.mjs`: 0.9 at its
  middle, 0.42 a third of the way out, 0.13 at 0.62 and nothing at its
  edge, so a halo drawn at the kit's strength is the kit's halo.
- **Its specs** (`soft_disc.spec.ts`): the texture is a canvas texture as
  wide and as tall as the size; what it is painted with, read from the
  texture's own context as it is painted over (the move to its middle, the
  scale to its radius, the gradient and each of its stops); and the same
  again once a lost context is restored.
- **The docs:** `frontend/lib/phaser/AGENTS.md`'s `src/scene/` paragraph,
  and the `phaser-core` skill's paragraph on `paintTexture()` and its
  triggers (`softDisc`, `halo`), an addition to a shared file (§7.3).

### Choices the plan left open

- **The fade is the library's, not the caller's.** §4 gives the signature
  as `softDisc(scene, key, size)`, and every halo of the kit fades the same
  way but a rock's glow, whose kit gradient (`rockDefs()` in `rocks.mjs`:
  0.85 at its middle, 0.35 at 0.35, nothing at its edge) is as strong a
  third of the way out and a little broader past it, 0.21 against 0.13 at
  0.62, so the disc draws its outer half about a third fainter. C13 makes
  every halo one soft disc, so the rocks' take this one, at their own
  strengths, which the halo painter may widen should the glows look thin.
  A game that wants another fade paints its own wash with
  `paintTexture()`.
- **No stops exported.** Nothing outside the library needs them yet: a
  game's view draws a halo by its size and strength, and the scene shows
  the disc.

## Commit 2: `feat(asteroid-breaker): …`, the halos

### What it did

- **The palette's halos** (`src/view/colors/palette.ts`, add-only):
  `BeltPalette.haloStrength`, how strongly a halo shows in the palette, 1
  in a dark one and 0.62 in a light one (`HALO_STRENGTH`, §5.2), the kit's
  `energyOf()`'s `glow`, since a halo on a white field shows only as a
  tint; and `rockGlows`, the color of each light a rock gives off, by its
  tone: a crystal's gems' in its ink, and a volatile rock's core's in its
  pale gold where the field is dark, sunk 35% toward the rock's own ink
  where it is light (`LIGHT_CORE_GLOW`), as the kit's `rockDefs()` mixes
  it, since a gold so pale tints white nothing.
- **The `halos` layer** (`src/view/field/layers.ts`, add-only): after the
  field's frame and before the tags and the rocks, drawn by the field's
  camera, so it kicks and shakes with what it glows round, and no halo
  covers what it glows round.
- **The view's halos** (`src/view/art/energy.ts`, new): a `Halo` is a soft
  ellipse about a thing as it points, in field units, its reach along its
  way and across it, how far ahead of the thing its middle lies, how far
  its way is turned from the thing's, and its strength; `roundHalo()` makes
  a round one about a thing's middle, and `haloAlpha()` shows a strength as
  strongly as the palette lets it, held to what an image shows. A rock's
  come from its shape's glows (`rockHalos()`): a crystal's gems' and a
  volatile rock's core's and heart's, each about the rock's middle as it
  lies unturned, so they turn with it, made once for every kind, size and
  outline, and `MOST_ROCK_HALOS` (2) is as many as any rock gives off.
- **`HaloPainter`** (`src/scene/painters/halo_painter.ts`, new, first in
  the painters after the field's): paints the soft disc once
  (`HALO_DISC`, `belt-halo`, 128 pixels), and shows each halo as an image
  of it, tinted, turned, scaled and faded with `setAlpha()`, under plain
  alpha, never an additive blend: through `Copies`, on both sides of an
  edge it reaches across (`glow()`), or once where it lies, on the field
  or off it, for a thing that crosses rather than wraps, as a comet does
  (`glowOnce()`, through a new `Copies.showOnce()`). As each field starts
  it makes as many as all its rocks could give off at once, and as many
  more as other painters reserve (`reserve()`), so no frame of play makes
  one; each frame it shows the live rocks' halos, then those other
  painters show after it, and hides the rest of what showed the frame
  before.
- **`BeltScene`** (`belt_scene.ts`, add-only) makes it and draws it with
  the others (`BeltKit.halos`), so the shot, pickup and hazard painters to
  come show their halos through it.
- **Its specs:** `energy.spec.ts` (pure) holds the view's halos to the
  shapes' glows, by kind, place, reach and strength, and their alphas in
  both modes; `palette.spec.ts` the halos' strength and the glows' colors,
  the light core's to the kit's number, `#eaa15f`; `halo_painter.spec.ts`
  (dom) runs the scene and holds the halos to their layer, a crystal's
  halo's place, tint, turn, scale and alpha, a volatile rock's two, both
  sides of an edge, none for a stone, none once its rock breaks, the room
  a field makes for them; and another painter's: shown after the rocks',
  a long one's turn, place ahead and scale along and across, its copy past
  an edge as far as it reaches along its way, one shown once off the
  field, hidden the next frame, and nothing past the room made.
  `layers.spec.ts` takes the layer.
- **The docs:** the game's `AGENTS.md` (the palette's `haloStrength` and
  `rockGlows`, `energy.ts`, `HaloPainter`), the testing skill (a painter's
  own spec beside it) and the content skill (a new kind's glows shown as
  halos, each tone in its color).

### Choices the plan left open

- **One halo per glow, no more.** The kit draws each glow of a rock as a
  radial gradient as broad as its `r`, at its strength; here each is one
  image of the soft disc as wide, its alpha the glow's strength times the
  palette's, so a rock's halo is its kit glow under the disc's fade (Commit
  1's choice), which keeps one draw a glow.
- **0.62 on a light field, for the rocks too.** The kit fades a rock's
  glows to 0.7 of their strength in a light palette (`rockSvg()` in
  `rocks.mjs`), where its other halos take `energyOf()`'s 0.62; C13 and
  §5.2 give every halo the palette's one strength, so a light crystal's
  halo shows at 0.31 here against the kit's 0.35.
- **The rocks' halos sit in the halos layer, under the rocks,** as the kit
  draws a rock's glows first, behind its outline and facets ("what it
  gives off shows behind it and around it"), and as C13 puts every halo,
  so the crystal's gem glow shows as light round the rock; the face's own
  colors come from L1's lighting. Where a glow reaches past the rock, as a
  volatile rock's core's 1.25 radii, the rim of light shows past it.
- **Images made as a field starts,** two a rock slot and what others
  reserve, as every painter here makes its objects, so a frame of play
  makes none; a halo past them is skipped, not made.
- **Reserved, not registered.** A painter that shows halos says how many
  at most it shows (`reserve()`) and calls `glow()` or `glowOnce()` as it
  draws, after the halo painter, which the painters' order keeps; P4's
  static and L6's flame may take it the same way.
