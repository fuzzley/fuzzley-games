## 1. The face cam, first among the bar's crew, and the chain's pips

### What it did

- **The face and the lift, from the view.** `RunFrames` keeps the face's
  moments, as the pod's painter keeps its own: `createFaceMoments()` once,
  reset whenever the wave in play's field is a new one; `hearFace()` for
  each event the frame hears, at the field's step (`FieldPlay` steps, then
  hears, so the step is the frame's last); and `watchFace()` once a frame,
  for a near miss. Each frame's `FrameChange` carries the face
  (`faceOf()`, given the frame's `motion`, so the pilot blinks only where
  motion is welcome) and the lift (`liftOf()`), whatever the run's
  outcome. `RunState` holds both, at rest and flat as a run begins, and
  `withFrame()` keeps the same state while neither changes, so a frame of
  play still makes nothing. `GameService` hands them on as `face()` and
  `step()`, and the chain's multiplier as `multiplier()`, as §5.3 has P1
  add it, which the bar reads. The moments stay in the view, pure and
  covered, never in a component, as L6 asked.
- **`<app-pilot>`** takes a `face` and a `lift`, at rest and flat unless
  given others, and hands them to `<fz-fuzzball>`.
- **`<app-face-cam>`** (`src/app/face_cam/`) draws the pilot at the controls
  behind the canopy's glass, as the art kit's face cam does: the glass, the
  rim and its third toward the light lit in the pod's own colors, which it
  takes from the view's `podTones()` over the palette on the page (the
  board's `fieldPalette()`), so it and the pod can't drift apart, and a
  glint, white, over the pilot; the rim 6.5% of the cam's width and never
  under 1.5 px, as the kit's is. The
  pilot is sized by how far `fuzzballDrawing()` says its fur reaches at
  static's last step (`MOST_LIFT`), so at ×4 its fur on end and its sparks
  reach the rim's inner edge, and at rest its flat fur fills about 70% of
  the glass, as in the kit. Its body sits in the glass's middle, raised by
  how far it lies below its grid's. The glass is empty once no one is at
  the controls. It is hidden from assistive technology, and moves nothing
  of its own: the face swaps as the field's does, with no transition.
- **The bar.** The Ships figure's term holds the face cam, the pilot at
  the controls, where it held a small pilot as the figure's icon. The
  Chain figure now shows at every multiplier, ×1 too, as "×2" and four
  pips, one lit for each time a break scores; the pips are hidden from a
  screen reader, which hears "×2", and the face is never said.
  - Where the bar has room (from the width the actions keep their labels
    at, not compact), the figures are four cards: the wave, the score, the
    ships, the face cam at the card's left beside "Ships" over the line in
    tow, and the chain. The cards are a little narrower than the games'
    (56 px at least, 12 px each side), so four leave the title more room.
  - Below that, across the bar, one card (`bar-figures-paired`'s, laid as
    a grid): the face cam with the ships' number as a badge on its lower
    left, in the type scale's smallest size, beside the score over the
    chain and its pips. The score's icon goes there, for room (below). On
    a rail, the score, the face cam with its badge, and the chain stack,
    each a card.
  - On a compact bar, New game moves into the overflow menu, after
    Sectors, as Brick Fuzz Pop's does, leaving Pause and the menu.
  - The comment about the Ready card, gone since P2, goes (P2's note).
- **Specs.** `poseIn()`, in `src/testing/pilots.ts`, reads the face and the
  lift of the first fuzzball drawn in an element, matching its eyes' marks
  and its fur's path against what `fuzzballDrawing()` draws for each face
  and each whole step. The face cam's spec holds every face and every
  lift to being drawn (and so `poseIn()` to telling every face apart), the
  pilot in its ink, the canopy in the pod's colors in a light and a dark
  palette, the empty glass and its hiding. The bar's spec holds,
  on a roomy screen, the four figures, the face cam's pilot and those in
  tow, the face sleepy while the wave waits, set as it fires, dizzy as a
  hit knocks one loose and happy over a cleared wave, and, at ×1 to ×4,
  the chain's words, its pips lit (by their `data-lit`) and the fur's
  lift, on `LONG_CHAIN_WAVES`, thirty small stones, as `CHAIN_WAVES`' dozen
  reach only ×3;
  at four compact shapes, the three figures, the badge counting the ships,
  no one drawn in tow, the face and the fur, and ×2 with two pips lit; on
  a compact screen, Pause in the bar and New game in the menu, which

### Choices the plan left open

- **Auto-fire shows no "set".** L6's rule answers the held trigger only,
  and the face cam reads the same rule, so the field and the bar never
  disagree. Firing by itself never stops, so with it "set" would hold for
  the whole wave and the pilot would never rest or blink; a thrust still
  shows "set".
- **The pips follow `multiplier()`,** lit `i < times`, four of them from
  the ladder's last (`CHAIN[0].times`); the lift is `step()`. Both are
  §5.3's, so R2's static takes their place without the bar changing; the
  four pips' count, from `CHAIN`, is what R2 repoints at static's ladder.
- **The Chain figure shows at ×1,** so the bar doesn't shift as a chain
  starts and runs out, and its four pips show the ladder there is to
  climb; the plan's risk note counts four figures on a laptop's bar.
- **New game goes into the menu on a compact bar.** At 360 px the status
  area had 71 px, too little for the face cam, the score and the chain;
  with New game in the menu it has 119 px, which holds a score of six
  figures beside the face cam with the score's icon left out (it needed
  129 with it). Brick Fuzz Pop already keeps New game there.
- **No `@fz/ui` change.** `<fz-fuzzball>`'s `face` and `lift` are enough;
  the face cam leaves `look` and `lean` to the field.

## 2. Rebased onto R2: the face cam and the pips on static

before it did (`8e136c0e`, after L2's `8944df8d`), taking the chain out and
static in, so `abu/p1` was rebased onto it and each conflict settled

### What was settled

- **`RunState`, `FrameChange` and `GameService`.** R2 added `multiplier`
  and `step`, static's multiplier and its step (0 at ×1 to `FULL_STEP`, 4,
  full), the step null in a frame that leaves it; both kept as R2 wrote
  them, and P1's own `multiplier()`, a copy of the chain's, dropped. P1's
  `step`, the fur's lift, isn't static's step, which runs one past the fur's
  last lift, so it is `lift` now (`FrameChange.lift`, `RunState.lift`,
  `GameService.lift()`): `liftOf()`, which R2 repointed at static's
  multiplier less one, 0 to 3, full static on end too. A run begins with
  R2's multiplier and step from the level it carries or is taken up at,
  and P1's pilot at rest, its fur flat, until its field's first frame, as
  before. `withFrame()` keeps the same state while none of the four
  changes.
- **`RunFrames`.** R2 took L6's `watchFace()` out, the pilot eeping at the
  rules' near miss (`grazed`), which `hearFace()` hears; its import and
  call go, as the coordinator said, and nothing else changed there.
- **The bar.** R2 renamed the figure Static, its class `multiplier`, and
  showed it past ×1 alone; P1's figure stands, under R2's name, at every
  multiplier with its pips, which now count `STATIC_STEPS[0].times`, four,
  and the face cam's lift is `lift()`. The one card's grid area and the
  styles follow the class.
- **Specs.** R2 took `CHAIN_WAVES`, `chainUp()` and `clusterOf()` out of
  `play.ts`, so `LONG_CHAIN_WAVES` goes too, and `play.ts` is R2's. The
  bar's spec brings static to each multiplier by R2's `takeUpCharged()`,
  then a frame of the paused run, which shows the fur: at 0, ×1, one pip,
  flat; at each of `STATIC_STEPS`' starts, ×2 to ×4, as many pips and the
  fur a step higher for each. R2's specs of the figure only past ×1 give
  way to P1's of it at ×1. `run_frames.spec.ts` lifts the fur at each of
  static's starts and lays it flat as static drains below its first,
  keeps it through a pause at ×3, and the cleared wave's spec holds the
  face alone, as static, and the fur with it, is carried on.
  `game.service.spec.ts`'s `BEGUN_OWN` and `ownOf()` hold all four;
  P1's specs of `multiplier()` go, R2's "static" ones holding it, and
  the fur's spec takes a run up at ×3 and reads `lift()` after a frame.
- **Docs.** The game's `AGENTS.md` (`run_frames.ts` without
  `watchFace()`, `play/` with `face` and `lift`, the bar on static, the
