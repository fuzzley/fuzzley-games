## Commit 2: `feat(asteroid-breaker): …`, the budget

### What it did

`plan.md`'s budget: the field shakes for a hit and a boulder alone, along
the blow, still within 0.3 s; it holds still for a boulder, a zap and the
run's end only; one flash every 0.34 s at most.

- **The shake** is along its blow now, not round about: `shakeShare()`
  swings the field the blow's way first, then back, dying away, and
  `cameraShift()` adds it to the kick, each `Jolt` (when, and the way it
  came) its own in `Jolts`, the shake's with how long the field held still
  first (`Shake`). A boulder's break shakes the field, in place
  of its kick; a large rock's still kicks it. The field is still again
  `SHAKE_MS` (300 ms) after the blow, the time it holds still first among
  it.
- **The run's end** no longer shakes the field. A lost pod's event has no
  way to shake along, the table's row names no shake, and the budget lists
  the run's end among the holds, which the field frozen at the run's end
  already is, the card waiting `resultDelayMs()` (500 ms, as before, now
  the burst's alone, `SHAKE_DELAY_MS` gone).
- **The hold:** `holdMsOf()` says how long an event holds the field still,
  a boulder's break's first beat (`BOULDER_HOLD_MS`, 90 ms) and nothing
  else's yet. `WavePlay.advance()` takes whether motion is welcome; where
  it is, an event heard holds the field from the next frame, its clock
  skipped for that long, and `RunFrames` hands it the player's motion. The
  shake that follows starts as the hold ends, both counted from the blow.
  A fire or a dash pressed while the field holds still is kept for its
  next step, so a tap made and let go within the hold isn't lost, and a
  frame of no time, less or not a number holds none, as the clock takes
  none from it.
- **The limiter:** `FlourishPlayer` keeps when the field last flashed and
  lets it flash once each `FLASH_GAP_MS` at most, whatever flashes
  (`flashDue()`), forgetting it as a new field starts (`start()`, from the
  scene's `startField()`); `RockPainter.flashSlot()` flashes whatever it is
  handed. The blast's flash and light, and the muzzle's, are to go through
  it.
- **The specs:** `flourish.spec.ts` holds the shake's hold, its way first,
  its dying away, its rest by `SHAKE_MS` and its swing both ways at 60
  frames a second after a boulder's hold, and the camera along the blow
  alone, never across it, a kick and a shake added; `effects.spec.ts`
  holds a boulder's break alone among every event shaking the field,
  `holdMsOf()` for each size and event; `timing.spec.ts` the budget's 0.3 s
  and the hold within it; `wave_play.spec.ts` and `run_frames.spec.ts` the
  hold where motion is welcome, across frames, and none where it isn't,
  the presses kept through it and no longer, and frames of no time;
  `flourish_player.spec.ts` the camera held then shaken along the blow,
  and the limiter across rocks, its gap and a new field;
  `flourishes.spec.ts` a boulder's hold, its shake along the blow both
  ways and its rest within 0.3 s. That a lost pod shakes nothing is
  `effects.spec.ts`' to hold: its event has no way, so a shake of it
  would scroll the camera by nothing a scene's spec could see.
- **`BLEED_CSS_PX`** stays: the kick (2 CSS px) and the shake (6) along a
  blow move the camera 8 CSS px at most, as the kick and the shake along
  each axis did, which the bleed is.
- **What it touched outside its own files:** P2's `run_frames.ts` hands
  `WavePlay` the motion it has, and its spec holds the hold through it;
  L1's `rock_painter.ts` lost its own check of the gap, the player's now;
  `belt_scene.ts`, add-only, starts the player with the painters.

### Choices

- The hold is only where motion is welcome: where it isn't, a boulder's
  break plays no flourish, and a field that stopped for 90 ms would read as
  a stutter.
- "A hit" in the budget is the pod's (`knocked`), whose flourishes the
  table leaves to F2; so today the spec holds a boulder alone among the
  events that shake the field, and F2 adds `knocked`, shaken along its
  way with `FieldCamera.shake()`.
- A zap's hold is F2's too, with its flourish; `holdMsOf()` is where it
  goes.
