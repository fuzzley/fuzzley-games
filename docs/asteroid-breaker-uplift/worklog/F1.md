## Commit 1: `refactor(asteroid-breaker): …`, the times in one place

### What it did

What L2 left: a break draws its dust with its shards, so nothing played
"dust" but a flourish that drew nothing, and the numbers the old breaks
used were left with nothing reading them.

- **`timing.ts`** takes `BREAK_MS` and `HELD_MS` from `breaks.ts`, which
  imports them, so every flourish's time is in one file, as §5.2 has it.
  `SHARDS_MS`, the lost pod's burst's alone now, is `BURST_MS`, beside
  `BURST_SHARDS`, and the "shards" flourish is "break", as `BREAK_MS` and
  `breakRock()` name what it plays.
- **Gone:** the "dust" flourish (`Flourish` loses it, a rock's break calls
  for its break alone, and a large or a boulder's for its kick too, and
  `FlourishPlayer` loses its case); `shardCount()`, `LEAST_SHARDS` and
  `MOST_SHARDS`; `DUST_MS`, `SPARK_MS` and `SPARK_REACH`; and the atlas's
  `spark` frame and `FLOURISH.spark`, which only the old held hit drew. The
  `shard` frame stays for the lost pod's burst and the `puff` for the
  exhaust, their docs saying so.
- **The specs** lose what tested what went: `shardCount()`'s, the dust's
  in `flourish_player.spec.ts`, and the spark frame among the white ones in
  `art.spec.ts`; `effects.spec.ts` holds each size's break without dust.
- **The docs:** the game's `AGENTS.md` says `timing.ts` holds the break's
  and the held hit's times.
- **What it touched outside its own files,** as `integration.md`'s "Left,
  each with an owner" hands F1 what breaks no longer use: L2's `breaks.ts`
  (its two times moved out, imported back) and `breaks.spec.ts` (the same

  `timing.ts`); `art.ts`, add-only to every package, loses the `spark`
  frame and `FLOURISH.spark`, as that list asks, and `art.spec.ts` the
  frame among the white ones; and `flourishes.spec.ts` imports the times
