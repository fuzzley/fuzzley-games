# Work log: L2, breaks and hits

L2 is `implementation-plan.md`'s §6 L2: a rock's break throws its own
facets, tumbling and fading to the field's color, with a ring of dust and a
few sparks of light, all under half a second (the art kit's `makeBreak()`
rule, in the view), and a held hit's spark; the debris painter draws them
from the rock's facets. It is done when a break in a browser looks as
`art/energy-and-effects.svg` does, and the debris specs hold the pieces'
count and time. The branch is `abu/l2`, in `.claude/worktrees/abu-l2`, cut
from `asteroid-breaker-uplift` at `4e7c1bee`, L1's last commit.

Before the first edit, the tip was built (`yarn workspace asteroid-breaker
build`) and its `dist/` copied to the session's scratch folder, as the

## Commit 1: `feat(asteroid-breaker): port the art kit's break and held hit's spark, a rock's own facets thrown from its seed`

### What it did

The view's half, which nothing draws yet: commit 2 draws it.

- **`src/view/art/breaks.ts`.** The kit's `makeBreak()` as `makeBreak()`,
  from a `RockShape` and a seed: the kit's `BREAK_STYLES` for each kind
  (`Record<RockKindId, …>`, so a kind R3 adds must say how it comes apart),
  the rock's facets cut for breaking (a dent's left out, ice's triangles
  split into slivers, iron's joined into plates and its flat top halved),
  the heroes spread round it by angle, each shard's way out, its sway,
  reach, spin, shrink and life, its sparks, its dust's puffs and its
  glints, every draw in the kit's order. `breakOf()` gives each kind,
  size and outline's break, made once as the module loads, from the seed
  its outline is cut from (`FIRST_SEED + outline`), as the kit's sheet
  breaks each rock by its own seed.
- **Where each piece is a time after,** written into a record the scene
  keeps (`Placed`, `Round`, `Streak`), in device pixels from the break's
  middle at an `ArtScale`, rather than a new one each frame: the rock whole for its
  first 30 ms, flashing toward the flash's tone (`wholeAt()`); each shard
  (`shardAt()`), flying out along its way and the blow's push, which
  `shardPushOf()` turns back by the rock's turn, eased out, tumbling by its
  spin, shrinking, its color the shader's for its facet,
  `inLight(ink, litBy(facing, turn, palette.rockLight))` at the turn it has
  tumbled to, flashed first and mixed toward the field's color as it goes,
  and its alpha falling as the kit's; the dust's ring and puffs
  (`dustAt()`, `puffAt()`); the sparks (`sparkAt()`, through `tickAt()`),
  in the tones the kit's `energyOf()` gives the flame's color; and the
  glints (`glintAt()`).
- **A held hit's spark,** the kit's `impactFx()` as `heldHitFx()` plays it,
  its time stretched over the hit's 200 ms (`HELD_MS`): five ticks, seven
  off iron, of one of eight shapes made once from seeds 1 to 8, which the
  event's serial picks (`impactOf()`, `makeImpact()`), fanned back about
  the way the shot came (`impactTickAt()`), and the four-point spark in
  the bright middle (`impactFlashAt()`); thrown from the rock's rim
  toward that way (`rimToward()`), which is toward the ship, the short
  way across the wrap, or toward the field's light while none flies, as a
  held hit's flash stands (`towardShip()`).
- **`rock_art.ts`** exports `FIRST_SEED`, which the breaks are seeded by,
  and `bySize()`, which makes them for each kind and size.
- **The specs** (`breaks.spec.ts`, 92) hold each kind's largest break to the
  kit's numbers, copied in to four places: its shards and heroes, the sums
  of their headings (the way out and the push together, as the kit gives
  them), reaches, spins, lives and facings, its sparks', puffs' and glints' counts
  and sums; that a stone's shards are its own facets where they lay, the
  dents left out, ice's slivers, a facet of more than three corners flying
  whole, iron's few plates that barely turn, more heroes, sparks and puffs
  the larger the rock, a crystal's gems all flying the whole time, a
  volatile rock's no dust; `breakOf()` for every kind, size and outline,
  the same every time and from its outline's seed; that nothing of any
  break shows at `BREAK_MS`, under half a second, and a held hit's spark
  at `HELD_MS`; and each piece's place, turn, size, color and alpha over
  its time, in a dark palette and a light one.
- **`AGENTS.md`** says what `breaks.ts` holds, and that a break's seed is
  mixed by hand, as a rock's cut and cracks are; the content skill that a
  new kind says how it comes apart in `BREAK_STYLES`.

### Choices the plan left open

- **A break is made once for each outline, from the rock's own seed.** The
  kit's sheet breaks each rock with the seed it is cut from; the game's
  rocks are six outlines of each kind and size, each cut from
  `FIRST_SEED + outline`, so each outline's break is made once from the
  same seed and the same rock breaks the same every time. The rock's turn
  and the blow only turn and push it as it is drawn: the kit's heading is
  the way out plus the push plus a sway, so the port keeps the way out and
  the sway, and adds the push, `shardPushOf()`, as it draws.
- **The kit's smaller rocks are the rules'.** The kit draws the pieces a
  rock leaves coming out of its middle; in the game they are rocks of the
  rules, laid as it breaks and drawn by `RockPainter`. Their draws are
  taken all the same, in the kit's order, so the sparks, puffs and glints
  after them are the kit's; the pieces' own rocks, their headings and the
  crystal's pickup coming out are left out.
- **Halos are L4's.** A gem shard's glow and a held hit's glow are the
  kit's soft halos, which C13 gives L4's soft disc and halo painter; the
  break draws neither, and L4 or F1 may add them over it.
- **A held hit's way is toward the ship.** The kit's `toward` is the way to
  where the shot came from; the event of a held hit names none, and
  `RockPainter`'s flash already stands toward the ship, or the field's
  light while none flies, so the spark is thrown from the same edge.
- **A held hit's sparks come in eight shapes,** made once from seeds 1 to 8
  and picked by the event's serial, rather than made at each hit, so a hit
  makes nothing.
- **The kit's pixels are CSS pixels.** Its least line, a pixel, and its
  glint's padding, 0.6 of one, are drawn at the pixel ratio.
- **The tones of something that gives off light** are the kit's
  `energyOf()`, in `breaks.ts`, its light palette's middle mixed toward the
  field's text, which the palette holds as `shot`. L4, whose
  `src/view/art/energy.ts` the plan names, may move it there.
- **A broken rock's first 30 ms is one flat fill.** The kit draws the lit
  rock, then its outline in the flash's tone over it, at the flash's
  strength; `wholeAt()` writes the outline in the rock's ink mixed that far
  toward the flash's tone, so the scene draws one shape, not the rock's lit
  frame beneath it. At 0.86 of the flash over two frames, the 14% of the
  rock left under it is the only difference.

### Review

A fresh `general-purpose` agent reviewed the staged change. Its findings,
and the answers:

1. **The per-frame functions made objects each call, though the file said a
   frame made nothing.** Fixed, in this file: `shardAt()`, `glintAt()` and
   `shardPushOf()` turn their points by the turn's cosine and sine where they
   write them, `puffAt()` writes its place without `polar()`, `tickAt()`
   writes both streaks through a function of the module's
   (`streakAlong()`) rather than a closure, and takes where sparks start as
   a `TickStart` made once (a `Break`'s `sparkStart`, a held hit's
   `IMPACT_START`) and the turn apart, so `sparkAt()` and `impactTickAt()`
   make none. Declined for `mixTone()`, `inLight()` and `litBy()`: they are
   the tones' and L1's, each a small closure or point the engine inlines,
   and §9's frame budget, measured at commit 2 with a crowded wave
   breaking, says whether they cost anything. The file's comment and this
   log no longer say a frame makes nothing, only that the records are kept.
   Declined too, working a shard's heading out once per break: it would
   take a list per break, which the pool would have to keep, for one
   `atan2()` a shard a frame.
2. **`BREAK_MS` and `HELD_MS` belong in `timing.ts`, beside `SHARDS_MS`,
   `DUST_MS` and `SPARK_MS`.** Declined for now: `timing.ts`, and
   `resultDelayMs()` that reads those three, are F1's, which this package
   keeps out of. Commit 2's painter reads `BREAK_MS` and `HELD_MS`; F1 may
   move them into `timing.ts` and retire the three when it times the
   flourishes, which the report to the integrator says.
3. **`BySize` and its nested `recordOf()` were copied from `rock_art.ts`.**
   Fixed: `rock_art.ts` exports `bySize()` and `BySize`, and `breaks.ts`
   makes its breaks with them. `breakOf()` keeps its own lookup and throw,
   which name the outline, as `rockShape()`'s do for its own.
4. **Specs that would pass with the code broken.** Fixed: the heroes spec
   asks for exactly the heroes, not that whatever is left is one; each
   kind's facings are summed beside the kit's (iron's plates among them),
   copied from the kit as the other sums are; and the spec of a rock with
   no facets, which only pinned `around()`'s error, is gone.
5. **`glintAt()` wrote white rather than the palette's `glint`.** Fixed: it
   writes `palette.glint`, which is white, and is the role a rock's glint
   is drawn in.
6. **Two loose ends in the docs.** The whole rock's flash, one flat fill
   where the kit lays its flash over the lit rock, is now a choice above.
   `AGENTS.md`'s list of seeds mixed by hand names a held hit's spark too.
