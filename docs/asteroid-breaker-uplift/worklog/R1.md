# Work log: R1, the crew in tow

R1 builds `implementation-plan.md`'s C17 to C19 in Asteroid Breaker's rules:
a crew of three, its pilot at the controls and the rest in tow behind the
pod as a chain of links, a hit with someone in tow knocking the last loose
rather than taking the ship, a loose fuzzball that can be caught again for
six seconds before it floats home, a stray caught joining the line or, with
five in tow, going home for 1,000, and a hit with no one in tow ending the
run. The pod is never replaced, so ships by points and the Extra ship go.
The saved game moves to version 2. `describe.ts` says what happens, the
autopilot catches a loose fuzzball as it catches a stray, and
`report:waves` counts what is knocked loose and what floats home. How to
play and the bar change only as far as the compiler needs; P3 draws the
crew. The newest entry is last.

- **Branch:** `abu/r1`, in `.claude/worktrees/abu-r1`, cut from
  `asteroid-breaker-uplift` at `da697c6e`.
- **Owned:** §7.3's R files: `src/rules/`, the autopilot and the report's
  helpers in `src/testing/`, `tools/report-waves.mjs` and
  `src/view/debug_tuning.ts`. Elsewhere, only what renaming and reshaping
  `Crew` needs.

## 3. The line in tow follows the pod

- **Did:** C18's line, in `src/rules/ship/tow.ts`: a link made once for
  each of the most that ride in tow (`MOST_CREW - 1`), the first `crew.tow`
  of them riding, in its order. A line is laid straight out behind a ship
  as it comes, in a wave's field, a run taken up and the next ship today
  (`layTow()`), one who joins its end a link behind the last
  (`trailLink()`), and each step after the ship flies every link is pulled
  straight toward the one ahead to a link's length, the first toward the
  hook at the ship's tail, the short way across the wrap (`stepTow()`).
  A pause's stretch moves the links with the rest. Nothing hits a link.
  The numbers: the hook 0.9 behind the pod's middle and a link of 1.6
  (`TOW_HOOK`, `TOW_LINK`), so the first bubble rides 2.5 behind the
  middle, clear of the pod's rings, as the mocks' tow line lays it.
- **Outside R's files:** `src/view/field/frame.ts`, shared and add-only:
  `FieldView` gains `tow`, for P3's painter.
- **Checks,** in order: the game's `y lint` failed once on an arrow in the
  new spec returning a void call, then passed; `y typecheck`, passed;
  `y vitest run --maxWorkers=4`, 96 files and 5,173 specs, passed; the
  root's lint under the lock, passed.
- **Review:** a fresh reviewer found these:
  1. A cleared wave's links were left a step behind where they were, so
     they would be drawn moving while the rest stood still, and so would a
     line with no ship. Fixed: `settleTow()`, from `clear()` and from
     `stepTow()` with no ship, each with a spec.
  2. The wrap's spec never crossed the wrap: plain subtraction in place of
     `across()` would have passed. Fixed: two specs whose lines straddle the
     bottom edge and the left one.
  3. One who joined a line the ship had turned on, without moving, was laid
     the way the tail points, which could put it on the ship or on the link
     ahead. Fixed: `trailLink()` lays it on along the line's own way, the
     tail's only where the line runs no way, with a spec of a turned ship.
  4. A stretch scaled the links like any place, so a turned phone left a
     long line stretched by its shape until the first step snapped it back.
     Fixed: the links move with the ship, as far from it the short way as
     they were, and keep their lengths, with specs across the wrap.
  5. §5.1 says `Field.tow` holds the pilots and their points; the links
     hold places alone. Kept, and said below for P3.
  6. Stale docs (`StretchField`'s, `stepField()`'s, `fieldFrom()`'s) and
     "pod" in the new code beside "ship" everywhere else in the rules.
     Fixed: the rules say ship.
  7. Spec nits: `.every(...).toBe(true)` and a check of `x` alone, both now
     whole comparisons. Rounding places by hand is kept: the specs compare
     whole lists of places, which `closeTo` would spell out one by one.
- **Checks after the fixes,** in order: the game's lint failed once on an
  `expect.closeTo` mapped over a list, typed `any`, so the spec compares
  rounded offsets instead; then its lint, typecheck and specs (5,180)
  passed, and the root's lint.
- **Choices:** the links hold only places; who rides in each is
  `crew.tow`'s, so the two can't disagree. **P3 pairs `field.tow[i]` with
  `field.crew.tow[i]`**, rather than reading pilots off `Field.tow` as §5.1
  words it. A link is pulled to exactly its length, so the line neither
  stretches nor bunches, and one lying on the one ahead holds, with no way
  to be pulled along. The line is laid behind the ship rather than drawn in
  from where a stray was caught: the join's flight is the view's to draw.
