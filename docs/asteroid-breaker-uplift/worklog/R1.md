# Work log: R1, the crew in tow

R1 builds `implementation-plan.md`'s C17 to C19 in Asteroid Breaker's rules:
a crew of three, its pilot at the controls and the rest in tow behind the
pod as a chain of links, a hit with someone in tow knocking the last loose
rather than taking the ship, a loose fuzzball that can be caught again for
six seconds before it floats home, a stray caught joining the line or, with
five in tow, going home for 1,000, and a hit with no one in tow ending the
run. The pod is never replaced, so ships by points and the Extra ship go.
The saved game moves to version 2. `describe.ts` says what happens, the
autopilot catches a loose fuzzball as it catches a stray, and
`report:waves` counts what is knocked loose and what floats home. How to
play and the bar change only as far as the compiler needs; P3 draws the
crew. The newest entry is last.

- **Branch:** `abu/r1`, in `.claude/worktrees/abu-r1`, cut from
  `asteroid-breaker-uplift` at `da697c6e`.
- **Owned:** §7.3's R files: `src/rules/`, the autopilot and the report's
  helpers in `src/testing/`, `tools/report-waves.mjs` and
  `src/view/debug_tuning.ts`. Elsewhere, only what renaming and reshaping
  `Crew` needs.

## 2. The crew is a pilot at the controls and a line in tow

- **Did:** reshaped `Crew` into C17's `{ pilot, tow, home, lastGiven }`,
  each behaviour as it was: the first in tow takes the controls as a ship
  is lost (`bailOut()`), a stray brought home rides last in tow, or waits
  at home with the most aboard (`bringHome()`), and the run is over once
  no one is at the controls. `aboard()` counts the pilot and those in tow,
  where `flying.length` was read. `FIRST_SHIPS` and `MAX_SHIPS` are renamed
  `FIRST_CREW` and `MOST_CREW`. The saved game keeps `pilot` and `tow` in
  place of `flying`, its version already 2, and holds a run kept to a pilot
  at the controls of a color and no more in tow than the most aboard
  leaves.
- **Outside R's files:** `GameService.ships()` counts `aboard()`, the bar
  draws the pilot and those in tow and the pilot at the controls from
  `crew.pilot`, the ship painter seats `crew.pilot`, and the docs of the
  board card's `home` and `GameService.crew()`; How to play takes the new
  name; the shell's, the scene's and the view's specs build crews in the
  new shape.
- **Checks,** in order: the game's `y lint`, passed; `y typecheck`,
  passed; `y vitest run --maxWorkers=4`, 95 files and 5,150 specs, passed;
  the root's lint under the lock, passed.
- **Review:** a fresh reviewer found the refactor correct, every reader of
  `flying` mapped, and these:
  1. Docs still saying who flies the run's ships, on `Run.crew`,
     `Field.crew` and the view's `FieldFrame.crew`, and the run spec's
     `NONE_FLYING`. Fixed: they say who is at the controls, in tow and at
     home, and the constant is `NO_PILOT`. `frame.ts` is shared, add-only,
     and only that doc line changed.
  2. `bringHome()` on a crew with no one at the controls made one in tow
     behind no pilot, a state no step reaches today. Fixed: it throws, as
     `atControls()` does, with a spec, and `Crew.pilot` says no one is in
     tow without a pilot.
  3. The saved game's `pilot` was typed nullable though its reader asks for
     one. Fixed: it is `Pilot`, written with `atControls()`, which
     `saveGame()` never reaches for a run that is over.
  4. The service's spec of a stray brought home no longer checked the
     pilot. Fixed: it checks `pilot` and `tow`.
  5. Nits: `hasSoundCrew()` reads `1 + tow.length <= MOST_CREW` as its doc
     says, and the scene's spec seats `atControls()`, both taken. The bar's
     `pilots` making a new array as `lastGiven` changes is declined: the bar
     is P1's, and the cost is one row's input.
- **Checks after the fixes,** in order: the game's lint, typecheck and
  specs (5,151) passed, then the root's lint.
- **Choices:** a refactor of its own, before the rule changes, so the
  rule's commit reads as the rule. `home` still means the strays waiting
  past the most aboard here; C17's meaning, every fuzzball gone home, comes
  with the rule.
