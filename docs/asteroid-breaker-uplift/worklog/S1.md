# Work log: S1, the synth and the sounds

Package S1 of the build `implementation-plan.md` lays out: the sound kit's
synth, music, measures and two instruments lifted into `@fz/dev/synth`, in
TypeScript with specs; `@fz/dev/sounds`' sprite writer taught a sample rate,
takes, a gap and a fade; the nine families of the kit's recipes moved into
the game's `tools/sounds/` on that library, rendered, packed and encoded by
`tools/build-sounds.mjs`; and the kit's encodes and their map copied into
`src/scene/sounds/` beside today's sprite, which the game goes on playing
until S3 (C2). It is done when every take renders byte for byte as the
kit's did, which this log records, and the build's spec holds the shipped
map. Branch `abu/s1`, in `.claude/worktrees/abu-s1`. The newest entry is
last.

No agent can hear, so nothing here was judged by ear: what is said of a
sound below was read by measure, or by comparing bytes with what the user
heard.

## Commit 1: the synth and the sprite writer's layout

`feat(dev): @fz/dev/synth, the sound kit's synth, measures and two instruments ported to render its every take byte for byte, and a sprite writer that packs takes at a layout of its own`

### What was done

- **`@fz/dev/synth`** (`frontend/lib/dev/src/synth.ts`, exported in
  `package.json`), the entry of five modules ported from the kit's
  `sound/lib/`: `synth_signal.ts` (`synth.mjs`), `synth_music.ts`
  (`music.mjs`), `synth_measure.ts` (`analyze.mjs`'s loudness and bands, not
  its spectrogram, which needs sharp), `synth_impacts.ts`
  (`parts-impacts.mjs`) and `synth_voice.ts` (`parts-voice.mjs`). The port
  keeps the kit's arithmetic in its order, each random draw in its order,
  and every default, so a take renders the same doubles; the specs and the
  byte comparison below hold it to that.
- **Added in the port:** `loudness()`, the peak and loudest 50 ms that the
  build levels by, the same numbers as `measure()` reads at a fraction of
  its time (measure's spectrum and weighting took most of the build);
  `amplitudeAt()`, a pitch's amplitude through a Hann-windowed DFT, which
  the specs read harmonics and aliases with; and `wavSamples()`, which reads
  a WAV back as the kit's `encode.mjs` did, so the build packs exactly what
  it wrote.
- **The name clashes between the kit's files**, settled once in the
  library: `unit()` lives in `synth_signal.ts` (the kit had one in
  `parts-impacts` and another in `parts-ship`), and the ship's own `sing`
  and `crackle` are renamed in the game's `ship_parts.mjs` (commit 2).
  Types that shadowed TypeScript's or the DOM's are renamed: the kit's
  `Partial` (a struck partial) is `Mode`, its `Shape` (a waveform)
  `Waveform`, and music's `C` (the octaves' Cs) `OCTAVE_C`.
- **The sprite writer's layout** (`src/sounds.ts`): `packSoundSprite()`
  packs takes rendered elsewhere into one sprite's samples and map, at a
  `SpriteLayout`'s sample rate, gap, fade and decimal places, each
  defaulting to today's (16,000, 0.1 s, 10 ms, 3), a sound of several takes
  named `<name>-<n>` from 1 (`takeNames()`); `spritemapOf()` lays the map
  out from the takes' lengths alone, for the build's spec. The plan named
  `renderSoundSprite()` as the one to learn these; it renders a `Sound`'s
  wave, which a synthesized take has no use for, so it stays as it was and
  is now built on `packSoundSprite()`: `render()` scales to the peak and
  the packer fades, which gives the same doubles in the same order, so the
  three games' byte-exact specs pass unchanged.
- **The specs** hold what the kit's self-test held and each instrument's
  basics: 72 for the signal, 11 for the measures, 9 for the key, 19 for the
  impacts and 13 for the voice, and 43 for `sounds.ts`, the packer's and
  the map's among them. The entry's spec, `synth.spec.ts`, holds the port
  to the kit itself: 36 calls, every instrument and `wav()`, each to the
  first 16 hex digits of the SHA-256 of what the kit's own `.mjs` rendered
  for the same call, so a reordered draw or regrouped sum fails there.

### Choices the plan left open

- **No `@fz/core` dependency.** The synth keeps its own `seeded()`, the
  same mulberry32 as `@fz/core/random`'s, rather than depend on `@fz/core`,
  which S2 is changing in parallel; a later package may fold them together.
- **Reads that can't miss assert their sample.** Under
  `noUncheckedIndexedAccess` the kit's code falls back on every read
  (`x[i] ?? 0`), and 90 of those fallbacks were branches no spec reached:
  with them the library's branch figure fell from the rest's 96.6 % to
  90.0 %, under its 95 % floor. A helper reading past the ends as silence (`sampleAt()`) put
  the figure at 96.6 %, but slowed the build from 31.8 s to 49.6 s, as one
  shared read sees the reads that do fall past an end and boxes every
  sample; a bench of the heavy instruments ran 1.5 to 3 times slower (pink
  noise 119 to 302 ms, the reverb 19 to 38 ms). So the synth's modules
  assert a read the loop bounds (`x[i]!`), which `eslint.config.js` lets
  them, with its reason, and keep the fallback only where a read may fall
  past a signal's end (a comb's, an echo's or a room's tail, the limiter's
  look ahead, a frame past the sound, a shorter channel of a WAV), each
  taken by a spec. Two of the kit's branches no input reached went, with
  the same results: `points()` held its first point's value through a test
  that could only find that point, and `measure()` found a frequency's band
  through a fallback past the last, which ends at half the sample rate.
- **Empty inputs keep the kit's answers,** each now specced: a parameter of
  no samples reads 0, `points()` through no points and `along()` along no
  path are silent, and `resample()` of nothing is one silent sample.

### Figures, by measure

- **The kit's self-test, ported:** a 1,000 Hz saw's aliases at 100, 900 and
  1,100 Hz lie at -142, -105 and -100 dB against its fundamental's -3.9 dB
  (a naive saw's lie at about -37 dB; the spec holds them 90 dB under); pink
  noise falls 2.8 to 3.2 dB an octave between 200 and 3,200 Hz (the kit
  logged 3.05); a low-pass at 500 Hz falls 12.4 dB from 2 to 4 kHz and a
  high-pass 12.0 dB an octave below its cutoff (the kit logged 12.5).
- **Byte for byte:** the kit's build, run from the repository's root
  (`node docs/asteroid-breaker-uplift/sound/build.mjs`, 74.6 s), and the
  game's (`node tools/build-sounds.mjs`, 31.0 s after the last edit) each render
  178 takes; a Node script compared each pair of WAVs: all 178 identical,
  29,426,490 bytes. The game's map (`tools/sounds/wav/sprite.json`) is the
  shipped `assets/sound/sprite/sounds.json` byte for byte, and its sprite
  WAV, 172 takes over 103.86 s, the 9,160,926 bytes `encode.mjs`'s packing
  hands ffmpeg, by a script that packs as it does. The kit's build rewrote
  its nine tracked `sound/sheets/*.png`, restored by name with
  `git restore`; its reports and `assets/sound/sounds.json` came out as
  committed.
- **Today's sprites:** Snake's, Brick Breaker's and Brick Fuzz Pop's builds,
  and Asteroid Breaker's as committed, rebuilt with the new `sounds.ts`,
  leave git clean.
- **Coverage of `@fz/dev`:** 98.9 % of statements, 97.15 % of branches,
  99.15 % of functions, 98.98 % of lines, every synth module at 100 %.

### The review

A fresh agent read the staged change against the kit's files and found no
line that renders a take differently. Its findings and their answers:

1. **No spec held the port to the kit's numbers,** only to properties a
   reordered draw would keep. Fixed: `synth.spec.ts`, above, its digests
   rendered by the kit's own modules and matched by the port's before they
   were written down.
2. **The `places` spec passed with the option ignored** (1,234 samples at
   44.1 kHz round alike at 3 and 4 places). Fixed: 1,250 samples, 0.0283 s.
3. **`AGENTS.md` said what isn't so at this commit:** Asteroid Breaker's
   build packing with `packSoundSprite()` (commit 2's), the triangle
   band-limited (only the saw and the pulse are) and `wav()` taking more
   than two channels. Fixed, each; the game's use goes in with commit 2.
4. **`renderSoundSprite()` renders before it refuses two sounds of one
   name,** which the old code checked first. Kept: the refusal and its
   message are unchanged, the specs hold them, and a list with both a
   duplicate and a silent sound now names the silent one; a second check
   of the names ahead of `layOut()`'s would say the same thing twice.
5. **`degree()` and `centsOf()` asserted a read a fractional step misses**
   (NaN, where the kit's fallback quietly played a C). Fixed: both read
   through one `semitonesOf()`, which throws a `RangeError` for a step that
   isn't whole, specced; no recipe passes one, as the byte comparison
   shows.
6. **Nits,** each taken: `loudness()` returns
   `Pick<Measured, "peak" | "rms50">`; the scatter spec collects the shares
   and asserts after the act; `pitch[1102]`; the ladder spec pins the
   literal ladder, steps and cents, rather than recomputing it; the voice
   spec names `SR`.

After the fixes the game's build ran again (31.6 s) and every take, the map
and the sprite WAV compared identical with the kit's, as above.
