# Brick shooters, read for bounce geometry and board design

Web research for Rickashay, 2026-11-10. It adds to "What other games teach" in [`../../archive/brick-fuzz-pop/plan.md`](../../archive/brick-fuzz-pop/plan.md) and does not repeat it.

How to read these notes:

- Every finding names its game and the page it came from. Pages were read through a fetch tool that summarises, so wording is paraphrased throughout; there is one direct quote in the whole file (Holedown's developer, under question 1).
- "(inferred)" marks my own deduction. "(search snippet only)" marks a claim seen in a search result's summary whose page would not open (403, 404 or 405), so it is weaker.
- No source code was opened: see the last section.

## 1. Shapes that change the bounce

- **Holedown, rounded corners, the developer's reason.** Martin Jonasson found in early prototypes that the best shots were the ones where balls landed in a small pocket and bounced wildly, and that such set-ups were rare. Rounding the blocks' corners made them common: the corners, he says, "open up the playfield much more", and they suit the larger tetromino-style blocks. <https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/the-ins-and-outs-of-bouncing-balls-in-the-joyful-brick-breaker-i-holedown-i->
- **Holedown, rounded corners as the breakthrough.** He calls the corners the key breakthrough of the project; a friend helped him see it could work, and it cost real time to build. Level generation took long to get right, and about a hundred physics glitches each needed a special fix. <https://www.pocketgamer.biz/indie-spotlight-grapefrukts-holedown/>
- **Holedown, what a critic says the corners do.** Electron Dance argues sharp corners (Breakout, Peggle) add unpredictability, while smoothed corners turn the corner into the place where a player controls where balls go; experienced players work the corners to set up long attacks. The same piece finds the round core's rebounds random and confusing. <https://www.electrondance.com/?p=13232>
- **Holedown, a longer answer exists but was not heard.** A 2020 podcast episode's description says Jonasson explains the reasons behind his rounded corners; only the description is on the page, no transcript. <https://thespelunkyshowlike.libsyn.com/42-the-secrets-of-simplicity-with-martin-jonasson>
- **Brick Breaker Journeys (MobilityWare, level-based), the clearest shape list found.** Normal bricks are squares, double-width rectangles and right triangles in one of four orientations. Round bricks are a _special_ brick, and the official help says outright that being circular makes the ricochet angle harder to get right. Rotating rectangular bricks spin clockwise about their centre, deflect by whatever angle they are at, do not come down with the rows, and may be indestructible. <https://mobilityware.helpshift.com/hc/en/68-brick-breaker-journeys/faq/4180-what-are-the-different-types-of-bricks/>
- **Ballz has triangle blocks and never explains them.** A 2018 App Store reviewer asks for the triangle blocks to be explained in the how-to-play. <https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ballz/id1139609950>
- **Ballz Shooter (Ketchapp), players welcome angled blocks.** One 2017 reviewer says angled blocks are a good addition because they let a shot be angled precisely; another says triangular blocks add variety. <https://apps.apple.com/app/ballz-shooter/id1266679534>
- **BBTAN added shape-like blocks late.** Version 10.0.1 (December 2023) lists five new blocks, among them ones named Angle, Rotation and Moving; the notes do not say what they do. <https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bbtan-break-brick/id1060433597>
- **Okay? (one ball, see question 5) uses rectangles, circles and triangles.** Reviewers say the shapes change the ball's course and a slightly different angle decides a level; the cost is trial and error by tiny adjustments, the main complaint. <https://apps.apple.com/us/app/okay/id962050549> and <https://www.taptap.io/post/6009669>
- **Bricks Breaker Puzzle (Mobirix), corner hits read as bugs.** A reviewer calls the angles weird: the same hit seems to go different ways, and a hit on a box's side can come off vertically. With square corners and a small ball, edge cases look like glitches (inferred). <https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bricks-breaker-puzzle/id1245519115>
- **Bricks n Balls, shapes as pictures.** The store text says bricks come in themed shapes such as fish and hamburgers; these read as pictures built of blocks rather than new bounce surfaces (inferred). <https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bricks-n-balls/id1331794412>
- **Bounzy (Zynga), one-sided reflectors.** Shielded monsters carry a shield on one of their four sides that bounces shots off, so the side a shot arrives from matters. <https://www.touchtapplay.com/bounzy-cheats-tips-strategy-guide/>
- **Brickies (paddle game), praised for shapes.** TouchArcade's review singles out non-rectangular bricks, bricks that can only be hit from one side, and round levels as what sets it apart. <https://toucharcade.com/?p=172974>
- **Not found:** how triangle bricks are oriented and placed in hand-made levels, beyond their coming in four orientations; any write-up of BBTAN's or Bricks n Balls' triangles; any player discussion of diamonds. A RAWG user comment that a polygon brick makes the ball very hard to predict (Break Brick Out) is search snippet only.

## 2. Getting behind the wall and staying there

- **Ballz, the standard advice.** Get balls stuck at the top of the screen where they keep bouncing; angle a shot through a gap so it lodges under a cluster higher up; prefer diagonals; think of bounces as a combo. The same guide warns to clear low blocks first rather than chase combos high up. <https://www.gamezebo.com/walkthroughs/ballz-tips-cheats-and-strategies/>
- **Ballz, angle is height.** The angle decides how high the ball climbs and so how long it stays up; aim for the gaps between bricks, because a ball inside keeps bouncing; the common mistake is firing every shot the same way. <https://www.levelwinner.com/?p=9150>
- **Bricks n Balls, find or make an opening.** Squeeze balls through an opening so they keep bouncing and reach far blocks; if there is none, hit the block needing the fewest hits to make one; the second-best shot is off a side wall at a very small angle. <https://www.touchtapplay.com/bricks-n-balls-cheats-tips-strategy-guide-to-beat-all-stages/>
- **Bricks n Balls, three hand-made levels solved the same way.** Level 429: a gap on the left leads to a hollow space above the highest blocks; two or three shots there thin the structure from the top. Level 436: a small tunnel on the right, a hollow above, and wall shots that travel in a snake-like path. Level 440: first destroy the two right-hand blocks that have nothing behind them, to open the hollow above. <https://www.touchtapplay.com/how-to-beat-bricks-n-balls-level-429/>, <https://www.touchtapplay.com/how-to-beat-bricks-n-balls-level-436/>, <https://www.touchtapplay.com/how-to-beat-bricks-n-balls-level-440/>
- **Holedown is built on the trapped ball.** Electron Dance says its whole design takes the best moment of Breakout, the ball trapped behind the blocks, and makes survival depend on it. <https://www.electrondance.com/?p=13232>
- **Holedown, sneak under.** A PC review advises slipping a ball under other bricks to trap it, aiming into tight nooks, and learning where to break into a tower; fixed blocks hold up what rests on them, so they are the priority. <https://higherplaingames.com/pc/holedown-review-pc/>
- **Bounzy, bank or waste the shot.** A straight shot hits one target and comes home, a one-way trip; angle for the largest bank; look for small gaps inside monster formations, where bolts rattle. <https://www.touchtapplay.com/bounzy-cheats-tips-strategy-guide/> and <https://www.levelwinner.com/bounzy-tips-cheats-tricks-hints/>
- **Ball x Pit (2025), behind is worth double.** Guides say ricochet angles matter more than precise aim and tell players to angle throws so balls end up behind or through the enemy line; one passive doubles damage from behind. <https://commonsensegamer.com/ball-x-pit-mega-guide-tips-tricks/> and <https://gameluster.com/breaking-the-brick-ceiling/>
- **Ball x Pit, a sharper rule (search snippet only).** Aim so that the first wall the ball touches is behind the enemy line; corners and close crevices give more hits. <https://deltiasgaming.com/?p=381616>
- **"Bouncing Balls" (a browser Ballz-like), two named shots.** Never straight up; a shallow opening shot that carves a path upward between the side walls; and a ball caught in a vertical column gives dozens of hits on the same bricks. <https://freegmzn.blogspot.com/2025/07/bouncing-balls.html>
- **Balls Bricks Breaker, the lane.** Clearing a whole column opens a lane for direct shots deep into the wall; side-wall angles send balls back through for a second pass. <https://www.brickbreaker.net/game/balls-bricks-breaker/>
- **Bricks and Balls Crusher, same advice in a 130-level game.** Most damage comes from balls sent through gaps or off the side walls to the top rows. <https://www.bubbleshooter.net/game/bricks-and-balls-crusher/>
- **KBreakout (paddle game), the size of the hole.** Its handbook says to get the ball above the bricks, and that the smaller the hole, the less likely the ball is to fall back through. The same holds for a one-ball shooter (inferred). <https://docs.kde.org/trunk_kf6/en/kbreakout/kbreakout/strategies-and-tips.html>
- **What makes boards frustrating.** One More Brick: in later levels blocks plug the gaps and balls cannot reach the bricks, so it buries the player whatever their skill. <https://apps.apple.com/us/app/one-more-brick/id1084813529> Bricks Breaker Puzzle: levels of almost solid blocks worth hundreds of hits. <https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bricks-breaker-puzzle/id1245519115> Bricks n Balls: too many blocks per row in the 450s, and arrow blocks whose directions seem random. <https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1331794412/bricks-n-balls/reviews>
- **The gap moves.** Ballz players are warned that a gap seen this turn may be gone the next, since rows are random (search snippet only, GameSkinny; page returned 403).
- **Numbers and rules of thumb: none found.** No guide gives an angle in degrees. The nearest: Holedown's endless mode past 300 m is said to need aim accurate to a single degree. <https://a.wholelottanothing.org/holedown-is-the-best-game-of-2018/>

## 3. Items that lengthen a flight

- **One More Brick, the floor bounce.** A green button gives a ball that touches it one extra bounce off the floor. <https://www.levelwinner.com/one-more-brick-cheats-tips-hints-tricks-to-get-a-high-score/> Hardcore Gamer describes its icon as a ball with a person on it: the ball changes colour when it takes the pickup, and after landing it goes back up into the bricks until it drops for good. Pickups vanish once triggered, so it lasts one round. <https://hardcoregamer.com/features/casual-corner/casual-corner-one-more-brick/261794/>
- **One More Brick, the names.** The store text names a _Double Bouncer_ power-up, and dearer balls carry an extra-bounce skill. A developer reply quoting a ball skill of two floor bounces per bounce power-up is search snippet only. <https://apps.apple.com/us/app/one-more-brick/id1084813529>
- **Brick Breaker Journeys, _Blockers_.** A power-up the player triggers by hand: indestructible blocks cover roughly 40 to 45 per cent of the floor, starting from the wall opposite the shooter, and bounce balls back up instead of letting them leave. How long they stay is not stated. <https://mobilityware.helpshift.com/hc/en/68-brick-breaker-journeys/faq/4183-what-are-manual-power-ups/>
- **Ball x Pit, a character whose balls use the floor.** The Flagellant's balls bounce off the bottom of the screen instead of leaving, which a guide says about doubles their active time. The Repentant gains 5 per cent damage per bounce; the Shieldbearer's shield doubles damage on each bounce off it. <https://commonsensegamer.com/ball-x-pit-mega-guide-tips-tricks/>
- **Kill the Brickman (2025), bounce count as a stat.** Each bullet has a number of bounces it may make off bricks and walls before it vanishes; clips and relics bought between levels raise it. <https://www.thexboxhub.com/kill-the-brickman-review/>
- **Okay?, wrap-around.** Levels 51 to 100 let the ball leave one screen edge and come back on the other; wormholes swallow a ball that touches them. <https://www.taptap.io/post/6009669>
- **Break Bricks: Ball's Quest (paddle game), the block that gives the ball back.** Gray blocks cannot be cleared but return the ball; its guide calls setting up shots that live off them the best strategy. <https://www.gamezebo.com/walkthroughs/break-bricks-balls-quest-tips-cheats-and-strategies/>
- **Brick Breaker Journeys, props are lasers only.** Row, column and cross lasers fire each time a ball passes, stay until used, can fire several times in one shot, and do not outlast it. Nothing there redirects the ball. <https://mobilityware.helpshift.com/hc/en/68-brick-breaker-journeys/faq/4182-what-are-props/>
- **Balls Bricks Breaker, a bouncing item exists, undescribed.** The store text mentions catching split and bouncing items; no page read says what the bouncing one does. <https://apps.apple.com/us/app/balls-bricks-breaker/id1382613712>
- **Drawkanoid (paddle game), wall bounces as a charge.** Its pierce upgrade charges by bouncing off walls 25 times. <https://selectbutton.com/reviews/drawkanoid-review>
- **Not found:** side bumpers, springs or portals in any aim-and-release brick shooter; what BBTAN's items do, and whether it has a bounce item (store pages, an aggregator and three searches gave nothing).

## 4. The stuck ball

- **One More Brick, a pickup that appears when nothing is being hit.** A 2018 reviewer reports that a bouncer used to appear once balls stopped hitting blocks, that it had stopped appearing, and that balls then bounced wall to wall in the same place for ever until recalled by hand. So the game detects a flight with no brick hits and drops a redirecting pickup into it, with a recall as the fallback (inferred from the review). <https://apps.apple.com/us/app/one-more-brick/id1084813529>
- **Holedown, stuck balls were shipped bugs.** Version 1.0.1 fixed balls stuck on the core for ever; 1.0.7 fixed a soft lock with balls stuck in the upper right. <https://apps.apple.com/us/app/holedown/id1297270249> A critic notes balls sometimes leave a rebound pattern for no clear reason, which reads like a quiet nudge (inferred). <https://www.electrondance.com/?p=13232>
- **Peggle, remove what the ball is stuck on.** Pegs a ball lodges against are removed while the ball is still in play. <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peggle>
- **Bricks n Balls, shake the board.** The store text says an earthquake shakes the blocks loose when the player is stuck. <https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bricks-n-balls/id1331794412>
- **Bricks Breaker Quest, forbid the flat shot, and players mind.** A reviewer says an update took away the shallow shots along the bottom, so the block just over the shoulder can no longer be reached. A minimum launch angle prevents level flights at the cost of a shot players want (inferred). <https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1288430163/bricks-breaker-quest/reviews>
- **BBTAN, return and fast-forward liked, forced speed disliked.** After the 2023 rewrite a long-time player still praised automatic ball return and fast-forward, while two others said faster balls and a speed-up animation ruined the long, relaxing, near-endless bounce they played for. <https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bbtan-break-brick/id1060433597>
- **Ballz, players ask for an end.** Reviews ask for a button to end the round when balls are hitting nothing, and call waiting on one last ball hitting one block tedious. <https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ballz/id1139609950>
- **The general case.** Ron Dippold's essay on Breakout's flaw: with few bricks left a player watches for some twenty seconds with nothing to do; power-ups only soften it; the cure is some influence over the ball during the flight (Shatter's push and pull, a resource that refills). He names Wizorb's mazes of unbreakable bricks as the worst case. <https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/shatter-solved-the-breakout-problem-please-don-t-keep-making-the-same-mistake->
- **Bricks Breaker RPG, the opposite complaint.** A reviewer says a bad shot wastes everything and a good shot always takes the same path, and suggests unbreakable blocks that wear down and redirect, and slimy walls a ball slides along before leaving. The developer liked the slime wall. <https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bricks-breaker-rpg/id6749246731>
- **Not found:** how Ballz or BBTAN detect a ball bouncing level between the side walls, or any statement that they nudge its angle. Forum and itch.io comments about near-level loops were all from other games (search snippets only).

## 5. One ball versus many

- **No aim-and-release brick shooter read is built on one ball's long flight under a descending wall.** Every one of the genre's games fires a swarm. The nearest are below. (The gap is inferred from not finding one in several searches.)
- **Okay? (Philipp Stollenmayer, 2015), one ball, one shot.** Clear every white shape with a single launch; 150 levels in three sets (plain, wrap-around, bosses); black blocks are obstacles, some blocks must be passed through, some move. Reviewers mention hidden songs and a music-chords idea, so hits seem to sound notes (inferred). <https://www.taptap.io/post/6009669> and <https://apps.apple.com/us/app/okay/id962050549>
- **Kill the Brickman (2025), one shot a turn.** No paddle: pick ammunition, set the angle, see the projected ricochet path, fire; bricks then take their turn. Reviewers like chaining bounces between bricks and find it slow and its levels alike. <https://finalboss.io/kill-the-brickman-review-a-turn-based-roguelike> and <https://www.thexboxhub.com/kill-the-brickman-review/>
- **Ball x Pit, a turn-based character.** One late-game character turns the real-time game turn-based, which a reviewer calls a different test of skill. <https://gameluster.com/breaking-the-brick-ceiling/>
- **Brick Breaker Journeys, points climb within a shot.** Each broken brick shows its points in small text, and the points per brick rise linearly through a shot; a meter fills toward three stars. <https://mobilityware.helpshift.com/hc/en/68-brick-breaker-journeys/faq/4184-how-are-points-calculated/>
- **Brickies, an unbroken run pays.** Consecutive hits without touching the paddle earn more points. <https://toucharcade.com/?p=172974>
- **BBTAN, a combo system arrives eight years in.** Version 10.0.0 (December 2023) adds a combo system to its endless mode; the notes do not describe it. <https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bbtan-break-brick/id1060433597>
- **Holedown, a player counts hits unprompted.** A 2024 reviewer uses endless mode as a fidget toy and tracks brick touches per shot as a challenge of their own. That is demand for a counter the game does not show (inferred). <https://apps.apple.com/us/app/holedown/id1297270249>
- **Holedown, small sounds.** Balls make bouncing sounds, bricks pop, and there is a purring click while a shot is lined up. <https://ladiesgamers.com/holedown-review-nintendo-switch/> and <https://higherplaingames.com/pc/holedown-review-pc/>
- **Peggle, how an ending is celebrated.** Style points for hard shots; a multiplier that builds to ten times; the last orange peg brings a zoom on the ball, the _Extreme Fever_ banner and Ode to Joy, and the floor turns into scoring bins. <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peggle>
- **Ballz starts as a one-ball game.** One ball at round one; a writer notes one strong move can clear a screen of high blocks. <https://techcrunch.com/?p=1483446>
- **Swipe Brick Breaker is a swarm game too.** Monthly23 is a one-man developer; the game is an endless ball chain grown by green circles, with paid speed and power modes; one listing says it is no longer in operation. <https://m-apps.qoo-app.com/en_us/app/4353> and <https://apps.apple.com/app/id1039195468>

## 6. Aim guides

- **Bricks n Balls, a longer guide is sold, and missed when gone.** _Golden Aim_ shows up to three corners of the path; version 3.32.0 says it is back after player feedback. Another reviewer finds the dotted line hard to move. <https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1331794412/bricks-n-balls/reviews> and <https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bricks-n-balls/id1331794412>
- **BBTAN, the same name, and it lied.** Its 2023 _Golden Aim_ previews the ideal shot; 5.0.7 improved its accuracy and added an off switch; a 2024 reviewer still found shots not going where the preview said. An aggregator repeats that the mission mode's shot does not match its line. <https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bbtan-break-brick/id1060433597> and <https://gamebrain.co/game/bbtan-break-brick>
- **One More Brick, line and landing disagree.** A reviewer says the aim line does not match where balls go, corner hits included. <https://apps.apple.com/us/app/one-more-brick/id1084813529>
- **Bricks Breaker Quest, two ways to aim, both doubted.** It has a slider and freehand aim; reviewers call the guide inaccurate and aiming too imprecise for the shots levels need. <https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bricks-breaker-quest/id1288430163>
- **Bricks Breaker RPG, a fire button for twitchy screens.** A reviewer finds the guide finicky enough to push shots off target; the developer plans a separate shoot button because some phones are too sensitive. <https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bricks-breaker-rpg/id6749246731>
- **Bricks and Balls Crusher, two lines.** A main aiming line and a secondary line for the trajectory after it. <https://www.bubbleshooter.net/game/bricks-and-balls-crusher/>
- **Kill the Brickman, coarse then fine.** Put the cursor near the target, then fine-tune with the shoulder buttons; stick aiming is criticised and a mouse is much better. <https://www.thexboxhub.com/kill-the-brickman-review/>
- **Ablockalypse (web), keys do aim.** Mouse to aim and click to fire, or A/D or the arrow keys to aim and Space to fire; a click during the flight fast-forwards. This corrects the earlier plan's note that no web version read can be aimed by keys. <https://www.crazygames.com/game/ablockalypse>
- **Holedown, fire downward so the finger is not on the target.** The developer says shooting down avoids a finger hiding the aim; shooting up under reversed gravity was tried and was very confusing. <https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/the-ins-and-outs-of-bouncing-balls-in-the-joyful-brick-breaker-i-holedown-i->
- **One More Brick and Bounzy, where the launcher stands.** A One More Brick reviewer says the shooter's limited side-to-side movement can trap the player. <https://apps.apple.com/us/app/one-more-brick/id1084813529> In Bounzy the wizard moves after every volley to where its first shot crossed the wall, marked beforehand by a small white swirl, so the angle chosen also chooses the next position. <https://www.touchtapplay.com/bounzy-cheats-tips-strategy-guide/>
- **Okay?, hint as a range.** A reviewer wants the hint to show the range of angles that work rather than less. <https://www.taptap.io/post/6009669>
- **Peggle, the full path as a power.** One character's power shows the ball's path including rebounds. <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peggle>
- **Not found:** how many bounces the free guide shows in Ballz, Holedown, BBTAN or Bricks Breaker Quest. Reviews of Subpar Pool (Holedown's developer) arguing that bounce lines make play too predictable are search snippets only.

## 7. Level layout in the level-based games

- **Bricks n Balls, the side door and the attic.** The three levels in question 2 share one plan: a mass of bricks, one gap or tunnel at a side, and an empty space above where balls rattle and eat the wall from the top. Players are told to look for it first. (Sources as in question 2.)
- **Bricks n Balls, long levels in waves.** Levels 436 and 440 are described as very long, with bricks arriving in waves; 440 adds fortified bricks that turn to metal and cannot be hurt for a round, changing from round to round; 429 has an explosive block low on the right. <https://www.touchtapplay.com/how-to-beat-bricks-n-balls-level-440/>
- **Bricks n Balls, levels are retuned for years.** Release notes cut level 277's difficulty and corrected level 831 to eleven columns from ten, so that level at least is eleven wide. <https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bricks-n-balls/id1331794412>
- **Bricks n Balls, what players hate.** Arrow blocks whose directions look random make a win partly chance; armoured blocks arriving with 450 hits; too many blocks per row. <https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1331794412/bricks-n-balls/reviews>
- **Holedown, the generator's four knobs.** How full the board is, how big the blocks are, how many are stuck in the wall, and their hit points, tuned over months. <https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/the-ins-and-outs-of-bouncing-balls-in-the-joyful-brick-breaker-i-holedown-i-> Fixed blocks act as shelves: clear one and the stack on it falls. <https://www.electrondance.com/?p=13232> Its identical cores are the common complaint. <https://apps.apple.com/us/app/holedown/id1297270249>
- **Brick Breaker Journeys, ten stages a theme.** A Journey is ten stages sharing a theme that fixes the power-up bricks, the colours and the brick size; rotating bricks that never descend work as fixed deflectors. <https://mobilityware.helpshift.com/hc/en/68-brick-breaker-journeys/faq/4185-what-do-the-in-game-icons-mean/>
- **Bricks Breaker RPG, unbreakable bricks that can lose the game.** Unbreakable bricks count as enemies, so one reaching the floor ends the run; a player docked a star for it and asked for a switch; the developer refused, saying levels are puzzles built to be beaten. Another reviewer praises its clever stage layouts. <https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bricks-breaker-rpg/id6749246731>
- **Bricks Breaker Quest, spikes and rests.** Players name levels 189 and 654 as where they quit; one who stayed likes that a very hard level is followed by several easier ones. <https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1288430163/bricks-breaker-quest/reviews>
- **Bricks and Balls Crusher, tell before the bang.** A brick with flashing corners holds a bomb; players are told to find booster bricks first and choose the angle that reaches them. <https://www.bubbleshooter.net/game/bricks-and-balls-crusher/>
- **Wizorb, the maze of unbreakables.** Mazes of unbreakable bricks the ball must thread to reach the last targets are an essayist's example of numbing waits. <https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/shatter-solved-the-breakout-problem-please-don-t-keep-making-the-same-mistake->
- **Not found:** players naming channels, funnels or sealed chambers as such; any level-by-level account of One More Brick's levels mode.

## 8. Names

- **"Rickashay" is taken, by a jam game.** _Rickashay_ by Deex (crystal-studios) is on itch.io: a free, browser-playable Unity entry for the GMTK Game Jam 2020 (theme "Out of Control"), tagged a 2D top-down shooter. The page describes no mechanics and shows no ratings or comments. <https://crystal-studios.itch.io/rickashay> Four searches found no other game, app or store listing with that spelling; no brick game uses it.
- **Ricochet (1989, Superior Software):** an arcade adventure. <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ricochet_(disambiguation)>
- **Ricochet Xtreme (Reflexive, about 2001; year from a search snippet):** a paddle-and-ball Breakout game, first of its series.
- **Ricochet Lost Worlds (Reflexive, 2004) and Lost Worlds: Recharged (2004):** Breakout sequels with new bricks and power-ups and five rings to collect per level. <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ricochet_Lost_Worlds>
- **Ricochet Infinity (Reflexive, 2007):** the last of the series, known for weekly player-made level sets; a fan site keeps them alive (search snippet). <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ricochet_(disambiguation)>
- **Ricochet (Valve, 2000):** a multiplayer Half-Life mod; players throw discs that bounce off barriers (the description is from a search snippet). <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ricochet_(2000_video_game)>
- **Ricochet Kills (browser series, four games):** a physics puzzle shooter: a few bullets a level, banked off walls to hit every target. Sources are game-portal pages only (search snippet). <https://www.seeles.ai/games/puzzle/ricochet-kills-physics-puzzle-shooting-game>
- **Ricochet Robots (board game, Alex Randolph, 1999):** robots slide in straight lines until they hit a wall or another robot. <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ricochet_Robots>
- **How Rickashay differs (inferred):** all the brick-breaking Ricochets are real-time paddle games; Rickashay has no paddle, is turn-based, and scores the length of one aimed flight.

## What this suggests for Rickashay (all inferred)

- Round the bricks' corners, as Holedown does, if pockets are to be common and the corner is to be something a player steers by rather than a coin toss.
- Triangles earn their place as deflectors that send a ball sideways into a channel; circles make the next bounce hard to read, so keep them rare or out.
- Build levels around a side door and an attic: one narrow way up and a space above the wall. A small door keeps the ball in; a wall with no door is the genre's most hated board.
- A fixed, indestructible piece that never descends (shelf, deflector, rotating bar) gives the ball something to rattle against when the wall is thin. It must not be able to lose the player the game.
- Floor help has two known forms: a pickup that grants one more floor bounce, and blocks laid over part of the floor. The first lasts a round; how long the second lasts is not documented.
- End a dead flight by what it is hitting, not by a clock on the whole flight: a count of wall bounces with no brick hit, then a visible nudge or a pickup dropped in its path, with Skip always there.
- Count the hits on screen and let each brick's worth rise through the flight; players count for themselves when the game does not.
- A guide that shows more bounces is something players pay for and trust less; if it is shown, it has to be exact at corners.
