# Mechanics: what paddle brick breakers put in the field

Read 2026-11-10 from wikis, manuals, reviews, guides, interviews and design
essays; no source code was opened. Everything is paraphrased. "(inferred)"
marks my own reading; "(listing)" marks a fact taken only from a search result
summary whose page I could not open. Tags in square brackets are in Sources.

## 1. Bricks and obstacles we lack

- **Revenge of Doh, sliding bricks and exempt regrowers.** Some bricks move
  left and right while their sides are unblocked, so clearing a neighbour
  sets a row moving. Notched silver bricks regrow after a short delay and
  are not needed to clear the level; ours regrow, the exemption is new.
  [WP Revenge of Doh]
- **DX-Ball, unbreakable means "needs the right tool".** Unbreakables fall to
  a neighbouring explosion, Thru Brick or Fire Ball, pay points, drop items
  and are never required; steel takes 3 hits but 1 from an explosion. One
  capsule detonates every explosive brick at once; another grows each one
  into its four neighbours so later chains run further. [Namu DX-Ball]
- **Descending walls: Super Breakout, Alleyway, DX-Ball.** Progressive mode
  steps the wall down as the ball speeds up. In Alleyway's groups of three
  stages the second scrolls its bricks sideways and the third drops them a
  row in bursts that quicken per paddle bounce. DX-Ball's red Falling Bricks
  capsule drops a row per paddle hit to a stop line, and can make three
  stages unclearable. [WP Super Breakout] [WP Alleyway] [Namu DX-Ball]
- **Shatter, bricks that act when hit.** Rocket bricks fly off one way, gust
  bricks repel the ball, barrier bricks wall themselves in, loose bricks
  drift at the paddle and stun it on contact without costing a life.
  Reviewers praised the variety. [Destructoid Shatter] [Worthplaying Shatter]
- **Bricks that shoot or spawn.** Breakout: Recharged has a brick that fires
  a bullet down when destroyed and one that fires until broken. Woody Pop's
  mystery blocks release toy soldiers that deflect the ball. In Off the
  Wall, blocks parachute in to replace broken ones and guns shrink the
  paddle. [XboxHub Recharged] [WP Woody Pop] [WP Off the Wall]
- **Block Breaker Deluxe 2, swinging bricks and a teleporter.** Bricks swing
  down when hit and chain into what they strike; a teleporter hides in the
  middle of late walls. Pocket Gamer said each level felt different. [PG BBD2]
- **Breakout Beyond, fast-forward blocks.** They skip the scrolling stage
  ahead and leave uncleared bricks in the paddle's way; touching one stuns
  the paddle. [AV Club Beyond]
- **Puchi Carat, hanging groups and colour bombs.** Gems cut off from the top
  fall for a bonus; a flashing gem clears every gem of its colour.
  [WP Puchi Carat]
- **BreakQuest and Ricochet Infinity, the board as a machine.** Physics
  pieces on threads and bumpers; boards that shift as the paddle moves;
  bricks behind metal with hidden triggers. Both are praised, though
  BreakQuest can swamp beginners. [Gamezebo BreakQuest] [JIG Ricochet]
- **Design essay, the rest of the catalogue.** Switches and ball locks that
  open reward rooms, ball traps (Super Breakout's Cavity holds two spare
  balls in the wall), catch-and-eject mechanisms, wedges, channels, speed-up
  regions, pinball bumpers, roaming bombs. [GD Breakdown] [WP Super Breakout]

## 2. Power-ups and how games structure them

- **Arkanoid, scarcity rules.** Seven capsules (S, C, E, D, L, B, P). Only
  one is on screen at a time and none appear while more than one ball is in
  play, so multiball costs the next drop. A caught capsule also scores
  (1,000 in the Spectrum instructions). B opens an exit that skips the
  level. [PA Arkanoid] [PA Arkanoid 2] [Speccy Arkanoid]
- **Revenge of Doh, the later set.** Twin paddles with a gap; a trailing
  ghost paddle; N keeps three balls alive by re-splitting; Mega pierces and
  breaks gold until another capsule replaces it; a flashing capsule gives
  one of five extremes (40 balls, constant lasers). Catch fires by itself
  if held too long. Reduce, the bad one, shrinks the paddle but pays bonus
  points while it is small, until another capsule restores it.
  [PA Arkanoid 2]
- **DX-Ball, colour-coded and mostly stacking.** 18 items: 10 blue (good), 5
  red (bad), 3 grey (expand, shrink, split), one on screen at a time,
  drifting at ball speed and bouncing off walls. Effects stack unless
  opposed; the paddle has 5 sizes. Extra Life cancels Thru Brick, Shooting
  and Grab; Kill Paddle is instant death yet still pays 100. [Namu DX-Ball]
- **BreakQuest, 50+ capsules and reserve balls.** Capsules also arrive on a
  timer or sit in the level at the start. Star capsules add up to reserve
  balls the player may launch by hand. Gamezebo: good and bad are hard to
  tell apart in play. [WP BreakQuest] [Gamezebo BreakQuest]
- **Ricochet Infinity, 30 power-ups in three groups.** 16 ball, 11 ship, 3
  other. Freeze locks the paddle for a few seconds and pays 1,000 for
  surviving. Unlockable ship specials bundle effects with a cost: fireball
  and wide paddle, but a faster, smaller ball. [RU wiki] [JIG Ricochet]
- **Shatter, no bad pickups and a meter instead of a laser.** Pickups are
  few and all good (double shards, a stronger or more steerable ball, a
  life). The laser became Shard Storm, charged by collected shards, which
  the shield also drains. PCWorld called this player-positive. [WP Shatter]
  [PCWorld Shatter] [Electron Dance Shatter]
- **Wizorb, spells and a shop.** Fireball, a wind gust that shifts the ball
  and placing the ball by hand run on a small mana pool refilled by
  potions. Shops inside levels sell temporary upgrades (long paddle, three
  balls) for coins. Players report mana running short (listing).
  [Destructoid Wizorb] [Wizorb listings]
- **Drawkanoid, loadout instead of drops.** Up to three active abilities
  charge over time and are fired by hand, plus passives, all bought with
  gold between runs. [Entertainium Drawkanoid]
- **Breakout: Recharged, paddle guns.** Homing missiles, a straight shot or
  a three-way spread, plus a trajectory display; its reviewer found some
  over-powered. [XboxHub Recharged]
- **AlphaBounce, 26 items and permanent gear.** Items include column clears
  and a "drunk" ball; some levels drop 20 at once, and critics found them
  hard to tell apart. Paddle and ball upgrades found on the map are kept.
  [NL AlphaBounce] [WP AlphaBounce]
- **Other structures.** Ghox: five orbs of one colour buy a power-up that
  survives a death. Beyond: cleared stages unlock power-ups to add to later
  ones. Arkanoid DS versus: power-downs you catch go to your opponent
  (listing). Hyperballoid 2 sorts items into Ball, Bat, Weapon and Misc
  (listing). [WP Ghox] [Seafoam Beyond] [Arkanoid DS listings]
  [SW Hyperballoid]
- **Are bad capsules liked? (inferred)** Complaints are about telling them
  apart at speed, not about their existing. The accepted ones are
  colour-coded, pay something, or go to a rival. Electron Dance argues
  Arkanoid's capsules mostly lure players to death. [Electron Dance Shatter]

## 3. Enemies and bosses

- **Arkanoid, drifting enemies.** They descend at random and die on contact
  with ball or paddle for 100 points; their job is to distract and deflect
  the ball. [Speccy Arkanoid] [C64-Wiki Arkanoid]
- **Arkanoid, DOH.** The last stage has no capsules, so banked lives decide
  it. About 16 hits in the arcade (listing), about 25 on the C64; he spits
  shots that kill the paddle (listing); no continue. [C64-Wiki Arkanoid]
  [WP Arkanoid] [LIT Arkanoid]
- **Revenge of Doh and Doh It Again.** A brain mini-boss at round 17, then
  DOH in two forms, the second with tentacles that break off and regrow.
  Doh It Again has a boss every 11 of 99 rounds; EGM disliked the reuse.
  [WP Revenge of Doh] [WP Doh It Again]
- **Shatter, a boss per world.** Each has a weak point, some guarded; one
  must be blown over to show its weak side, and serving extra balls helps.
  Reviewers liked them; Destructoid found one, the Bat, irritating.
  [Destructoid Shatter] [Worthplaying Shatter]
- **Wizorb.** Each of five worlds of 12 levels ends in a boss with a health
  bar whose shots the paddle must dodge; some blow the orb away with wind
  (listing). Roaming enemies must also be cleared. [Destructoid Wizorb]
- **Strikey Sisters, enemies as the clock.** Enemies walk at the player,
  drop an item each, and respawn until the last brick breaks; the stage
  ends when the last one falls, which the reviewer admired. Ball-sized
  enemy shots hide the real ball, and enemies that bat the ball back make
  hits a matter of luck. [IGC Strikey]
- **Krypton Egg, set-piece stages.** Monsters block the ball; one kills an
  unshielded paddle. Each set of ten has a horde stage after the fifth and
  a duel with energy bars after the tenth. [WP Krypton Egg]
- **Enemy fire: Arkanoid vs Space Invaders, AlphaBounce, Jardinains.** The
  paddle bounces invaders' shots back. AlphaBounce's lasers damage the
  paddle and critics called the ship fragile. Jardinains' gnomes throw
  pots, and once knocked off can be bounced on the paddle for points
  (listing). [WP Arkanoid vs SI] [WP AlphaBounce] [Moby Jardinains]
- **Caromble.** Break a boss's shield several times, then land a final hit;
  the shield regrows if the player is slow or drops the ball, which the
  reviewer found tedious. [LadiesGamers Caromble]
- **Recent designs.** Eternal Battle's last four players fight a boss that
  shrinks or freezes paddles. Roguelike Bricks' bosses seal routes and
  shrink the field but always show their weak point. Bricks Survivors
  makes a boss of a wall to clear inside a minute. [GameSpew Eternal]
  [Steam Roguelike Bricks] [Steam Bricks Survivors]
- **Nervous Brickdown, a mercy ladder.** Every theme ends in a boss.
  Repeated failure unlocks an extra life, then a way to blow the ball
  upward, then skipping the boss. [Worthplaying Brickdown]
- **What reads as fair (inferred).** Hazards that stun or cost time, weak
  points always visible, no reliance on a capsule lottery. Disliked: bosses
  that return the ball unpredictably and shields that regrow.

## 4. Paddle and ball rules that add skill

- **Breakout, the original schedule.** The ball speeds up after 4 hits,
  after 12 hits, and on first touching the orange and red rows. The paddle
  halves once the ball reaches the top wall. [WP Breakout]
- **Alleyway, paddle motion matters.** The ball travels only at 15, 30 or 45
  degrees. Reversing the paddle at contact sends it up faster; moving fast
  against the ball returns it at 15 degrees the paddle's way. A held
  button changes paddle speed; the ceiling halves the paddle. [WP Alleyway]
- **Off the Wall, spin.** Hitting the ball with a fast-moving paddle makes
  it curve for a while. [WP Off the Wall]
- **Design essay, bump and friction.** Three paddle models: pure reflection,
  friction (paddle motion changes the angle), friction plus spin. A paddle
  "bump" rises to meet the ball for wider angles and power. Speed the
  player builds beats speed from capsules, since weaker players slow down
  by themselves. [GD Breakdown]
- **Timed and charged hits.** Kirby's Block Ball: a well-timed hit breaks
  harder blocks. Caromble: holding a button charges a shot but pins the
  paddle; the shot pierces until it meets something solid (listing).
  Strikey Sisters: a charged swing returns enemy shots. [WP Block Ball]
  [LadiesGamers Caromble] [Caromble listings] [IGC Strikey]
- **Shatter, steering with a cost.** Suck and blow bend the ball at any
  time and a mark shows where it will land. The same input gathers shards
  and drags loose bricks at the paddle. Players may also serve extra balls
  out of their lives. [Destructoid Shatter] [PCWorld Shatter]
- **BreakQuest and Ricochet, one-button rescues.** The gravitor adds
  downward pull on demand, useless on a ball moving straight up and down;
  Gamezebo called it the standout feature. Ricochet's recall returns the
  ball to the paddle. [WP BreakQuest] [Gamezebo BreakQuest] [JIG Ricochet]
- **Slow motion and speed.** Breakout Beyond's focus slows all but the
  stage clock, at a cost in score (listing). Drawkanoid slows time when
  the ball comes low, while the player draws a paddle at any angle.
  Breakorage has a held overdrive and a self-destruct that re-serves the
  ball. [AV Club Beyond] [PS Store Beyond] [Entertainium Drawkanoid]
  [Steam Breakorage]
- **More paddles, and walls that hurt.** Super Breakout Double, Revenge of
  Doh's Twin, Devilish (the upper paddle attacks), Kirby (screen edges). In
  Buster Breaks Out each outer-wall hit drains the ball, so the skill is
  keeping it inside the bricks. [WP Devilish] [Steam Buster]

## 5. The last brick problem and stalls

- **Sidhe named it.** Shatter's team listed the last brick, with limited
  interaction and static fields, as the problems to prototype against.
  [Sixth Axis Sidhe]
- **Steer the ball.** Reviewers credit Shatter's push and pull and
  BreakQuest's gravitor with fixing it. Ricochet's recall, Wizorb's spells
  (listing) and Anodia 2's tap-to-converge (listing) do the same job.
  [Worthplaying Shatter] [Gamezebo BreakQuest] [TA Anodia 2]
- **DX-Ball, lightning.** With one brick left and the ball bouncing too
  long, a charging sound plays and lightning destroys the brick. If the
  ball repeats a path among unbreakables, Zap Bricks fires by itself and
  turns them into normal bricks. [Namu DX-Ball]
- **Let the player leave.** Arkanoid's Break capsule and DX-Ball's Level
  Warp end the level (no points for skipped bricks). Hyperballoid 2's warp
  item appears when few bricks remain (listing). LBreakout2 offers a warp
  once a set share is cleared, per a reader comment. AlphaBounce arms a
  weapon when few blocks remain. [PA Arkanoid] [Namu DX-Ball]
  [SW Hyperballoid] [Electron Dance Shatter] [WP AlphaBounce]
- **Alleyway, loop breaking.** After the second cycle of a loop between
  ceiling, unbreakables and paddle, the ball's velocity changes at a random
  point. [WP Alleyway]
- **Change the goal or add a clock.** Breakout Beyond asks for a ball across
  a line behind the wall, Buster Breaks Out for enough blocks. Breakorage
  turns unbroken bricks into monsters after 60 seconds. [AV Club Beyond]
  [Steam Buster] [Steam Breakorage]
- **Keep something else moving.** Strikey Sisters' enemies keep a thin
  board busy, as Arkanoid's drifters do (inferred). Recharged refills the
  wall when nearly clear, yet its reviewer still noted lulls with one or
  two stragglers. [IGC Strikey] [XboxHub Recharged]
- **Essay tests.** Release a second ball if the first is stranded; hand out
  power-ups or auto-targeting on a timer; ask whether the level would stay
  interesting if the ball vanished for 10 seconds. [GD Breakdown]

## 6. Scoring and rewards

- **Row and colour values.** Breakout pays 1, 3, 5 and 7 by row colour (896
  maximum). Arkanoid pays 50 to 120 by colour and silver pays 50 times the
  round number (listing). [WP Breakout] [Speccy Arkanoid] [LIT Arkanoid]
- **Risk as stake.** DX-Ball's Slow Ball lowers points per brick and
  Roguebreaker speeds the ball as score rises. Reduce pays while the paddle
  is small; Freeze pays 1,000 for surviving. [Namu DX-Ball]
  [Steam Roguebreaker] [PA Arkanoid 2] [JIG Ricochet]
- **Shatter, a multiplier fed by pickups.** Collected shards raise it and a
  dropped ball saps it; in Endless it decays fast (listing). Electron Dance
  reads the game's difficulty as opt-in through score. [SuperJump Shatter]
  [PSN Shatter] [Electron Dance Shatter]
- **Breakout 71, coins and a strict combo.** Combo is the number of coins
  each brick drops, and coins must be caught. It resets when the ball
  returns without touching a brick; some upgrades add resets (side or top
  wall hits) in exchange for more coins. [itch Breakout 71]
- **Between-paddle chains.** Drawkanoid scores chains of breaks before the
  ball returns; Beyond's combo grows per paddle bounce, resets on a lost
  ball and drives the effects. [Entertainium Drawkanoid] [AV Club Beyond]
- **Optional collectibles.** Ricochet has five rings a level: all of them
  earn a trophy and gold rings unlock ships. BreakQuest's stars become
  reserve balls. [WP Lost Worlds] [JIG Ricochet] [WP BreakQuest]
- **Medals and ranks.** Caromble: three medals a level (finish, score,
  time) that unlock skill levels; its Mercy lives forfeit them (listing).
  Twin Breaker: a D rank passes, an A unlocks lore (listing).
  [Caromble listings] [Twin Breaker listings]
- **Bonus stages and penalties.** Alleyway's timed bonus stage (95 on the
  clock) lets the ball pass through bricks, costs no life and pays 500 to
  1,500. Eternal Battle pays for chains and docks points for a dropped
  ball. [WP Alleyway] [GameSpew Eternal]

## 7. Modes and progression

- **Branching campaigns.** Revenge of Doh: a left or right gate picks the
  next round (34 played of 64). Woody Pop: up to three next rooms.
  Arkanoid DS: 28 worlds of five levels in a pyramid. Beyond: finishing a
  stage opens two or three more. [WP Revenge of Doh] [PA Arkanoid 2]
  [WP Woody Pop] [WP Arkanoid DS] [AV Club Beyond]
- **Objectives over old levels.** Arkanoid DS Quest replays its levels with
  a time limit or a ball limit; Recharged has challenge missions with a
  target score or a set power-up. [WP Arkanoid DS] [XboxHub Recharged]
- **Mode menus.** Shatter: Story, Endless, Boss Rush, Time Attack, co-op,
  and bonus rounds juggling three balls. Twin Breaker: Marathon, Boss Rush,
  New Game Plus with more paddles (listing). Drawkanoid: main, timed, zen.
  [SuperJump Shatter] [Twin Breaker listings] [Entertainium Drawkanoid]
- **Co-op and versus.** Off the Wall: three players on different edges and
  a Pong round for a life. Recharged: split field, shared score. Arkanoid
  DS: race to clear a level or a colour. Puchi Carat: misses add rows to
  your side, dropped gems go to the rival. Eternal Battle: 25 players,
  lowest scores cut (listing). [WP Off the Wall] [XboxHub Recharged]
  [WP Arkanoid DS] [WP Puchi Carat] [GameSpew Eternal]
- **Level editors.** Home Revenge of Doh, Doh It Again (levels and
  power-ups), Ricochet's weekly player sets, Roguebreaker's custom maps.
  [WP Revenge of Doh] [WP Doh It Again] [JIG Ricochet] [Steam Roguebreaker]
- **Roguelite, Breakout 71 (2025).** Seven levels; after each, pick 1 of 3
  upgrades, with extra choices for clearing fast, never missing and
  catching every coin. Score unlocks more of its 50+ upgrades and 55
  levels; there is a reroll. [itch Breakout 71]
- **Roguelite, Buster Breaks Out (2026).** 25 floors and 5 wardens; cash
  buys from 40+ upgrades of several rarities in a shop; a vending machine
  adds special blocks to the pool; a weekly run board. Roguebreaker (2018)
  offers 1 of 2 upgrades per level, such as pulling the ball back or
  tilting the board. [Steam Buster] [Steam Roguebreaker]
- **Roguelite, survivors hybrids.** Breaking Survivors: catch XP, pick an
  upgrade per level-up, one of each ball type, falling rectangles hurt at
  a line. Bricks Survivors (2025): five bosses a run. Breakorage (not yet
  out): 60-second stages, refundable permanent upgrades.
  [Rogueliker Breaking] [Steam Bricks Survivors] [Steam Breakorage]
- **Roguelite, others (2026).** Brick By Brick: 20 stages, 11 heroes with an
  active and a passive, elemental synergies. Roguelike Bricks: pick one of
  three, elemental balls, daily and weekly boards. Ball x Pit (2025), the
  nearest hit, describes no paddle. [Steam Brick By Brick]
  [Steam Roguelike Bricks] [WP Ball x Pit]

## What stands out for our game

- **1. A hold-to-pull steer on a meter** (Shatter, BreakQuest). It answers
  the last brick and adds a skill our position-only paddle lacks. Risk: it
  can make aiming trivial, so it needs a cost as Shatter's has.
- **2. A guaranteed finisher** (DX-Ball's lightning). Our lull nudge and
  glow warn but do not end a stall. Risk: zapped bricks must pay nothing or
  players will wait for it.
- **3. Three medals per level** (Caromble: finish, score, time). Thirty
  levels gain replay without new boards. Risk: targets need tuning.
- **4. Collectibles that feed the multiplier** (Breakout 71's coins,
  Shatter's shards). A catch-or-chase choice on every break. Risk: clutter
  beside capsules on a 13-wide field.
- **5. A stricter streak** (Breakout 71: a return that touched no brick
  resets it). It rewards aim over rally length. Risk: harsh on new players.
- **6. Colour-coded capsules in slots** (DX-Ball's colours, Hyperballoid's
  classes). Good and bad read at a glance, one ball effect and one paddle
  effect at a time. Risk: slots cut the wild stacking some players enjoy.
- **7. Bad capsules that pay** (Reduce, Freeze). Our narrow paddle and fast
  ball become choices. Risk: the deal must be obvious as the capsule falls.
- **8. Bricks that act**: shooters, rockets, sliders (Recharged, Shatter,
  Revenge of Doh). They suit hand-made levels. Risk: shots must stun, not
  kill, or they read as unfair.
- **9. A descending-wall level type** with a stop line (Alleyway, DX-Ball).
  Pressure without enemies. Risk: DX-Ball's made stages unwinnable.
- **10. Drifting enemies that stop when the last brick breaks** (Arkanoid,
  Strikey Sisters). They keep thin boards lively. Risk: random deflections
  were Strikey's main complaint.
- **11. One boss per set** with a visible weak point and no capsule lottery
  (Shatter, Roguelike Bricks). Risk: content cost; avoid regrowing shields.
- **12. A timed or charged paddle hit** (Kirby, Caromble). A second verb for
  the paddle. Risk: mouse, keys and touch must all be able to do it.
- **13. Exempt bricks and tool-only bricks** (Revenge of Doh, DX-Ball):
  steel that blast or fire can break, regrowers that need not be cleared.
  Risk: the clear rule must be visible on the board.
- **14. Objectives over existing levels** (Arkanoid DS Quest) and a weekly
  run (Buster). Cheap modes. Risk: thin if the objectives are too easy.
- **15. A seven-level draft run** (Breakout 71: pick 1 of 3, more for clean
  play). It reuses our levels and capsule effects. Risk: balance work.
