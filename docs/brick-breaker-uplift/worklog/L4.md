# Stream L's log: L4, the places

Package L4 of stream L, the look, run in a worktree of its own
(`bbu/L4`) beside L3, which runs in stream L's own; so its log is a file of
its own, kept as `L.md` is (`../implementation-plan.md` §7.6): each commit,
what each review found and how it was answered, the choices made where the
plan was silent, the figures measured and what was left. Only L4 writes it.

## L4. Places

### 2026-11-14: `svgOf()` into `@fz/phaser/shapes` (lift 4)

`feat(phaser): write a drawing's commands as an svg document with svgOf()`
(commit 1 of L4).

- `src/shapes/svg.ts`: `svgOf()` writes a `Drawing` (an `ArtFrame`'s
  width, height and commands) as an SVG document of its size, its view box
  its own, each command as an element in turn, so each lies over those
  before it, as `atlasFrameOf()` plays them into a canvas: a rectangle and
  a rounded one as `<rect>`, a circle and a ring as `<circle>`, a line as
  `<line>`, a polyline and a polygon as theirs. Colors are written with
  `color`'s `cssHex()`, so `shapes` now imports `color`, as the pure
  entries may import each other.
- Its spec reads the document back through the browser's parser (jsdom's
  `DOMParser`): a square drawing and one twice as wide as tall, every kind
  of command with its attributes, the order, and the numbers' rounding.
- The library's `AGENTS.md`, the `phaser-core` skill and the `fuzzley-ui`
  skill's table name it.

Choices:

- A polyline is written with `stroke-miterlimit="10"`, a canvas's own,
  since SVG's default of 4 would cut a sharp corner shorter than the scene
  draws it. Line caps and joins are otherwise both's defaults, butt and
  miter, which Phaser's canvas `Graphics` never changes.
- Numbers are written to a thousandth of a pixel, never as `-0`, so a
  document stays short and reads the same on every engine.
- It writes only the document; a page wraps it in a `data:` URL itself, as
  the one user, the board's backdrop, does.

The review, and the answers:

1. `num()`'s `|| 0` did nothing for -0, which `String()` writes as `0`,
   and turned a NaN into 0, which a canvas would not draw. Fixed: removed.
2. The doc didn't say the size is in the commands' own pixels, which a
   page reads as CSS pixels, nor that the `#` of its colors needs
   `encodeURIComponent()` in a `data:` URL. Fixed: `svgOf()`'s doc says
   both, and `Drawing`'s the first.
3. `strokeOf()`'s parameter spelled out `LineStyle`. Fixed: it takes one.
4. The spec named `Drawing["commands"][number]` for `DrawCommand`, and a
   `document` that shadowed jsdom's. Fixed: `DrawCommand`, and `xml`.

Checks: the library's lint, typecheck and tests (59 files, 909 tests);
its coverage (`src/shapes/` whole; all files 99.34% statements, 96.51%
branches); the root's lint, typecheck and tests under the lock.

### 2026-11-14: the places' art

`feat(brick-breaker): draw each set's place as a tile of its motifs`
(commit 2 of L4).

- New `src/view/places/`. `places.ts`: `PlaceArt` and `PLACE_ART`, a
  record keyed by R1's `Place`, so a new place fails to compile without its
  art: each place's motif, whether a drawing board's grid lies under it (the
  workshop's), and how much of the text it takes over the field (0.045) and
  over the page (0.055), the sheet's washes. `placeTile()` lays a place's
  tile out at a scale in its inks (`PlaceInks`) as a `Drawing`, which the
  scene will bake and `svgOf()` write; `tileSide()`, `placeInks()` and
  `fieldInks()` beside it. `motifs.ts`: the five places' motifs, from the
  sheet's `motif()` in `art/scene.mjs`.
- `src/view/colors/palette.ts` (L's) gains `text`, which a place is washed
  from over the field, with its spec.
- Specs: `places.spec.ts` holds every set of `LEVEL_SETS` to a place it
  draws, every command of every place's tile inside the tile at each of
  `FIELD_SCALES`, the workshop's grid under its motifs, in the faint ink,
  across the tile's whole side and on whole pixels clear of the seams, the
  motifs over every row and column, and every place, in each of the four
  palettes, to 1.05:1 to 1.2:1 against the field and the page, the
  workshop's grid fainter than its motifs. `motifs.spec.ts` holds each
  motif to its ink, its reach and the shapes each pick chooses. The arcade
  has no levels yet, so its art is held by these specs alone.
- The workspace's `AGENTS.md` (`src/view/places/`, the palette's text),
  `brick-breaker-content` ("A Set" names its place's art, a new "A Place",
  and the places spec in the table of checks) and `brick-breaker-testing`
  (the places spec).

Choices where the plan was silent:

- **A tile, not a scatter.** The sheet scatters its motifs over any
  rectangle, clipped at its edges; a scene tiles one texture. So a tile
  holds 6 × 6 motifs (`TILE_MOTIFS`) at the sheet's step over a field, 4.2
  units (`MOTIF_STEP`), 25.2 units a side, about the field's width, so a
  repeat shows at most once across. Rows stand a quarter step in, every
  other one half a step more, so the stagger carries on across the seam;
  each motif is the sheet's size (0.34 to 0.54 of a step) and as far off
  its place (±0.15 of a step), then moved a device pixel in from any edge
  it would come near, so a seam never cuts it or its soft edge.
- **Drawn from a seed.** The sheet's motifs come from a hash of `Math.sin`;
  here from `@fz/core/random`'s `createRandom()` with the tile's own seed,
  as the view draws numbers only from a seed. Every place shares the
  layout, as the sheet's do, and a pick both chooses a motif and turns it,
  as the sheet's does.
- **Strokes round at ends and corners,** as the sheet's, through `shapes`'
  `stroke()` (lines and dots) rather than polylines, so a canvas and the
  page draw them alike and `extentOf()` measures them exactly; all but a
  cog's outline, which is one polyline, mitred (below).
- **The grid** is a line every half step at quarter steps, on whole device
  pixels, in the art's own line (`lineWidth()`), in an ink 0.55 as strong
  as the motifs', as the sheet washes it.
- **The washes are each place's own** in the record, though all take the
  sheet's today, so one can be tuned alone. Measured in the four palettes,
  the motifs keep 1.087:1 to 1.126:1 against the field and 1.104:1 to
  1.144:1 against the page; the spec's bounds, 1.05:1 and 1.2:1, leave room
  for a tuned place and still sit far under the 3:1 everything played keeps.
- **`BricksPalette.text`** is new rather than reading `glow`, which is the
  text today but means the glow.
- The plan's sixth place, the attic, is not in R1's `PLACES`, so it has no
  art yet; it comes with the Attic.

The review, and the answers:

1. The block's turn spec picked exact twelfths (1/2, 5/12), which a smooth
   turn would draw alike. Fixed: picks inside their twelfths, 0.52 and
   0.45, which a smooth turn would reach 51 and 57 pixels with, not 47
   and 61.
2. Specs that restated the code: `placeInks()` at 0.5 couldn't tell the
   text from what lies behind, `tileSide()` and `fieldInks()` recomputed
   their formulas, and the scatter asked for 30 of 36 cells. Fixed: inks
   at 0.25 pinned to `0x404040` and `0x232323`; sides pinned to 1008 and
   605 pixels; `fieldInks()` pinned to `0xf4f4f4` and `0xf9f9f9` over a
   white field with black text; the commands' middles held to every row
   and every column.
3. The motifs spec typed its table of motifs by hand. Fixed: built from
   `PLACES`, and the content skill's "A Place" says the motifs spec holds
   a new place's motif.
4. Before the canvas is laid out (a unit of 0), the 1-pixel tile couldn't
   hold a motif a pixel clear of its edges, and the grid inked it whole.
   Fixed: under a unit of a device pixel, `placeTile()` is empty; its doc
   and a spec say so.
5. The workshop's tile would weigh the page's mask down: a cog was 66
   commands. Fixed: a cog's outline is one polyline, from the middle of a
   tooth's top round to it again, mitred where the sheet rounds it, which
   at 5% no one sees; its corners are no sharper than 60° (`SHARPEST`,
   which the motifs spec holds every motif's polylines to), so its mitres
   reach no farther than half its width past its extent, which
   `coverOf()` adds and `keptInside()` and the specs measure by. As the
   page's mask, written by `svgOf()` and `encodeURIComponent()`, the
   workshop's tile went from 212 KB to 34 KB; the garden's, 70 KB, its
   leaves' 40-point ellipses, is now the heaviest, the others 15 to 30 KB.
   Kept a `data:` URL rather than a Blob's: the mask's inks are white in
   every palette, so it is written again only as the place or the field's
   size changes, and a Blob URL would need revoking each time, which jsdom
   can't. `outlined()` drew its first corner's dot twice; it draws each
   once.
6. `AGENTS.md` and the content skill described the pattern as drawn, and
   nothing draws it until the next commit. Fixed: `AGENTS.md` says it is
   drawn to lie there; the skill's browser check moves to the next commit.
7. `turnedAbout()` was used once. Fixed: the leaf is placed from turned
   points with `placed()`.

Checks: the workspace's lint, typecheck and tests (`pure` and `dom`, 67
files, 2705 tests); the root's lint, typecheck and tests under the lock,
with the next commit's work set aside.
