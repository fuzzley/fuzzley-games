How to play

Steer the snake to the apple. Each apple it eats scores a point and makes it a square longer and a little faster. The run ends when the snake runs into a wall or into itself; fill the whole board and you win.

Starting. The snake waits for your first direction. You can press two turns in quick succession, such as up and then left, and it takes both, one square apart.

Speeds. Slow, Normal and Fast each keep their own best score. Change the speed, and whether the snake glides or steps, in the settings.

Pausing. The game pauses itself when you switch away, or open a menu or a dialog, and goes on only when you say: with a direction, Space or P, or Resume.
