Eat apples to grow. Each apple is 1 point, and the snake gets a little faster every time. If you hit a wall or your own tail, the game is over. Fill the whole board to win.

Touch or mouse Keyboard
Start or turn Swipe, or the on-screen arrows Arrow keys or W A S D
Pause or resume Pause, then Resume Space or P
Pause Pause Esc
New game New game Enter or Space

Quick turns. If you turn twice quickly, such as up and then left, the snake makes both turns, 1 square apart.

Speeds. Each speed has its own best score. If you change the speed, a new game starts.

Pausing. The game pauses when you switch tabs or open a menu. Press Space or P to resume.
