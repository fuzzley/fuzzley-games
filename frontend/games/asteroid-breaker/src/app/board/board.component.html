The left and right arrow keys, or A and D, turn the ship, and the mouse turns it toward the pointer. The up arrow or W thrusts, and so does the right mouse button. Space or the left mouse button fires, and Shift, the down arrow or S dashes. Any of those keys starts a wave, goes on, and goes to the next wave. Escape pauses, P pauses and goes on, and Enter goes on, or to the next wave. Once an ended run's result is put away, any flight key, Enter or P starts another. On a card's button, the keys are the button's: Space and Enter press it.
