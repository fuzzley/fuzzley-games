Aim the fuzzball and charge it up. Let go to shoot. It bounces around breaking bricks until it lands on the floor. After each shot, the wall moves down 1 row. Break every brick that can be broken to clear the level. If one of them reaches the floor, you lose the level. The game ends when you lose a level or clear them all. You can try a lost level again as often as you like.

The jump. When there's nothing left to break below the dashed line, the wall jumps. It moves down a third of the screen instead of 1 row. A jump never loses a level, and it gives your next shot a boon.

Boons. {{ boons }}

Scoring. A brick scores when it breaks, and tougher bricks score more. The more bricks one shot breaks, the more the later ones are worth.

Stars. {{ stars }}

No hurry. Nothing moves until you shoot. Fast-forward speeds up shots and stays on until you turn it off. Drop ends a shot early and keeps what it has broken. A fuzzball that goes too long without hitting a brick gets bored and drops.

Pull back. If you'd rather aim like a slingshot, choose Pull back under Aiming in Settings.

Playing by ear. With a screen reader, you hear the aim and the charge when you change them with the keys. Press R to hear the {{ rowsRead }} lowest rows that have bricks in them.
