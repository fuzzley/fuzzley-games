Aim the fuzzball, charge it and let it fly. It turns off the sides, the ceiling and every brick it doesn't punch through, until it touches the floor, where it waits for the next shot. Then the wall comes down a row. Break every brick a level needs to clear it, those still waiting over the ceiling among them; the level is lost once one it needs stands in the floor's row.

The jump. Once no brick the level needs is left below the dashed line, the wall jumps a third of the field at once rather than a row, and your next shot carries the level's boon. A jump never loses a level.

Scoring. Each brick scores its points for every hit it had, as it breaks. {{ scoring.streak }} {{ scoring.bonuses }} {{ scoring.stars }}

No hurry. Nothing moves until you shoot. Fast-forward and Skip hurry a flight along, and the game pauses itself when you switch away, turn the screen or open a dialog. A run goes on from level to level until one is lost, and a level can be tried again as often as you like.

Keys by place. The letter keys are read by where they sit, whatever they print, so they fall under the same fingers on every keyboard. The left hand's home keys under its little, ring and middle fingers do as ←, ↓ and → do, and the key above the ring finger's as ↑ does.

Ways of aiming. In the settings, Aiming can have the mouse and fingers pull back instead: press anywhere on the field and drag away from where to aim, the longer the pull the more charge, and slide back to where you pressed to call the shot off.

By ear. The aim and the charge are said as the keys change them, and the key above the left index finger's home key reads the wall's three lowest rows, from the floor up.
