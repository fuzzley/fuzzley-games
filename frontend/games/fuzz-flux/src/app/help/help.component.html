Clear each level by meeting its goals before your moves run out. Swap 2 fuzzballs side by side or one above the other. When 3 or more of a kind line up, they make a bunch and hop off the board. The fuzzballs above fall into their place, and new ones come in at the top. A swap that makes no bunch goes back and costs no move. If your moves run out first, you lose the level.

Goals. Each goal shows a picture and how many are left: fuzzballs of a kind to gather, or something on the board to clear. A goal met shows a check mark. You clear the level when a move ends with every goal met.

Moves to spare. When you clear a level, you earn 1 star, and up to {{ mostStars }} if you have enough moves to spare. Each move you have left then turns a fuzzball into a special and sets it off, up to {{ mostEncoreMoves }}. Tap, click or press a key to speed it up.

Deals. Each level has {{ deals }} deals. A deal is the starting board and every fuzzball that comes in after it. You can clear every deal with the moves you have. Restart plays the same deal again. Try again, Play again and coming back to the level play the next deal.
