Aim the fuzzball and let it go. It bounces off the walls and the bricks until it lands on the floor, and each brick it hits takes a hit. After each shot, the wall moves down 1 row. Break every brick that can be broken to clear the level. If one of them reaches the floor, you lose the level. The game ends when you lose a level or clear them all.

Every hit bounces. The fuzzball never breaks through. It bounces off every brick it hits, even one that breaks. Each time it changes direction counts as a turn.

Cut corners. Every brick has its corners cut. Off a side, the fuzzball bounces back like light from a mirror. Off a corner, a steep shot leaves shallow, and a shallow one leaves steep. So a shot only ever flies in 8 directions, all from your aim.

Banking. To reach a gap you can't hit straight, bounce off a side wall. Picture the gap mirrored on the far side of the wall, and aim at that, the way pool players do.

The jump. When there's nothing left to break below the dashed line, the wall jumps. It moves down a third of the screen instead of 1 row. A jump never loses a level, and it makes your next shot a hot shot.

Stars. You get a star for clearing a level, one for clearing it in par or fewer shots, and one for a shot whose rally reaches the level's rally mark. Par and the rally mark depend on how many rows the screen shows when the level starts.

No hurry. Nothing moves until you shoot. Fast-forward speeds up shots and stays on until you turn it off. Skip ends a shot at once, with everything it would have done. A fuzzball that goes too long without hitting a brick gets bored and drops.

Other ways to aim. If you'd rather aim like a slingshot, choose Pull back under Aiming in Settings. To shoot with the Release button instead of letting go, turn on Release with a button.

Playing by ear. With a screen reader, you hear the aim when you change it with the keys. Press G to hear where the guide goes, and R to hear the {{ rowsRead }} lowest rows that have bricks in them.
