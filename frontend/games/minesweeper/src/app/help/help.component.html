How to play

Open every square that hides no mine. A number says how many of the eight squares around it hide one; flag the squares you know are mines.

Lives. A mine you step on costs a life, not the game, and stays open. You start with {{ lives() }}, and the game ends when you lose the last.

Your first square is never a mine.
