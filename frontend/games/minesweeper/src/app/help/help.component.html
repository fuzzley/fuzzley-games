Open every square that isn't a mine. A number tells you how many mines are around it. If you're sure a square is a mine, flag it. Your first square is never a mine.

Mouse Touch Keyboard
Open a square Click Tap Enter or Space
Flag a square Right-click Long press, or Flag mode F
Open around a number Click it Tap it Enter on it
Move Arrow keys, Home, End
