Fill the grid so that every row, column and 3 × 3 box has each digit from 1 to 9 once. Every puzzle has one solution, and you can find it without guessing. If a digit repeats in a row, column or box, it's marked.

Mouse or touch Keyboard
Select a square Click or tap it Arrow keys, Home, End
Place a digit The digit on the pad 1 to 9
Add or remove a note The digit in Notes mode Shift and the digit
Notes mode Notes N
Erase Erase Backspace, Delete or 0
Undo Undo Ctrl + Z
Hint Hint, then Fill in, Add note or Take out H, then H or Enter

Notes. Turn on Notes to jot down the digits that might go in a square. Fill in notes does this for every blank square without notes. When you place a digit, it comes out of the notes in its row, column and box. You can turn off Auto-clear notes in Settings.

Hints. If there is a mistake on the board, Hint shows it. If not, it shows the next square you can solve and explains why. Press Hint again to fix it or fill it in. A puzzle solved with a hint doesn't count for a best time.

Mistakes. Turn on Mark mistakes in Settings to also mark any digit that doesn't match the solution.
