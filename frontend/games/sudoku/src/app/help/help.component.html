How to play

Fill every square so that each row, each column and each 3 × 3 box holds the digits 1 to 9 once. Every puzzle has one solution, which logic alone can find.

Notes. Jot down the digits a square might hold, in Notes mode or with Shift. A digit you place comes out of the notes of the squares it sees, unless you turn that off in the settings.

Hints. A hint points out a digit that doesn't belong, or shows the next square logic can fill and why; press Hint again to fill it in. A puzzle solved with a hint sets no best time.

Mistakes. A digit that repeats in a row, column or box is always marked. Turn on "Mark mistakes" in the settings to mark a digit the solution doesn't hold as well.
