The left and right arrow keys, or A and D, move the paddle, and so does the mouse or a finger. Space, the up arrow or W launches the ball, fires the laser, goes on, and goes to the next level. Escape pauses, P pauses and goes on, and Enter goes on, or to the next level. Once an ended run's result is put away, Space, Enter or P starts another. On a card's button, the keys are the button's: Space and Enter press it.
