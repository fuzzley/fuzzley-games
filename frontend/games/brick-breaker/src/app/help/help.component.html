Bounce the ball off your paddle to break the bricks. Break every brick that can be broken to clear the level. If the ball gets past your paddle, you lose a life. The game ends when you run out of lives or clear all {{ levelCount }} levels.

Scoring. Each hit on a brick scores points. The bricks you break before a ball comes back to your paddle make a streak, and a long streak multiplies your points.

Stars. Each level has 3 stars: 1 for clearing it, 1 for not losing a life, and 1 for reaching the streak shown before the level starts. Stars don't unlock anything. You can win them at any speed.

Pausing. The game pauses when you switch tabs, turn your phone or open a menu. If you leave the page, your game is saved, and you can resume it when you come back.
