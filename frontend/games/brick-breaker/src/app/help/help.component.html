How to play

Keep the ball in play with the paddle, and break every brick a level needs to clear it. A ball that falls past the paddle costs a life; the run ends when the last is lost, or when the last level is cleared.

Scoring. Each brick scores its points for every hit it takes. {{ scoring.streak }} {{ scoring.clearing }} {{ scoring.lives }}

Pausing. The game pauses itself when you switch away, turn the screen or open a dialog, and goes on only when you say.
